## Repair Observations and Geometry Constraints

**Via enclosure repairs: shrink M3 in the long axis to resolve V2.M3.EN.2 / V2.M3.AUX.2**

The only via-related M3 repair in the measured history targeted cell VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36. The cu_pool engine applied a simultaneous y-axis shrink of the M3 via shape by 40 dbu and of four adjacent M3 routing polygons (p894-p897) each by 64 dbu. Both the via shape and the polygons were reduced on the same axis (y). The operation was accepted and delivered a net reduction of 8 DRC markers (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). V2.M3.EN.2 requires that M3 encloses V2 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides (or 5 & 0 nm); V2.M3.AUX.2 requires V2 to be the same width as M3 in the direction perpendicular to the M3 run. Shrinking the M3 bounding box in the direction that was overshooting the via extent is therefore the confirmed mechanism for clearing both V2 enclosure rules at this via cell type. When co-sizing the via shape and the connected M3 stubs, apply the same axis to all members of the group so that the relative alignment between the via centroid and M3 edges remains consistent and V2.M3.AUX.2 is not re-introduced (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00).

**Width and area floor after any shrink**

After any M3 polygon is shrunk to resolve a via-enclosure or spacing violation, the resulting geometry must still satisfy M3.W.1 (>= 18 nm width) and M3.A.1 (>= 504 nm2 area). No failing M3 width or area markers appear in the history following the 64 dbu shrink of trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, confirming that a 64 dbu reduction on those specific polygons remained above both floors. However, these floors set a hard lower bound: do not apply shrinks larger than what the pre-shrink geometry can absorb while staying at or above 18 nm width and 504 nm2 area, because no tested data exists for polygons near those limits.

**Instance moves: M3 inherits displacement from higher-layer stack moves**

The unit_gate engine moved six instances along y (four by +/-24 dbu, two by +/-72 dbu) and the touched-layer set included M3 alongside M4, M5, V3, and V4 (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07). The move was accepted under the conn_preserved gate, but 3 new in-crop violations were recorded (n_new_in_crop = 3, n_new_out_of_crop = 0). This means that an instance displacement that resolves higher-layer violations can simultaneously introduce new M3-layer violations within the same crop window. When a unit_gate move lists M3 among its touched layers, check for newly introduced M3 spacing violations (M3.S.1 through M3.S.6) and M3 via-enclosure violations (V2.M3.EN.2, V2.M3.AUX.2, V3.M3.EN.1) before committing; the gated_in decision does not guarantee a net-zero M3 impact (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07).

**Tip spacing rules require attention at short M3 edges**

M3.S.2 (tip-to-side, 25 nm), M3.S.3 (wide-tip-to-wide-tip, 27 nm), M3.S.4 (narrow-tip-to-narrow-tip, 31 nm), and M3.S.5 (mixed-tip, 31 nm) all apply specifically to M3 edges shorter than 36 nm; M3.S.4 and M3.S.5 apply to edges shorter than 24 nm and carry the most restrictive 31 nm requirement. No direct tip-spacing repair has been measured yet, but the 40 dbu via-shape shrink and 64 dbu polygon shrinks in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 altered the y-extent of M3 segments and, by reducing their end-edge lengths, may have changed which tip-spacing rule each end edge falls under. When resizing M3 in one axis, track whether the resulting end-edge length crosses the 24 nm or 36 nm thresholds, because crossing those thresholds changes the applicable minimum spacing rule and the required clearance jumps from 18 nm (M3.S.1, side-to-side) up to 31 nm (M3.S.4/M3.S.5, narrow-tip scenarios).

**Corner-to-corner spacing (M3.S.6)**

M3.S.6 (20 nm euclidean corner-to-corner) is distinct from the projection-based spacing rules and fires on shapes that pass the side and tip checks but come close at corners. No M3.S.6-specific repair has been observed in the two available trials, but the y-displacements in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07 (+72, +72, +24, +24, -24, -24 dbu) move M3 geometry relative to neighboring polygons and can change corner proximity. Verify M3.S.6 clearance after any instance move that shifts M3 shapes, particularly when two nearby M3 polygons move in opposite y directions (as i0067/i0070 moved by -24 while i0001/i0002 moved by +24 in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07).

**V3.M3.EN.1 enclosure**

V3.M3.EN.1 requires M3 to enclose V3 by at least 5 nm on at least two opposite sides. M3 was listed as a touched layer in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07 alongside V3 and V4, meaning instance moves that shift V3 relative to M3 directly stress this rule. The 3 new in-crop violations logged in that trial could include V3.M3.EN.1 failures, though the specific rule identities are not recorded in the available history. Treat any instance move that displaces both M3 and V3 as a V3.M3.EN.1 risk requiring explicit post-move enclosure verification (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07).

**Non-orthogonal geometry**

The NONORTHOGONAL rule fires on any M3 edge with an angle not in {0deg, 90deg, 180deg, 270deg}. All operations in the measured history used axis-aligned resizes (axis: "y") and orthogonal instance translations (delta_dbu pairs with one component zero), so no non-orthogonal edges were introduced. Restrict all M3 edits to rectilinear moves and axis-aligned resizes to avoid GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL violations, consistent with both measured trials (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07).
