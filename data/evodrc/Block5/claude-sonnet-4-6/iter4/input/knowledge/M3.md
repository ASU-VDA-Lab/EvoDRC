**cu_pool and unit_gate channel conflict resolution on shared polygons**

When both cu_pool and unit_gate target the same polygon in the same assembly window, cu_pool wins: the unit_gate op for that polygon is dropped via assemble_drops with reason "reserved_by_cu_pool_winner" and the remaining unit_gate ops proceed (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01, where p879 x+32 was dropped while the seven instance moves were retained). The unit_gate trial is still evaluated for gated_in on the surviving op set; the dropped op does not prevent the trial from committing. Do not assume that a unit_gate op list will be applied in full when cu_pool is active on the same crop window; verify the assemble_drops field to determine which M3 polygon edits actually executed.
