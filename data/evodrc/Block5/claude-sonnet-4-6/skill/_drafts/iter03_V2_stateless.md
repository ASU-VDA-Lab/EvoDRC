## Repair Strategy

**Two-phase repair on via cells is effective.** For a single via cell (def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36), a coordinated M3 y-axis shrink followed by a V2 shape x-axis move across two separate trials eliminated 15 violations total (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 reduced by 8; trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 reduced by 7). Both trials preserved connectivity. Do not attempt to clear all violations in a single operation on a via cell target; stage the M3 envelope resize first, then reposition the V2 shape.

**Group M3 polygon resizes with the via shape resize.** In trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 all five operations belonged to group g1 and acted on M3 (one resize_via_shape on shape_index 0, four resize on polygons p894–p897), all on the y axis. The via shape was shrunk by 40 dbu; the surrounding M3 polygons were shrunk by 64 dbu. Apply matched group membership to keep V2 inside the M3 envelope and satisfy V2.AUX.1 and V2.M3.AUX.2 simultaneously. A y-axis shrink of the M3 enclosure without moving the co-located M3 polygons in the same group produced a residual 17 violations in window leaf_0009 (not zero), confirming that the polygon resize alone is insufficient without the accompanying via shape adjustment, and vice versa.

**Follow M3 y-resize with V2 x-repositioning.** After the y-axis M3 shrink (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00), the remaining spacing violations were cleared by a single move_via_shape on V2 layer, shape_index 1, along the x axis by +144 dbu (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01). This lateral displacement of the V2 shape directly addresses V2.S.1 projection-based spacing (18 nm same-track, 27 nm cross-track) and the euclidean corner-to-corner spacing rules V2.S.2–V2.S.4. Never move V2 in x before the M3 envelope has been adjusted in y; attempting x-displacement on an oversized M3 envelope risks introducing V2.M3.AUX.2 or V2.M3.EN.2 violations because the V2 shape width must match M3 width perpendicular to M3 length.

**move_via_shape on V2 cross-splits violation reductions across multiple windows.** The single x-axis move in trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 reduced violations in two distinct windows simultaneously (leaf_0002: −4, leaf_0003: −3), confirming that repositioning a V2 shape can relieve spacing constraints in neighboring layout windows in one step. Target the move magnitude to resolve the tightest spacing constraint; +144 dbu was sufficient here without generating new violations (decision: applied, conn_preserved: true).

## Enclosure and Containment

**V2.M2.EN.1 and V2.AUX.1 constrain the y-axis resize budget.** V2.M2.EN.1 requires M2 to enclose V2 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides; V2.AUX.1 requires V2 to remain inside M2 ∩ M3. The M3 y-axis shrinks in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 touched M2 as well (touched_layers includes M2). When resizing M3 in y, co-adjust M2 bounds to maintain the 5 nm two-sided enclosure; failure to do so will produce V2.M2.EN.1 violations even if the M3 envelope is correct.

**V2.M3.EN.2 requires two-sided 5 nm or 5 & 0 nm enclosure.** The rule checks that for each V2 instance, M3 encloses V2 on two opposite sides by at least 5 nm (or one side 5 nm and the flush side 0 nm via the end-cap distinction). The y-axis resize of M3 in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 maintained this constraint across all applied operations (decision: applied). Do not allow M3 shrinkage in y to reduce the enclosure below 5 nm on either the top or bottom edge of V2; confirm by checking the post-resize enclosure before committing.

**V2.M3.AUX.2 locks V2 width to M3 width perpendicular to M3 length.** Any resize of M3 in the direction perpendicular to M3 length must be accompanied by a matching resize of the V2 via shape. The grouped approach in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 (resize_via_shape −40 dbu + polygon resizes −64 dbu, all y) demonstrates that via shape and M3 geometry must move together; ungrouped independent resizes risk leaving V2 width mismatched.

## Spacing Rules

**V2.S.1 spacing depends on end-cap classification.** The rule partitions V2 instances into those with a fully flush M3 edge (v2_nec, no end-cap) and those without (v2_wec, with 5 nm end-cap). Same-track minimum spacing is 18 nm; parallel non-aligned tracks require 27 nm; parallel aligned tracks require 18 nm. The +144 dbu x-move in trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 was sufficient to clear the projection-based spacing check, indicating the pre-move gap was less than 18 nm on the same-track axis. Use x-axis move_via_shape to increase inter-V2 spacing when S.1 violations are detected after M3 envelope adjustment.

**V2.S.2 (23 nm euclidean, wec–wec) and V2.S.3 (30 nm euclidean, nec–nec) are corner-sensitive.** These rules fire on euclidean corner-to-corner distance, not just projection. The v2_wec_mask is grown by a 5 nm lateral extension on the end-cap side before the 16.4 nm euclidean check (effective physical threshold 23 nm); v2_nec_mask uses a 5 nm extension plus 5 nm body extension before the 16.12 nm check (effective 30 nm). A lateral x-move of V2 increases corner separation for horizontally separated vias. The +144 dbu move in trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 resolved spacing violations spanning two windows, consistent with corner-to-corner relief under S.2 or S.3.

**V2.S.4 (27 nm euclidean, wec–nec mixed) requires cross-type clearance.** When one V2 has an end-cap and the other does not, the minimum euclidean corner-to-corner spacing is 27 nm (raw check 17.11 nm after mask extensions). Mixed-type pairs are common in cell arrays. The x-axis repositioning in trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 clears both same-type and mixed-type spacing violations in a single operation, making it the preferred first action after M3 envelope fix.

## Unit-Gate Channel Operations

**Unit-gate repairs on V2-touching units use move_instance and resize_end on x.** Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00 (gated_in, conn_preserved) applied five operations to unit leaf_0001: move_instance on i0104 (+8 dbu x), resize_end high on p946 (+28 dbu x), move_instance on i0061 (+8 dbu x), resize_end high on p968 (+64 dbu x), and move_instance on i0073 (−32 dbu x). All are x-axis adjustments. Touched layers include V2 (and M2, M3, M4, M5, V4). The unit gate accepted these as gated_in (not rejected), with 5 new violations entering crop but 0 leaving crop.

**Positive and negative x-axis instance moves are both valid within a unit.** In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00, two instances moved in the +x direction (+8 dbu each) and one in the −x direction (−32 dbu). Do not constrain unit-gate moves to a single direction; the solver may require counterbalancing moves to satisfy both spacing and enclosure rules simultaneously while preserving connectivity.

**resize_end high on M3-family polygons in x increases the high-x extent of the wire.** Operations on p946 (+28 dbu) and p968 (+64 dbu) in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00 extend the wire end in the high-x direction. These likely extend M3 to maintain V2.M3.EN.2 enclosure or satisfy V2.M3.AUX.2 width matching after instance shifts. When instance moves displace V2 relative to M3, use resize_end on the adjacent M3 polygon end rather than moving the entire polygon, to preserve enclosure on the opposite end.

Prefer cu_pool cell-level via shape moves when the violation is systematic across multiple instances of the same via cell; use unit_gate instance moves when the violation is local to a specific placement context.
