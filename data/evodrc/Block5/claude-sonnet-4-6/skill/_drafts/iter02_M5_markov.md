## Repair Mechanics Observed on M5

**Multi-instance vertical moves touching M5 can close in one operation.** trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07 resolved a DRC crop on the M5 layer using six simultaneous `move_instance` operations, all in the Y direction (deltas of +72 dbu, +24 dbu, and −24 dbu across instances i0113, i0099, i0001, i0002, i0067, i0070). The operation set was accepted with `decision: gated_in` and `conn_preserved: true`. No net out-of-crop violations were produced (n_new_out_of_crop = 0). This confirms that a mixed-direction vertical displacement spread across multiple instances is a viable repair primitive for M5 when connectivity is maintained.

**Mixed X- and Y-axis instance moves are also used.** trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01 moved eight instances and one polygon with a combination of x-deltas (+32 dbu) and y-deltas (−48, +48, +96 dbu). The trial was accepted as `gated_in` with `conn_preserved: true` and n_new_out_of_crop = 0. The x-delta of 32 dbu is not a multiple of 24 dbu (the M5.AUX.1 grid pitch), and the per-rule breakdown for this trial records 7 new M5.AUX.1 in-crop violations as a result. Off-grid x-moves are therefore applied when the connectivity-preservation criterion is met, even at the cost of new AUX.1 violations.

**Accepted moves can introduce new in-crop violations; connectivity preservation is the primary gate.** trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07 was accepted despite producing 3 new in-crop violations. trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01 was accepted with per-rule new in-crop violations of M5.AUX.1:7, M5.AUX.3:13, M5.S.4:2, and M5.W.5:5, while the net n_new_in_crop field reported 0 and n_new_out_of_crop was 0. In both cases `reason: conn_preserved` was the stated basis for gated acceptance. Downstream repair passes must account for residual in-crop violations (including AUX.1 offgrid and AUX.3 bend violations) left by connectivity-first acceptance decisions.

**M5 repairs co-move adjacent inter-layer constructs.** trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07 and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01 both record touched_layers that include M3, M4, V3, and V4 alongside M5. Vertical or horizontal instance moves that correct M5 violations simultaneously displace via stacks and the metal layers below. This is consistent with rule V4.M5.EN.2 (minimum V4 enclosure by M5 of 11 nm on two opposite sides) and V4.M5.AUX.2 (V4 must match M5 width perpendicular to M5 length): displacing M5 without co-moving V4 would break these via enclosure constraints. The repair engine co-moves the full via stack as a unit in all observed trials.

**Y-axis move deltas align to the 24 dbu grid; x-axis deltas may deviate.** The Y-direction deltas observed across both iterations (+72, +24, −24 dbu in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07; −48, +48, +96 dbu in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01) are all multiples of 24 dbu, consistent with M5.AUX.1 (M5 vertical edges must lie on a 24 nm grid) and M5.AUX.2 (minimum-width tracks at 192 dbu pitch with 48 dbu offset). The x-deltas in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01 (+32 dbu) are not multiples of 24 dbu and do generate M5.AUX.1 violations, which are tolerated under the conn_preserved gate. Move-magnitude selection for Y should therefore use 24 dbu multiples to avoid introducing AUX.1 violations; the same constraint does not appear to be enforced for X when connectivity is the deciding criterion.

**The cu_pool channel applies polygon-level resizes and can pre-empt unit_gate operations.** trial:i02.cu.poly:p879.00 applied a single `resize` on polygon p879 along the y-axis by +96 dbu, touching only M5, and reduced the violation count by 2 (delta_total −2, decision: applied). When trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01 subsequently attempted an x-axis move and a y-axis resize on the same polygon p879, those ops were dropped with reasons `reserved_by_cu_pool_winner` and `cu_pool:applied` respectively. The cu_pool channel operates at polygon granularity independently of the unit_gate channel; unit_gate trials must drop or skip ops on polygons already committed by cu_pool. The 96 dbu y-resize (4 × 24 dbu) used by cu_pool is grid-aligned.

**resize_via_shape on M5 is a high-yield repair primitive.** trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.02 applied a single `resize_via_shape` operation: y-axis, −88 dbu, on the M5 shape (shape_index 0) inside cell VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58. The result was a net reduction of 15 violations across two units (−9 in leaf_0002, −6 in leaf_0003), the largest single-operation yield observed in this layer's history. Touched layers were M4, M5, and V4; connectivity was preserved. The −88 dbu delta is not a simple multiple of 24 dbu (88 = 3 × 24 + 16), indicating that via-shape resizes use the via geometry's own dimensional constraints rather than the M5 routing grid. This operation category, distinct from move_instance and polygon move, directly adjusts the M5 extent within a via cell and resolves violations spanning multiple placement units simultaneously.
