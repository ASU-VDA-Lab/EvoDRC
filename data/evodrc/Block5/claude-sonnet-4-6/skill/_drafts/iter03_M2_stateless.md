## Instance-Move Operations on M2 (unit_gate channel)

All unit_gate trials that touched M2 through instance moves were accepted with `conn_preserved=true` and zero net new violations in the crop window. X-axis moves in the range of 4 to 112 dbu on a single instance consistently produced no new M2 violations (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.02, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0005.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0006.05, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.00). A pair of opposing x-axis moves (+36 dbu and −36 dbu on two separate instances within the same crop) also introduced no new violations (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.03). A larger single-instance move of +108 dbu combined with additional axis:x polygon resizes of +256 dbu and +328 dbu remained clean (trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row6.01). These records confirm that small-to-moderate x-axis instance displacements do not inherently create M2 width, spacing, or area violations provided connectivity is preserved.

## M2 Polygon Y-Axis Resizes

Y-axis shrinks of −64 dbu applied to M2 polygons are a confirmed repair strategy. In the cu_pool channel, simultaneously shrinking four M2 polygons (p894, p895, p896, p897) by −64 dbu y-axis, paired with a −40 dbu y-axis resize of the corresponding M3 via shape, reduced total DRC violations by 8 (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). The same −64 dbu y-axis resize applied to a single M2 polygon (p893) in a unit_gate crop was accepted with `conn_preserved=true` (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02). Do not apply y-axis M2 shrinks without confirming V1.M2.EN.2 and V2.M2.EN.1 enclosure margins are maintained; both rules require at minimum 5 nm enclosure on two opposite sides, and a −64 dbu reduction directly narrows the enclosure envelope.

## M2 Polygon X-Axis resize_end Operations

Extending the high end of M2 polygons in x by +28 dbu (polygon p946) and +64 dbu (polygon p968) via `resize_end` was accepted in iteration 3 with `conn_preserved=true`, but introduced 5 new in-crop violations across the touched layer set (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00). The touched layers for that trial include M2, M3, M4, M5, V2, and V4. The gating decision was `gated_in` because connectivity was preserved despite the new violations. Apply x-axis resize_end on M2 only when the connectivity benefit outweighs the new-violation debt; verify that the extended tip does not create new M2.S.2, M2.S.3, M2.S.4, M2.S.5, or M2.S.7 violations against adjacent features. The M2.S.7 rule is particularly sensitive: tip-to-tip gaps of 18 nm co-located with side-to-side spacing ≤32 nm and parallel run length <35 nm are forbidden, so any extension that lengthens a side edge must be cross-checked against M2.S.7 criteria.

## V2 Via-Shape Moves Affecting M2

Moving a V2 via shape +144 dbu in x (shape_index 1 of VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36) while keeping M2 and M3 polygons fixed reduced total violations by 7 (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01). This demonstrates that repositioning V2 within its M2 landing pad, rather than resizing M2, is a valid repair path for M2/V2/M3 interaction violations. V2.M2.EN.1 requires that V2 is enclosed by M2 on at least two opposite sides by 5 nm; a +144 dbu via displacement stays legal only if the M2 polygon is wide enough to maintain that enclosure after the shift. V1.M2.AUX.2 additionally requires that V1 width matches M2 width perpendicular to the M2 length direction; via moves must not disturb that coincidence.

## cu_pool and unit_gate Interaction: Op Dropping

When a cu_pool trial has already applied a resize to a via shape, a subsequent unit_gate trial targeting the same via drops the conflicting op automatically. In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02, the harness recorded an `assemble_drops` entry dropping a y-axis resize on VIA_VIA45 M5 shape because `cu_pool:applied` had already committed that change. The trial was still accepted. This means M2-touching unit_gate ops that also span via shapes shared with cu_pool repairs must be evaluated with the understanding that some sub-ops may be silently dropped at assembly time; the net M2 geometry may differ from the raw op list in such cases.

## Area and Geometry Constraints

M2.A.1 mandates a minimum area of 504 nm². No trial in this history introduced a new M2.A.1 violation, consistent with all recorded M2 resizes being reductions along one axis only while leaving the orthogonal dimension unchanged. The −64 dbu y-axis shrinks applied to polygons p894–p897 (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00) and p893 (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02) did not trigger area violations, indicating those polygons had sufficient y-extent before the shrink. Before applying any y-axis or x-axis shrink, verify the resulting area remains ≥504 nm². All ops in every recorded trial are rectilinear (x- or y-axis only), consistent with the GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL rule that prohibits non-90° edges on M2.

## Width and Spacing Minimums

M2.W.1 sets the minimum M2 width at 18 nm. M2.S.1 sets the minimum side-to-side spacing at 18 nm for edges longer than 36 nm. M2.S.2 requires 25 nm tip-to-side spacing when one edge is ≤36 nm and the other is >36 nm. M2.S.3 requires 27 nm tip-to-tip spacing when both edges are 24–36 nm. M2.S.4 and M2.S.5 require 31 nm tip-to-tip when one or both edges are <24 nm. M2.S.6 requires 20 nm corner-to-corner spacing (euclidean). No trial in this history introduced violations under any of these rules directly attributable to M2 polygon operations, confirming that the recorded resize magnitudes (−64 dbu y-axis, +28/+64 dbu x-axis resize_end) and instance shifts (4–112 dbu x-axis) remained within the design's available slack for these rules.
