**cu_pool and unit_gate channel conflict resolution on shared polygons**

When both cu_pool and unit_gate target the same polygon in the same assembly window, cu_pool wins: the unit_gate op for that polygon is dropped via assemble_drops with reason "reserved_by_cu_pool_winner" and the remaining unit_gate ops proceed (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01, where p879 x+32 was dropped while the seven instance moves were retained). **Width and area floor after any shrink**

After any M3 polygon is shrunk to resolve a via-enclosure or spacing violation, the resulting geometry must still satisfy M3.W.1 (≥ 18 nm width) and M3.A.1 (≥ 504 nm² area). No failing M3 width or area markers appear in the history following the 64 dbu shrink of trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, confirming that a 64 dbu reduction on those specific polygons remained above both floors. The symmetric y-shrink of polygon p892 in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 (resize_end y low +32 and resize_end y high −32, total 64 dbu y-reduction) introduced 15 new in-crop violations; while M3.W.1 and M3.A.1 failures cannot be separately isolated from that violation count without rule identity breakdown, the pre-shrink geometry must be verified to absorb the full planned reduction before committing. Pure y-translations via dual same-sign resize_end (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01, p891 y low −32 and y high −32) do not change polygon height and therefore do not directly threaten M3.W.1 or M3.A.1 in the translated dimension; however, such translations shift the polygon relative to adjacent M3 shapes and vias, creating new proximity risks that can still produce in-crop violations. These floors set a hard lower bound: do not apply shrinks larger than what the pre-shrink geometry can absorb while staying at or above 18 nm width and 504 nm² area (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01).

**Symmetric polygon shrinks vs. polygon translations via dual resize_end**

Two distinct dual-resize_end patterns on the same polygon and axis appear in the measured history and must not be confused. When the two resize_end ops carry opposite signs on the same axis (e.g., y low +32 and y high −32), the polygon undergoes a center-preserving symmetric shrink: both enclosure margins shrink simultaneously, risking V3.M3.EN.1 or V2.M3.EN.2 on both sides at once; the polygon height decreases by the sum of the absolute deltas (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02, p892, total 64 dbu y-shrink, 15 new in-crop violations). When the two resize_end ops carry the same sign on the same axis (e.g., y low −32 and y high −32), the polygon undergoes a pure translation by that delta in the named axis with no change in polygon height: M3.W.1 and M3.A.1 are not threatened in the translated dimension, but the polygon's position relative to adjacent shapes and vias shifts, introducing new proximity risks (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01, p891, y-translation −32 dbu, 8 new in-crop violations). Apply symmetric shrinks only after confirming both post-shrink enclosure margins satisfy V3.M3.EN.1 and V2.M3.EN.2 and that the residual polygon dimensions satisfy M3.W.1 and M3.A.1. Apply polygon translations via dual same-sign resize_end only after verifying that the shifted position does not introduce new M3 spacing violations or via-enclosure failures on the translated side (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01).

**Tip spacing rules require attention at short M3 edges**

M3.S.2 (tip-to-side, 25 nm), M3.S.3 (wide-tip-to-wide-tip, 27 nm), M3.S.4 (narrow-tip-to-narrow-tip, 31 nm), and M3.S.5 (mixed-tip, 31 nm) all apply specifically to M3 edges shorter than 36 nm; M3.S.4 and M3.S.5 apply to edges shorter than 24 nm and carry the most restrictive 31 nm requirement. The 40 dbu via-shape shrink and 64 dbu polygon shrinks in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 altered the y-extent of M3 segments and may have changed which tip-spacing rule each end edge falls under. The x+144 dbu via shape translation in trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 similarly shifted M3 edge proximity relationships in x. The resize_end ops on p946 (x high +28) and p968 (x high +64) in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00 extend M3 edges in x. The resize_end op on p951 (x high +128) in trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01 extends the right edge of p951 by 128 dbu in x; this is the largest single polygon-edge extension recorded in the M3 history and is certain to carry any pre-existing right-end tip edge (< 36 nm) well past the side-edge threshold (≥ 36 nm), switching that edge from a tip-category rule to M3.S.1 (18 nm side-to-side) and potentially creating new side-to-side spacing failures with formerly distant neighbors that were previously safe under tip rules. The simultaneous y-translation of p891 by −32 dbu shifts that polygon's tip and side edges relative to adjacent polygons in y, which can change which pairs of edges are in projection-based interaction. When resizing or translating M3 in any axis, track whether the resulting end-edge length crosses the 24 nm or 36 nm thresholds, because crossing those thresholds changes the applicable minimum spacing rule and the required clearance jumps from 18 nm (M3.S.1, side-to-side) up to 31 nm (M3.S.4/M3.S.5, narrow-tip scenarios); large extensions such as +128 dbu are particularly likely to trigger this reclassification and introduce new side-to-side violations with neighbors that were previously clear (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01).

**Corner-to-corner spacing (M3.S.6)**

M3.S.6 (20 nm euclidean corner-to-corner) is distinct from the projection-based spacing rules and fires on shapes that pass the side and tip checks but come close at corners. No M3.S.6-specific repair has been observed in any trial through iteration 4. The mixed x-direction instance moves in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00 (x+8, x+8, x−32) and the polygon resize_end extensions on p946 and p968 shift M3 geometry relative to neighboring polygons in opposing directions, which changes corner proximity relationships in both x and y simultaneously. The x+128 extension of p951 in trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01 extends a polygon rightward substantially while the y-translation of p891 by −32 shifts a nearby polygon downward; these two simultaneous M3 shape changes alter corner-to-corner distances for both polygons relative to their neighbors and relative to each other. Verify M3.S.6 clearance after any instance move or polygon resize that shifts M3 shapes, particularly when two nearby M3 polygons move in different directions or when a large single-edge extension is combined with a translation of an adjacent polygon (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01).
