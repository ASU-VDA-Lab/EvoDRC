## Minimum Spacing and Width Reference

All M2 shapes must be at least 18 nm wide (M2.W.1) and contain at least 504 nm² of area (M2.A.1). Every gated-in trial that applied resize_end operations to M2 polygons preserved width at or above 18 nm — trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row5.05 applied end resizes of 48–156 dbu on four M2 polygons and produced zero new M2 violations — showing that resize_end deltas must be chosen to keep the resulting width above 18 nm.

Side-to-side spacing between M2 long edges (both > 36 nm) requires 18 nm minimum (M2.S.1). Tip-to-side spacing (one edge ≤ 36 nm, other > 36 nm) requires 25 nm minimum (M2.S.2). Tip-to-tip spacing depends on edge lengths: 27 nm when both tips are 24–36 nm (M2.S.3); 31 nm when one tip is in the 24–36 nm range and the other is < 24 nm (M2.S.5); 31 nm when both tips are < 24 nm (M2.S.4). Corner-to-corner Euclidean spacing requires 20 nm (M2.S.6).

The only M2 spacing violations in this history are one M2.S.3 and one M2.S.7, both introduced in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03. No M2.S.1, M2.S.2, M2.S.4, M2.S.5, M2.S.6, or M2.A.1 violations appear in any trial.

## M2.S.3: Wide-Tip-to-Wide-Tip Spacing

M2.S.3 fires when two M2 edges in the 24–36 nm length range are separated tip-to-tip (projection) by less than 27 nm. The single M2.S.3 violation in this history was introduced in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03, which applied block-wide instance moves of +32 and -16 dbu to many instances simultaneously. During assembly, a large number of those moves were dropped as external_conflict_dropped (conflicting with trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02) or cross_crop_first_wins, and the remaining partial move set left at least one pair of M2 wide tips within 27 nm.

Do not assume that the per-unit M2 solution is DRC-clean after assemble-drop conflict resolution removes a portion of the applied moves: trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03 demonstrates that the retained subset introduced M2.S.3:1 even though the full intended move set from each unit was individually correct. Verify tip-to-tip clearance for the post-assemble M2 geometry, not only for the pre-assemble per-unit solution.

## M2.S.7: Compound Tip-to-Tip and Side Spacing

M2.S.7 is a two-part compound rule. A tip-to-tip gap of ~18 nm is forbidden when the same M2 shape pair also has a side-to-side spacing of ≤ 32 nm. Independently, when the side spacing is ≤ 32 nm, the parallel run length of the adjacent stripes must be ≥ 35 nm. One M2.S.7 violation was introduced in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03 via the same assemble-drop conflict resolution that produced the M2.S.3 violation: after external_conflict_dropped removals, the surviving M2 configuration contained an ~18 nm end gap adjacent to a section with side spacing ≤ 32 nm and run length below 35 nm.

Avoid move sequences that simultaneously tighten a tip gap toward 18 nm and reduce run length below 35 nm, especially when the move set is subject to partial application through inter-unit conflict resolution (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03). When side spacing in the local M2 context is already ≤ 32 nm, do not apply end moves that shorten the parallel overlap below 35 nm.

## M2.S.8: Diagonal Gap-Center Spacing

M2.S.8 requires that the Euclidean distance between tip-to-tip gap centers on different M2 tracks is ≥ 80 nm. Gap centers are computed by shrinking each 18 nm tip-to-tip gap region by 8.5 nm per side; gaps narrower than 17 nm collapse to zero and produce no trackable center. No M2.S.8 violations appear in any trial in this history. M2.S.8 requires the presence of 18 nm tip-to-tip gaps; when M2.S.7 is also violated on the same gap pattern, both rules fire. The 80 nm diagonal-center constraint applies across tracks even when each individual gap satisfies M2.S.7 independently.

## Gating Behavior

The unit_gate channel accepts a trial when conn_preserved=true and n_new_out_of_crop=0, regardless of the count of new in-crop violations. Trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02 introduced 80 new in-crop violations (M1, M4, V1, V2, V4 layers) and was accepted. Trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03 introduced 47 new in-crop violations including M2.S.3:1 and M2.S.7:1 and was also accepted. Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 introduced 8 new in-crop violations and was accepted (n_new_out_of_crop=0). The out-of-crop criterion is the hard gate: no accepted trial in this history has any new out-of-crop violations.

Connectivity breakage causes immediate rejection. Trial:i01.ug.leaf_0034.12 was gated_out with conn_preserved=false (reason: conn_broken) and 89 new in-crop violations. That trial's ops included large asymmetric M2 polygon end resizes (+192, +100, and -4 dbu on the right edge of different polygons) combined with six instance moves spanning M1/M2/M3/M4/V1/V2 in a locus covering most of the block (1728,2068–14256,13680). Avoid combining large asymmetric end resizes on M2 polygons with simultaneous instance moves across many layers in a single trial, as this combination severed connectivity in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0034.12.

The cu_pool channel applies a net-positive criterion: a trial is accepted only when delta_total > 0. Trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 was rejected because delta_total=0. Trial:i03.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 was applied because delta_total=-12 (net reduction of 12 violations).

## Inter-Unit Conflict Resolution

When two or more units have overlapping crop regions and claim the same instances or polygons, the assemble step applies three resolution mechanisms documented in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02 and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03:

**cross_crop_first_wins**: The first unit's polygon move is retained; the second unit's conflicting move on the same polygon is dropped. In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03, leaf_0003's x-axis moves on polygons p1143 and p1142 and y-axis move on p1561 were retained; leaf_0004's moves on those same polygons were dropped.

**external_conflict_dropped**: When two units claim the same instance with different delta values, both moves are dropped and the instance receives no displacement. Forty-one external_conflict_dropped events occurred for the leaf_0003/leaf_0004 pair in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03. Instances dropped this way retain their pre-repair M2 position, which satisfies neither unit's DRC intent. The M2.S.3:1 and M2.S.7:1 violations introduced in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03 resulted from this mechanism: the partially applied move set left M2 tip pairs in positions that neither unit's individual solution would have produced.

**external_duplicate**: When two units propose the same instance move with identical delta, one copy is kept and the move is applied normally.

When solving M2 violations in units whose crop regions overlap with adjacent units, moves that match the adjacent unit's direction and magnitude resolve as external_duplicate and are applied; moves that differ resolve as external_conflict_dropped and are discarded (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03). Verify M2 tip spacing for the post-assemble geometry — accounting for which moves survive conflict resolution — rather than only for the pre-assemble per-unit solution.
