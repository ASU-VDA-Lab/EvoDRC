## Rule Coverage and Violation Sources Observed

**M3.S.2 (tip-to-side spacing, 25 nm)** was the only M3 spacing rule that generated new in-crop violations across the accepted trials. Trial i01.ug.leaf_0035.13 applied uniform -64 dbu y-axis resize to 12 M3 polygons (p1501, p1493, p1491, p1460, p1459, p1458, p1543, p1538, p1535, p1420, p1435, p1434) and introduced 2 new M3.S.2 violations; the y-shrink reduces shape length along that axis, converting previously long edges into short tip edges (length <= 36 nm) that fall within 25 nm of adjacent longer side edges. Trial i02.ug.leaf_0004.03 moved M3 polygons p1143 by +32 dbu on x, p1142 by -16 dbu on x, and p1561 by +32 dbu on y, also introducing 2 new M3.S.2 violations. Both trials were accepted (gated_in, conn_preserved, n_new_out_of_crop == 0). Do not apply y-axis shrinks to M3 polygons in regions where adjacent M3 shapes present long side edges at close projection distances; the resulting tip geometry will introduce M3.S.2 violations (trial i01.ug.leaf_0035.13). Similarly, lateral x- or y-axis polygon moves that displace M3 shapes toward adjacent long-edge metal will create new tip-to-side gaps below 25 nm (trial i02.ug.leaf_0004.03).

**V2.M3.EN.2 (V2 enclosure by M3, 5 nm on two opposite sides)** was introduced in both iter-2 unit_gate trials. Trial i02.ug.leaf_0003.02 moved M3 polygons p1145 (+32 dbu x), p1144 (-16 dbu x), p1143 (+32 dbu x), p1142 (-16 dbu x), p1563 (-64 dbu y), p1562 (-112 dbu y), p1561 (-96 dbu y) alongside a large set of instance moves, and introduced 6 new V2.M3.EN.2 violations. Trial i02.ug.leaf_0004.03 moved M3 polygons p1143 (+32 dbu x), p1142 (-16 dbu x), p1561 (+32 dbu y) and introduced 6 new V2.M3.EN.2 violations. Moving M3 polygons on either axis without co-moving their enclosed V2 vias displaces the enclosing metal relative to the via, reducing projection-axis enclosure below the 5 nm minimum on at least one pair of opposite sides. Both trials were accepted despite the new V2.M3.EN.2 debt; the gate accepted the in-crop additions because connectivity was preserved and no out-of-crop violations accrued.

## Connectivity Failure from Combined M3 Ops

Trial i01.ug.leaf_0034.12 combined a resize_via_shape on M3 (y-axis -40 dbu, cell VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36), a right-edge resize of M3 polygon p1214 by -4 dbu, a right-edge resize of M3 polygon p1178 by +192 dbu, a right-edge resize of M3 polygon p1255 by +100 dbu, and six instance moves across the same locus. This combination broke connectivity (conn_broken) and the trial was gated_out, leaving 89 new in-crop violations uncorrected. Combining M3 tip shrinks, large asymmetric M3 end extensions, and instance moves in a single batch within this design region produced a connectivity break that caused gated_out; the ops were not applied (trial i01.ug.leaf_0034.12).

## Cross-Crop Conflict Resolution for M3 Polygons

Trials i02.ug.leaf_0003.02 and i02.ug.leaf_0004.03 both requested moves on shared M3 polygons p1143, p1142, and p1561. The assemble for leaf_0004.03 recorded cross_crop_first_wins drops for all three polygons, deferring to leaf_0003.02's prior claim. The net applied M3 state for those polygons is: p1143 at +32 dbu x, p1142 at -16 dbu x, p1561 at -96 dbu y, as applied by the winning leaf (trial i02.ug.leaf_0003.02). When two leaves claim conflicting moves on the same M3 polygon, the first-winning leaf's delta is applied and the second leaf's op is silently dropped; the final M3 geometry reflects only the winning leaf's operations (trial i02.ug.leaf_0003.02, confirmed by assemble_drops in trial i02.ug.leaf_0004.03).
