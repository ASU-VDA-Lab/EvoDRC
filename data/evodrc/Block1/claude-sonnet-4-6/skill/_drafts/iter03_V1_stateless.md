## V1 Repair Operation Patterns

Every trial that touched V1 also touched M1 and M2 simultaneously; no trial isolated a V1 change without co-modifying both metal layers (trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row3.03, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row6.06, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.00, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02). This is structurally required by V1.AUX.1 (V1 must reside inside M1 ∩ M2) and V1.M2.AUX.2 (V1 width must equal M2 width perpendicular to the M2 run direction): any positional change to a V1 instance must be reflected in both bounding metal layers or new violations will be introduced.

The dominant repair operation is `move_instance`. Pure instance moves with no accompanying polygon resize succeeded in trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row10.01 (deltas +108, +36 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row3.03 (+72, +108, +72 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row4.04 (+8, +36, +72 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row9.08 (+36, +36, +36 dbu), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0004.09 (+36 dbu), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0020.10 (−56 dbu), and trial:i01.ug.leaf_0031.11 (+36 dbu). Moves in both positive and negative x are valid; trial:i01.ug.leaf_0020.10 demonstrates a −56 dbu displacement resolving a V1 spacing constraint without breaking connectivity.

When instance moves are combined with `resize_end` on M2 polygons, the resize extends or contracts a metal end to maintain V1.M2.EN.2 enclosure (minimum 5 nm on two opposite sides). This pattern appears in trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row1.00 (instance +136 dbu, `resize_end` high +136 dbu on p1320), trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row5.05 (instance +136 dbu, multiple resize_end ops including high +156, low +56, high +92, high +48 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row6.06 (instances ±72 dbu, resize_end ops high +36, +128, low +36, high +128, +92 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row8.07 (instances +64/+136/−64/+136/+64 dbu, resize_end high +192, +192 dbu), and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.00 (instance +100 dbu, resize_end high +60 dbu on p1385). Always apply a corresponding M2 `resize_end` when an instance move would otherwise displace a V1 outside the M2 enclosure boundary.

Fine-grained displacements as small as 4 dbu are effective. Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 moved VIA12 instances i0485 and i0188 by dx=+4 dbu each to clear M1 spacing violations; the V1 layer was touched as a side effect of repositioning the parent via cells, and all enclosure and alignment constraints (V1.M2.EN.2, V1.M2.AUX.2) were preserved because the M2 overlap remained valid after the small shift. Do not round small required displacements up to larger grid increments when the circuit geometry allows the minimal move.

## V1 Spacing Rule Sensitivity

V1.S.1 governs projection-based spacing between V1 instances along and across M2 tracks (18 nm on same track, 27 nm between non-aligned parallel tracks, 18 nm between aligned parallel tracks). The rule operates on extended mask geometry (`v1_mask`) that incorporates 5 nm M2 end-cap regions, so V1 instances positioned near M2 ends are subject to the mask's inflated footprint. All successful gated_in trials that cleared V1.S.1 violations used `move_instance` to increase separation; no trial attempted to shrink an adjacent polygon to create clearance for a fixed V1 instance.

V1.S.2 (Euclidean corner-to-corner ≥ 23 nm, both vias with 5 nm end-cap) and V1.S.3 (Euclidean corner-to-corner ≥ 30 nm, both vias without 5 nm end-cap) operate on Euclidean distance and therefore fire on diagonal arrangements that projection-only checks miss. No trial in the measured history records a V1.S.2 or V1.S.3 violation as the named target, but the same instance-move pattern used for V1.S.1 is the only demonstrated repair vehicle.

## Enclosure Rules and Their Interaction with Moves

V1.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V1 by ≥ 5 nm on one pair of opposite sides and ≥ 2 nm on the other. V1.M2.EN.2 requires M2 to enclose V1 by ≥ 5 nm on both sides of one axis or by 5 nm on one side and flush (0 nm) on the other. When a move_instance displaces a V1 relative to a fixed M1 or M2 polygon, these enclosure margins decrease on the leading edge and increase on the trailing edge. Trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row8.07 demonstrates the pattern: two instances were extended with resize_end high +192 dbu on p1388 and p1345 after moving the corresponding instances +136 dbu, maintaining the 5 nm enclosure on the high-x side. Always check that both V1.M1.EN.1 and V1.M2.EN.2 are satisfied on all four edges after any instance translation.

V1.M2.AUX.2 mandates that V1 width in the direction perpendicular to the M2 run equals the M2 width in that direction (V1 must share exactly two edges with M2). Because V1 width along M2 is governed by V1.W.1 (minimum 18 nm), any resize of M2 in the along-length direction that reduces M2 below 18 nm in the perpendicular dimension will simultaneously violate V1.W.1 and V1.M2.AUX.2. No trial resized a V1 polygon directly; all width-related adjustments acted on M2 polygons or moved V1-containing instances without altering the V1 geometry within the cell.

## Locus Scope and Connectivity Risk

The sole gated_out trial touching V1 (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0034.12) used a locus spanning nearly the full design extent (1728,2068) to (14256,13680), involved 10 ops across M1, M2, M3, M4, V1, and V2, and introduced 89 new violations in crop while breaking connectivity. All gated_in trials used loci scoped to individual rows or small cells. Use focused loci covering only the units containing the V1 violation under repair; wide loci that span multiple rows increase the probability of introducing new cross-layer spacing violations and breaking connectivity.

## Multi-Layer Trial Context

Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 (iter 3) touched V1 while targeting M3.S.2 and M1.S.2 violations. The ops shrank M3 polygon p1543 right edge by −8 dbu, shrank M3 polygon p1458 right edge by −8 dbu, moved VIA23 instance i0180 by dx=−40 dbu, and moved VIA12 instances i0485 and i0188 by dx=+4 dbu each. V1 was affected through VIA12 repositioning; the M2 overlap for each VIA12 was verified against the parent M2 polygon span before the move was accepted. When V1 repair is triggered by a multi-layer op set, verify that the M2 polygon covering each displaced VIA12 (and therefore the embedded V1 land) retains sufficient overlap on both sides to satisfy V1.M2.EN.2 and V1.M2.AUX.2 before committing the move.
