## Operation Taxonomy

All accepted V1 repairs in this history use two operation types: `move_instance` and `resize_end`. Direct V1 polygon edits do not appear; V1 geometry changes arise exclusively through instance displacement. This pattern holds across all 12 accepted trials, from trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row1.00 through trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.00.

## Movement Axis and Granularity

All instance moves in V1-touching repairs displace along the x-axis only (delta_dbu of the form [x, 0]); no y-axis displacements appear in any accepted repair. Move magnitudes are multiples of 4 dbu; observed values include 8, 36, 56, 64, 72, 100, 108, and 136 dbu. The minimum move delta observed is 8 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row4.04). Sub-4-dbu displacements do not appear in any trial, accepted or rejected.

## resize_end as a Companion to move_instance

When a `move_instance` displaces an instance containing V1 but the bounding M2 wire is a standalone polygon not part of the same instance, a `resize_end` on that M2 polygon must accompany the move to keep M2 co-extensive with V1 perpendicular to the direction of travel, satisfying V1.M2.AUX.2 and V1.M2.EN.2. This compound pattern is observed in trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row5.05, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row6.06, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row8.07, and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.00. When M2 travels with V1 inside the same instance, no `resize_end` is needed, as demonstrated by trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row9.08 and trial:i01.ug.leaf_0004.09.

The `decision` field is governed by `conn_preserved`, not by the count of new violations introduced.
