## V4 Repair Knowledge (iteration 4)

### Rule summary and measured repair outcomes

**V4.W.1** (minimum width 24 nm along M5 length) and **V4.M4.EN.1 / V4.M5.EN.2** (11 nm enclosure on two opposite sides by M4 and M5, respectively) share the same geometric sensitivity: the via shape must be wide enough to satisfy the width rule while remaining enclosed by both metal layers. Trial trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01 resolved 52 violations across two windows (unit:leaf_0034 and unit:leaf_0035) using a single `resize_via_shape` operation on M5, y-axis, -88 dbu on cell VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58. The operation was applied via the cu_pool channel and connectivity was preserved. Shrinking the M5 shape along y in the cu_pool channel is therefore a directly confirmed, connection-safe strategy for eliminating violations in the locus [1728, 2068, 14256, 14132].

**V4.AUX.1** (V4 must lie inside both M4 and M5) is at risk whenever instance moves shift metal polygons without a corresponding adjustment to the via shape. Trial trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01 combined a [-76, 0] dbu instance move for i0181 with a +44 dbu `resize_end` on p1154 (y-axis, low end) and touched M4, M5, and V4; the gate check showed zero new in-crop and zero new out-of-crop violations, confirming that this paired move-and-resize approach kept V4 safely inside both enclosing metals. Trial trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00 further confirms this: a [+76, 0] dbu move of i0181 paired with a -32 dbu `resize_end` on p1187 (x-axis, high end), also touching M4, M5, and V4 in locus [4020, 14548, 4320, 15936], produced zero new in-crop and zero new out-of-crop violations with connectivity preserved. Trial trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 adds a third data point: a single [-76, 0] dbu move of i0181 with no paired `resize_end`, touching M4, M5, and V4 in locus [4180, 14548, 4364, 14732], also produced zero new in-crop and zero new out-of-crop violations with connectivity preserved. Across these three trials, x-axis-only instance moves touching M4/M5/V4 have produced zero new V4.AUX.1 violations regardless of whether a paired resize_end was included.

**V4.M5.AUX.2** (V4 must match M5 width in the direction perpendicular to M5 length) is the rule most vulnerable to instance moves with a y-component. Trial trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02 applied 85 ops including moves with y-components ([32, -64], [32, -112], [-16, -64], [-16, -112], [32, -96], [-16, -96]) and introduced 4 new V4.M5.AUX.2 violations while touching M2, M3, M4, M5, M6, V2, V3, V4, and V5.

Trial trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01 reinforces this finding with a larger dataset: 16 pure y-axis moves (all zero x-component, y-deltas of +/-48 or +/-96 dbu on instances i0267, i0265, i0392, i0393, i0341, i0337, i0416, i0401, i0189, i0193, i0127, i0130, i0175, i0182, i0043, i0038), touching M3, M4, M5, V3, and V4 in locus [1728, 2068, 14256, 13680], introduced 20 new in-crop violations with zero new out-of-crop violations. Connectivity was preserved and the decision was `gated_in`, but the 20 new in-crop markers represent residual debt. The trial confirms that pure y-axis instance moves alone--without any x-component--are sufficient to introduce new violations when M4, M5, and V4 are among the touched layers.

Trial trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02 applied 26 ops: 24 pure x-axis moves (+/-32 or -16 dbu) and 2 compound moves carrying a y-component ([32, -96] on i0345 and [-16, -96] on i0505), touching M3, M4, M5, M6, V3, V4, and V5 in locus [1728, 3072, 14256, 14132]. The trial introduced 8 new in-crop violations with zero out-of-crop violations; connectivity was preserved and the decision was `gated_in`. The 2 y-bearing ops are the only ops in the trial with non-zero y-delta, and their presence is consistent with the pattern in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02 and trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01 in which y-displacement of instances touching M5 and V4 introduces new violations.

By contrast, trial trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03 (x-only moves surviving conflict drops, zero new V4 violations), trial trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00 (single x-only paired move, zero new V4 violations), and trial trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 (single x-only move, zero new V4 violations) establish that pure x-axis instance moves do not introduce new V4.M5.AUX.2 violations even when M4, M5, and V4 are touched.

### Operational guidance grounded in measured records

Do not apply instance moves with a y-component when M4, M5, and V4 are among the touched layers. Trial trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01 demonstrates that 16 pure y-axis moves introduce 20 new in-crop violations. Trial trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02 demonstrates that 2 compound moves with y=-96 embedded in a 26-op batch introduce 8 new in-crop violations. Trial trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02 previously established that y-bearing compound moves in a large batch introduce V4.M5.AUX.2 violations specifically. The risk applies to pure y-axis moves as well as x+y compound moves: trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01 confirms pure y-axis moves are not exempt.

Pure x-axis instance moves touching M4/M5/V4 are safe: trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00, and trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 all produced zero new V4 violations with x-only moves.

When the cu_pool channel targets a via cell directly (as in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01), prefer `resize_via_shape` on the M5 layer along y before considering instance-level moves; the single-op, single-axis shrink resolved 52 violations without introducing any new violations.

For unit_gate moves touching M4/M5/V4 along the x-axis, a paired `resize_end` on the affected metal polygon endpoint is one confirmed safe pattern: trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01 (y-axis resize paired with y-axis move) and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00 (x-axis resize paired with x-axis move) both confirm zero-violation gate results under this discipline. Trial trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 also achieved zero violations with a pure x-axis move and no paired resize_end, in a small single-op locus [4180, 14548, 4364, 14732]. The paired resize_end approach is confirmed safe; the bare single x-axis move was also safe in the trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 context.

**V4.S.1, V4.S.2, V4.S.3** (33 nm spacing, same-net and different-net, euclidean and projection) did not appear in any violation delta across any of the recorded trials. No spacing-related V4 repair guidance can be drawn from the current history.

**GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL** did not appear in any trial result for V4. All recorded ops (resize_via_shape, move_instance, resize_end) produced orthogonal geometry changes; no non-orthogonal edges were introduced.
