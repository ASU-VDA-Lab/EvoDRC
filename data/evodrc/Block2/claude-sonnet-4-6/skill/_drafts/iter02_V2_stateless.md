## Repair Pattern: Y-axis M3 shrink paired with V2 shape resize

**Only two trials exist in this layer's history.** Both target the same cell (`def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36`) and apply an identical five-operation sequence: one `resize_via_shape` on the M3 layer of the via cell (`delta_dbu: -40`, y-axis) followed by four `resize` ops on M3 polygons (`delta_dbu: -64` each, y-axis). Both were applied (`decision: applied`), both yielded `delta_total: -8`, and both preserved connectivity (`conn_preserved: true`). trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00.

**The via shape resize must accompany the M3 resize.** Rule V2.M3.AUX.2 requires V2 to be exactly the same width as M3 along the direction perpendicular to the M3 length. Rule V2.M3.EN.2 requires M3 to enclose V2 by 5 nm on two opposite sides. Shrinking M3 in the y-axis without adjusting the via shape would break either AUX.2 (V2 no longer width-matched to M3) or EN.2 (enclosure lost). The repair sequence always issues `resize_via_shape` before the M3 polygon resizes, keeping V2 aligned. trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00.

**The same cell was repaired in two consecutive global iterations at different windows.** In iter 1, violations fell from 27 to 19 in `unit:leaf_0012` (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). In iter 2, violations fell from 20 to 12 in `unit:leaf_0003` (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). The affected windows differ while the locus coordinates (`[1728, 2068, 10368, 9812]`) and operation counts remain identical, meaning the cell definition is reused across multiple placement windows and repairing it in one iteration does not exhaust the violation budget across all windows.

**Connectivity is reliably preserved by this op sequence.** Both applied trials reported `conn_preserved: true`. The operation resizes M3 ends inward; it does not sever the M3 wire between the V2 instance and adjacent metal, so connectivity to M2 (required by V2.AUX.1 for V2 to remain inside both M2 and M3) is maintained. trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00.

**Rule V2.S.1 governs projection-measured spacing between V2 masks.** The mask construction in the rule extends coincident M3 edges outward by 5 nm. Shrinking M3 in y moves those extended edges inward, enlarging the effective spacing between V2 masks on parallel M3 tracks. A reduction of 64 dbu per M3 polygon edge directly increases inter-mask spacing on the projection axis. The -8 violation improvement per application is consistent with this geometry. trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00.

**Do not apply this sequence unilaterally without checking V2.W.1 and V2.M2.EN.1.** V2.W.1 sets minimum V2 width at 18 nm along the M3 length direction. V2.M2.EN.1 requires M2 to enclose V2 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides. Both rules constrain how far V2 can be shrunk before new violations are introduced. The two trials applied -40 dbu on the via shape without producing V2.W.1 or V2.M2.EN.1 regressions (delta_total was negative in both), but neither trial documents the absolute post-repair V2 dimension, so the margin above 18 nm is not known from these records alone.

**Corner-to-corner spacing rules (V2.S.2, V2.S.3, V2.S.4) are not yet exercised by recorded trials.** No trial explicitly targets Euclidean-distance violations between via instances of differing end-cap categories (wec vs. nec). The two available trials both show projection-axis improvements consistent with V2.S.1. No citation can be made for corner-spacing behavior under this repair pattern.
