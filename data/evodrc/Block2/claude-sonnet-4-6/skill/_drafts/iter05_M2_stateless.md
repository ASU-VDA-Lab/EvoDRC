## Effective Repair Strategies

**X-axis instance translation reliably eliminates M2 spacing violations with zero collateral cost.** All seven unit-gate trials in iteration 1 moved instances in the +x direction (36 dbu or 64 dbu) and were accepted with `n_new_in_crop=0` and `n_new_out_of_crop=0` (trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row3.01, trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row5.02, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0004.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0007.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0011.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.03). The step sizes observed are 36 dbu and 64 dbu; both cleared all in-crop violations without introducing new ones. A larger step of 128 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.03) also succeeded cleanly. Prefer the smallest step (36 dbu) that clears the violation before trying larger steps.

**When an instance is translated in x, pair each move with a `resize_end` on the same axis and same delta for any M2 polygon that bridges the moved instance to a stationary net.** This pattern appeared in trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row3.01 (move +36, resize high +36 on p1040), trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row5.02 (move +64, resize high +64 on p1059 and p1057), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0011.06 (move +36, resize high +36 on p1052), and trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.03 (move +128, resize high +184 on p1065). Failure to extend the bridging M2 polygon will break connectivity and violate V1.M2.EN.2 or V1.M2.AUX.2. All cited trials preserved connectivity (`conn_preserved=true`).

**For the `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` via cell, shrinking the M3 body in y by 64 dbu on each of the four flanking M2/M3 polygons, combined with a 40 dbu y-shrink on the via-shape itself, reduces the M2-touching violation count by 8 per application.** This was confirmed twice: trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 (iter 1, `delta_total=-8`) and trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 (iter 2, `delta_total=-8`). M2 is recorded in `touched_layers` for both applications, indicating the enclosure change propagates to M2 edges and the via shrink resolves violations attributable to V2.M2.EN.1 or adjacent spacing rules. Do not reverse this operation: expanding the same via by +40 dbu in y was `rejected_net_positive` with `delta_total=0` at iter 5 (trial:i05.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00).

**Y-axis instance moves (+36 dbu) are safe when combined with x-axis moves and M2 polygon adjustments but can introduce collateral M1 violations.** In trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01 a y move of +36 on i0063 was accepted with `conn_preserved=true`, and in trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01 a y move of +36 on i0071 was accepted but introduced one new M1.A.1 violation (`n_new_in_crop=1`). The new violation is on M1, not M2, so the M2 repair itself was net-neutral; however, the trade must be tracked as a debt against M1.

## Enclosure Rules (V1.M2.EN.2, V1.M2.AUX.2, V2.M2.EN.1)

**M2 must enclose V1 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides; enclosure of 5 nm on one side and 0 nm on the other is also legal (V1.M2.EN.2).** V1 must also match M2 width exactly in the direction perpendicular to M2 length (V1.M2.AUX.2). All successful instance-move trials maintained `conn_preserved=true`, which the harness verifies by checking these enclosure constraints before committing. Do not resize M2 polygon ends below the 5 nm enclosure on the non-zero side.

**V2.M2.EN.1 requires M2 to enclose V2 by at least 5 nm on at least two opposite sides.** The recurring `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` cu_pool shrink (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00) reduces M3 and via-shape size rather than M2 size, preserving M2's enclosure margin on V2. Shrinking M2 instead to resolve V2.M2.EN.1 conflicts is not supported by any trial in this history.

## Spacing Rule Thresholds and Edge-Length Sensitivity

**M2.W.1 requires minimum width 18 nm.** No trial recorded a resize operation that narrowed M2 below 18 nm; all accepted resize_end operations extended polygons outward (positive delta on high end, or negative delta on low end moving the low face outward), consistent with preserving width (trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row3.01, trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row5.02, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0011.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.03, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01).

**M2.S.1 (18 nm side-to-side) applies only to edges longer than 36 nm; M2.S.2 (25 nm tip-to-side) applies when one edge is <= 36 nm and the other is > 36 nm; M2.S.3 (27 nm tip-to-tip) applies when both edges are >= 24 nm and <= 36 nm; M2.S.4 (31 nm tip-to-tip) applies when both edges are < 24 nm; M2.S.5 (31 nm) applies when one edge is >= 24 nm <= 36 nm and the other is < 24 nm.** Instance translations of 36-128 dbu resolved all in-crop violations in the seven iter-1 unit-gate trials without introducing new violations, demonstrating that these step sizes reliably bring M2 edges outside the spacing exclusion zones for the violation geometries encountered in Block2 (trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row1.00 through trial:i01.ug.leaf_0011.06).

**M2.S.6 (20 nm corner-to-corner, euclidian) and M2.S.7 (18 nm tip-to-tip co-located with side spacing <= 32 nm is forbidden; parallel run length must be >= 35 nm when side spacing <= 32 nm) are sensitive to combined positional and length changes.** No trial in this history recorded a violation introduced for M2.S.6 or M2.S.7 as a result of the accepted moves, indicating that the step sizes and resize-end pairing pattern preserved the required corner and run-length margins.

**M2.A.1 requires minimum area 504 nm-sq.** All resize_end operations in this history extended M2 polygons rather than shrinking them, which increases area and cannot violate M2.A.1. Avoid any resize that reduces M2 polygon length or width if the resulting area could fall below 504 nm-sq.

## Operation Ordering and Drop Handling

**When the cu_pool channel proposes a via expansion that would be net-positive, the harness drops it at assembly time.** In trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01 the `assemble_drops` field records the +40 dbu M3 via-shape expansion being dropped with reason `cu_pool:rejected_net_positive`, consistent with the standalone rejection in trial:i05.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00. Do not re-propose expanding `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` in y; the iter-5 evidence confirms this direction has zero benefit at the current design state.

**Multi-layer operations involving M2 alongside M4, M5, V4 require larger x translations (-64 dbu) and may need y corrections.** In trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01 resolving the leaf_0002 unit required moving five instances by -64 dbu in x plus two items by +36 dbu in y, touching M1/M2/M4/M5/V1/V4. The operation was accepted with `conn_preserved=true` and one new in-crop violation (subsequently addressed). When M2 is part of a multi-metal stack move, verify that each metal layer's spacing and enclosure constraints are preserved simultaneously.
