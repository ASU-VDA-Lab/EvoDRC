## Repair Strategy

**Co-modify V2 via cell and M3 metal together in a single repair pass for loci where the via-shrink template is effective.** Both completed repairs at iterations 1–2 applied five operations atomically: one `resize_via_shape` on the V2 cell along Y and four `resize` ops on the associated M3 polygons along Y (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). Splitting these into separate passes risks introducing transient violations on whichever layer is modified first. However, **the same via-shrink template (40 dbu Y, single op) yields delta_total = 0 at certain loci and is rejected as net-positive** (trial:i05.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, windows leaf_0001 and leaf_0002 both held, delta = 0). The template is not universally effective; locus geometry determines whether the via resize actually clears violations.

**When the via-cell resize is rejected by cu_pool, instance moves combined with M1 polygon resize can succeed.** trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01 was gated in on the unit_gate channel using three ops — `move_instance` on i0071 (Y +36 dbu), `move_instance` on i0063 (X +36 dbu), and `resize_end` on M1 polygon p1036 (X-axis, high end, +56 dbu) — after the via cell resize was dropped from that assembly (`assemble_drops` records the Y-40-dbu `resize_via_shape` as `cu_pool:rejected_net_positive`). `conn_preserved: true` was reported. This is a distinct repair pattern from the via-shrink template.

**Shrink magnitude on M3 (64 dbu per polygon) must exceed the shrink on the V2 cell (40 dbu) when reducing Y-extent.** In trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, four M3 polygons (p962–p965) were each shrunk 64 dbu while via cell VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 was shrunk 40 dbu. trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 replicates the same magnitudes on polygons p958–p961 at a different locus with identical outcome. This asymmetry satisfies the M3 end-cap enclosure geometry required by V2.M3.EN.2 (≥5 nm enclosure on two opposite sides) and keeps the V2 shape within M3 as required by V2.AUX.1 and V2.M3.AUX.2; a uniform shrink at the same magnitude on both layers would leave the via cell underenclosed or flush the M3 edge with the via edge on the wrong side.

## cu_pool / unit_gate Channel Mechanics

**The cu_pool channel evaluates a proposed repair in isolation; a net-zero or net-positive delta triggers rejection regardless of conn_preserved status.** trial:i05.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 was rejected (`decision: "rejected_net_positive"`) even though `conn_preserved: true` and all windows were stable (delta_total = 0). cu_pool requires a strictly negative violation delta to accept.

**Ops rejected by cu_pool are removed from the assembly before unit_gate evaluation (`assemble_drops`).** trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01 records the Y-40-dbu `resize_via_shape` in `assemble_drops` with reason `cu_pool:rejected_net_positive`, yet the remaining three-op assembly was gated in. The unit_gate channel therefore operates on the post-drop op set, not the originally proposed set.

**The unit_gate channel can gate in a trial that introduces new in-crop violations if connectivity is preserved.** trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01 reports `n_new_in_crop: 1` (M1.A.1, one new violation) alongside `decision: "gated_in"` with `reason: "conn_preserved"`. The unit_gate acceptance criterion is conn_preserved, not zero new violations; downstream passes must address any newly introduced M1.A.1 violations.
