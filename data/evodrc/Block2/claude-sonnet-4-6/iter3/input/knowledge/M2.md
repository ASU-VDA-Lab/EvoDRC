## M2 Repair Patterns -- Iteration 2 Knowledge

### X-direction instance moves are the primary unit-gate repair vehicle

Every unit_gate trial in iteration 1 was accepted as `gated_in` with `conn_preserved=true` and zero net new violations (`n_new_in_crop=0`, `n_new_out_of_crop=0`). All used positive x-direction displacements applied to M1/M2/V1-touching instances. The minimum effective displacement observed is 36 dbu: single-instance moves of exactly 36 dbu cleared violations without introducing new ones in trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0004.04, and trial:i01.ug.leaf_0007.05. This step size equals 2 x M2.W.1 (18 nm), which is also 1 x M2.S.1 side-to-side minimum, making 36 dbu the natural repair grid pitch for M2 spacing violations on the x-axis.

### Compound move-and-resize operations safely extend M2 wires during instance shifts

When an instance move would disconnect a M2 polygon that bridges the moved cell to its neighbor, pairing the `move_instance` op with `resize_end` (axis:x, end:high) on the bridging polygon preserves connectivity while simultaneously expanding the wire to fill the new gap. Trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row3.01 combined two 36 dbu instance moves with a single `resize_end` (axis:x, delta:36, end:high) on polygon p1040 and was gated_in clean. Trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row5.02 used two 64 dbu instance moves paired with two `resize_end` ops (axis:x, delta:64, end:high) on p1059 and p1057, also gated_in with zero new violations.

When the resize delta equals the move delta (36 dbu move -> 36 dbu resize; 64 dbu move -> 64 dbu resize), the M2 wire high-end tracks the moved instance exactly and no M2 width (M2.W.1) or enclosure (V1.M2.EN.2) shortfall is created. Trial:i01.ug.Block2_union_row5.02 confirms this parity rule at the 64 dbu scale.

### Large asymmetric resizes are valid when M2.W.1 and spacing minimums are maintained

Trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.03 applied a 128 dbu instance move together with a 184 dbu `resize_end` (axis:x, end:high) on p1065 and a 176 dbu `resize_end` (axis:x, end:low) on p957 -- resize deltas that differ from each other and from the move delta -- and was still gated_in with conn_preserved=true and no new violations. This confirms that asymmetric high/low end resizes are acceptable provided the resulting M2 geometry satisfies M2.W.1 (>= 18 nm width) and M2.S.1/M2.S.2 spacing on all affected edges. The 184 dbu and 176 dbu magnitudes are large relative to the M2 minimums, indicating no concern about underwidth or underspacing arises from large positive resize steps in isolation.
