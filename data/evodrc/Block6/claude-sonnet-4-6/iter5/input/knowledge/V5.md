A decision=applied outcome indicates the repair was unconditionally committed and produced a net reduction in violations, as in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02 (-16) and trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.00 (-14).
