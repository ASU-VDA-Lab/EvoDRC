## Repair Mechanics Observed in Layer V1

### Primary Repair Operation: move_instance in X

All accepted trials use `move_instance` with a non-zero x-component as the dominant or sole operation. Positive x shifts ranging from 4 dbu (trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row7.01) to 136 dbu (trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01) have been used successfully. Negative x shifts also appear: −36 dbu in trial:i05.ug.leaf_0001.00 and −28 dbu in trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01. A shift of 36 dbu is the most frequently repeated delta across the dataset (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row8.03, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0011.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0018.07, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.04).

All instance moves across the full history are along the x-axis only. The sole y-axis moves in the dataset (delta_dbu:[0,24] for instances i0384 and i0528) are in trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01 and affect layers M5/M6—not V1.

### M2 End Extension via resize_end

Seven trials supplement instance moves with a `resize_end` operation on an M2 polygon: trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01 (p2072, +92 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02 (p1903, +124 dbu; p1920 low-end +56 dbu), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.04 (p2016, +132 dbu), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.06 (p1923, +48 dbu), trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00 (p2020, +132 dbu), trial:i05.ug.leaf_0001.00 (p2071, +12 dbu), trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01 (p2086 +168 dbu, p1946 +132 dbu, p2046 +100 dbu).

With the single exception of the low-end resize in trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02, all resize_end calls specify end:"high" on axis:"x", extending the M2 polygon in the positive x direction. No y-axis resize appears anywhere in the recorded history. The resize_end operation always co-occurs with at least one move_instance in the same trial; it never appears as a standalone operation (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01 through trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01).

### Rule-to-Operation Mapping

**V1.M2.EN.2 (M2 enclosure ≥5 nm on two opposite sides):** M2 end extensions via resize_end directly increase the M2 overhang beyond the V1 boundary, satisfying the projection-based 5 nm enclosure check. Trials pairing a +132 dbu or larger M2 extension with a coincident instance move were all accepted with zero new violations in crop during iters 1–3 (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.04, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02). The rule also allows a 5 & 0 nm end-cap variant; the presence of zero-end-cap cases in the design is consistent with the absence of y-axis resize operations in all recorded trials.

**V1.M1.EN.1 (M1 enclosure ≥5 & 2 nm on opposite sides):** M1 is listed in touched_layers for every trial, confirming that V1 repositioning via move_instance co-moves M1 geometry. Co-moving M1 and V1 in the same instance move preserves the enclosure relationship without a dedicated M1 resize. All trials with M1 in touched_layers were accepted (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00 through trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01).

**V1.AUX.1 (V1 inside M1 ∩ M2):** Every accepted trial touches both M1 and M2 simultaneously with V1. No trial repositions V1 without also touching its enclosing M1 and M2 layers; this co-motion is required to maintain V1.AUX.1 (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00 through trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01).

**V1.M2.AUX.2 (V1 width equals M2 width perpendicular to M2 length):** No resize operation perpendicular to M2 length appears in any trial. The move_instance operations preserve the existing width relationship without modification; all 15 accepted trials confirm that V1.M2.AUX.2 violations are not addressed by y-axis resizing in this design (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00 through trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01).

**V1.S.1 / V1.S.2 / V1.S.3 / V1.S.4 (spacing rules):** Lateral separation between V1 instances is adjusted by shifting complete instances along x. Multiple-instance moves within a single trial—up to 4 move_instance ops in trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01 and 4 in trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01—demonstrate that satisfying spacing rules requires coordinated displacement of several instances rather than moving one instance alone. The spacing rules are direction-dependent (same-track vs. parallel-track vs. corner-to-corner), and the exclusive use of x-axis moves in the history is consistent with M2 running along one axis and V1 spacing violations arising in that direction.

**V1.W.1 (minimum width 18 nm along M2 length):** No resize operation that reduces V1 width appears in the history. All resize_end operations target M2 polygons and extend rather than contract. Width violations on V1 itself are not addressed by any resize in the recorded trials (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00 through trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01).

Every accepted trial has conn_preserved: true. No trial with conn_preserved: false appears in the history. Move-then-resize strategies that touch M1, M2, and V1 simultaneously within the same trial preserve inter-layer connectivity, satisfying this hard gate (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00 through trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01).
