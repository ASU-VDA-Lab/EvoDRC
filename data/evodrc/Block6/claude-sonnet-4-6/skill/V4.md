## Repair Patterns

**Spread-and-grow on V4 with co-resize of M4 reduces spacing and enclosure violations.**
In trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01, two V4 shapes inside cell VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58 were first moved symmetrically outward along x (shape 0 by −116 dbu, shape 1 by +116 dbu) and then each resized by +384 dbu in x; simultaneously M4 shape 0 was resized by +152 dbu in x. The combined five-operation sequence reduced total violations by 52 across two windows (leaf_0019: −28, leaf_0020: −24) while preserving connectivity.

**Symmetric lateral spreading of adjacent V4 shapes is effective for V4.S.* violations.**
The opposing moves applied in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01 increase the inter-shape gap in x without introducing net displacement of the via pair, which directly addresses the 33 nm projection-spacing requirements of V4.S.1, V4.S.2, and V4.S.3.

**Resizing V4 shapes in x after spreading maintains M4 and M5 enclosure.**
After the lateral spread in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01, each V4 shape was grown by +384 dbu in x. This growth extends each shape back toward the enclosing metal edges, counteracting any enclosure loss (V4.M4.EN.1: 11 nm on at least two opposite sides; V4.M5.EN.2: 11 nm on two opposite sides) that would otherwise result from moving the via away from its original centroid.

**Co-resizing M4 in x is required when V4 shapes widen.**
Because V4.AUX.1 requires V4 to remain inside both M4 and M5, and because V4.M5.AUX.2 requires V4 width perpendicular to the M5 length to match M5 exactly, growing V4 in x can push V4 outside M4 unless M4 is grown concurrently. In trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01, M4 shape 0 was resized by +152 dbu in x alongside the V4 resize operations, and the repair was accepted with conn_preserved=true.

**Operations on touched layers M4, M5, and V4 must remain jointly consistent.**
trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01 touched layers M4, M5, and V4 in a single five-operation commit. No V4 or M4 shape was moved or resized in isolation; every V4 geometric change was paired with a compensating M4 resize. Applying V4 moves or resizes without the corresponding M4 adjustment risks V4.AUX.1 and V4.M4.EN.1 violations.

**Y-axis instance spreading does not reduce V4 violations and produces a net-negative DRC result regardless of magnitude.**
Two separate trials confirm this across different dbu scales. trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06 applied 24 move_instance operations at ±24/±72 dbu along y with 12 resize_end operations on M4/M5, touching M3, M4, M5, V3, V4; result: +26 new in-crop violations, gated_in by conn_preserved only. trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 applied 20 move_instance operations at ±48/±96 dbu along y, touching M3, M4, M5, V3, V4 (no polygon resize_end); result: +35 new in-crop violations, gated_in by conn_preserved only. Neither trial reduced violations. Larger y-axis magnitudes (±48/±96 vs ±24/±72) produced more new violations (35 vs 26).

**Asymmetric x-axis instance moves without via resize-back produce new violations; adding symmetric via shape spread-and-grow on the primary via layer converts the pattern to a net-positive outcome.**
trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 applied asymmetric x-axis instance moves (+32/−16 dbu, 16 instances, 2 polygons) across M4/M5/M6/V4/V5 with no via resize-back, and produced +26 new in-crop violations. trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.00 applied the same asymmetric x-axis move magnitudes (+32/−16 dbu, 14 instances, 2 polygons) plus mixed x+y instance moves and—critically—a symmetric y-axis spread-and-grow on four V5 via shapes inside cell VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58 (shapes 0 and 1 moved −132 dbu, shapes 2 and 3 moved +132 dbu, all four resized +512 dbu in y), also across M4/M5/M6/V4/V5; result: −14 violations in leaf_0002, conn_preserved=true. **Symmetric y-axis spread-and-grow on V5 via shapes is an effective repair operation when targeting a VIA_VIA5x cell.**

**Gated_in with conn_preserved does not alone imply violation reduction; a via resize-back operation is the distinguishing factor.**
trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 were each accepted into the solution set solely because connectivity was preserved (reason: "conn_preserved"), and each added 26, 35, and 26 new in-crop violations respectively. trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.00 was also accepted with conn_preserved=true but reduced violations by 14. **Broader layer scope (5 layers, two inter-metal via levels) is not inherently negative when a via shape spread-and-grow is included for the primary via layer.**
The three negative-outcome trials (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02) all touched five layers spanning two inter-metal via levels and produced new in-crop violations. Five-layer scope paired with via resize-back can be net-positive; five-layer scope with only instance moves and no via resize-back is net-negative in all measured cases.

## Rule Interaction Summary

V4.S.1/V4.S.2/V4.S.3 share the 33 nm minimum spacing threshold (projection and euclidean). The x-axis spread-and-grow pattern in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01 addressed spacing violations in both affected windows simultaneously, confirming that a single multi-operation commit targeting x-axis geometry with a grow-back resize resolves co-located spacing errors without re-triggering enclosure rules, provided M4 is co-resized and layer scope is confined to M4/M5/V4.

Y-axis instance moves at ±24/±72 dbu (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06) and ±48/±96 dbu (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01) with M3/V3 in scope both produced new in-crop violations. In all four negative-outcome trials (adding trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02), no resize-back was applied to any via shape targeted by the repair.
