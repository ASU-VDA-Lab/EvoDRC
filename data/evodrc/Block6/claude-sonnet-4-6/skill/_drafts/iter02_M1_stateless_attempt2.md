All 12 accepted trials in the layer M1 history use exactly two operation types: `move_instance` and `resize_end`. ## Axis Constraint

Every `move_instance` op across the entire history has `delta_dbu` of the form `[N, 0]` — a nonzero x-component and a zero y-component. Every `resize_end` op specifies `axis: "x"`. No y-axis displacement or y-axis resize has been applied to any M1-touching trial at any iteration. Apply only x-axis displacements and x-axis resize operations when correcting M1 DRC violations; y-axis motion does not appear in any accepted record (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.06, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row8.02).

Accepted `resize_end` operations use deltas of 48 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.06), 56 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02, `end: "low"`), 92 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01), 124 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02, `end: "high"`), and 132 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.04, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00). The dominant `end` value is `"high"` — five of six `resize_end` ops use `end: "high"`, with one `end: "low"` occurring alongside a paired `end: "high"` resize in the same trial (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02). When a single trial applies two `resize_end` ops to different polygons, one uses `end: "high"` with a larger delta and one uses `end: "low"` with a smaller delta, and both are accepted with no new violations (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02, delta 124 high on p1903 and 56 low on p1920).

## Spacing and Width Rules

M1.W.1 sets minimum M1 width at 18 nm. M1.S.1 sets minimum side-to-side spacing at 18 nm when both edges exceed 36 nm. M1.S.2 sets minimum tip-to-side spacing at 25 nm when one edge is at most 36 nm and the other exceeds 36 nm. M1.S.3 sets minimum tip-to-tip spacing at 27 nm when both edges are between 24 nm and 36 nm. M1.S.4 and M1.S.5 set 31 nm tip-to-tip minimums for edges below 24 nm. M1.S.6 sets a 20 nm minimum corner-to-corner spacing. M1.A.1 requires a minimum area of 504 nm². The accepted x-axis resize operations expand M1 polygon endpoints (all accepted `resize_end` operations have positive `delta_dbu` values: 48, 56, 92, 124, 132 dbu), which increases metal coverage and resolves enclosure shortfalls without introducing spacing violations, as confirmed by `n_new_out_of_crop: 0` in every accepted trial (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.06, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00).

## M1.R.0 Redundant Island Avoidance

M1.R.0 flags M1 polygons that enclose exactly one small V0 via and sit near a large empty M1 region (empty region at least 500 nm wide, area above 2.5 µm², expanded by 400 nm). No accepted trial introduced a redundant island violation (`n_new_in_crop: 0` in all cases). The combination of move and resize operations applied in accepted trials does not produce isolated single-via M1 fragments near large empty regions, as connectivity is preserved across all trials (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00 through trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.04, all `conn_preserved: true`).
