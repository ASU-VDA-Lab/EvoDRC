## Via Enclosure and M4 Horizontal Resizing (cu_pool Channel)

In the cu_pool channel, V4.M4.EN.1 violations on via cell `VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58` were repaired by expanding M4 in the x-direction by +152 dbu while simultaneously repositioning and resizing V4 shapes within the same cell (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01). This single compound operation reduced the net violation count by 52 across two windows and was accepted as applied with connectivity preserved. V4.M4.EN.1 requires that M4 enclose V4 by at least 11 nm on at least two opposite sides; expanding the M4 shape horizontally is the direct correction when V4 sits too close to the M4 edge in the x-dimension. The co-repair of V4 shapes (two moves and two resizes in x) was necessary to keep V4 centered and within M4 after the M4 boundary shifted.

When a cu_pool via cell triggers V4.M4.EN.1, expand the M4 enclosure shape in the axis that is deficient rather than shrinking V4, because V4 must also satisfy its own width rules. A resize of M4 in x by at least the enclosure shortfall (minimum 11 nm per side) restores compliance without introducing M4.W.1, M4.W.5, or M4.S.2 violations provided the resulting horizontal dimension remains ≥ 44 nm (M4.W.5) and the gap to the nearest M4 shape in x remains ≥ 40 nm (M4.S.2). trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01 used a +152 dbu x-expansion for M4, confirming that the repair delta may significantly exceed the minimum 11 nm rule value when the via cell geometry requires it.

## cu_pool Compound Via Cell Repairs: Mixed-Axis Moves and Via Shape Operations

In trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.00, a 30-operation compound repair on via cell `VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58` (touching M4, M5, M6, V4, V5) was accepted with decision "applied" and a net violation delta of −14 (leaf_0002 window: 70→56; leaf_0003 window: unchanged). This repair combined four distinct operation types in a single group (g1): direct polygon x-moves (op:"move", axis:"x") at +32 dbu on p1685 and −16 dbu on p1684; compound instance moves carrying both x and y components simultaneously ([32,−64], [−16,−64], [32,−112], [−16,−112] dbu); direct polygon y-moves (op:"move", axis:"y") at −64 dbu on p2109 and −112 dbu on p2108; and via-internal shape operations (move_via_shape, resize_via_shape) on V5 shapes within the via cell definition.

The via shape operations moved V5 shape_index 0 and 1 by −132 dbu in y (with +512 dbu resize) and V5 shape_index 2 and 3 by +132 dbu in y (with +512 dbu resize), applying symmetric inward repositioning with large-magnitude size expansion. These are distinct from polygon-level move and resize_end operations and operate on indexed shapes within a named cell definition (cell_name: "VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58", layer_name: "V5"). The resize_via_shape delta applies to the shape's extent in the named axis, not to a single edge endpoint. In trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.00, the +512 dbu resize on all four V5 shapes is a large-magnitude correction consistent with resolving V5 enclosure by M5 or M6 rather than an M4-specific rule; M4 is in the touched layer set because the surrounding instance and polygon moves in the same group affect M4-hosting geometry at the via cell boundaries.

The y-move values −64 and −112 dbu applied to M5/M6-region polygons in this cu_pool trial are not integer multiples of 24 dbu (64/24 ≈ 2.67; 112/24 ≈ 4.67). These values are therefore specific to M5/M6 layer grid requirements and must not be inferred as valid M4 y-step sizes. M4 horizontal edge alignment (M4.AUX.1: 24 nm grid) continues to restrict M4-specific y-displacements to multiples of 24 dbu.

## Unit-Gate Instance Moves: Y-Axis Step Sizes and M4 Grid Constraints

The operative vertical step sizes observed in unit_gate repairs touching M4 are {±24, ±48, ±72, ±96} dbu. In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06, 24 instances were moved by deltas from the set {+72, +24, −24, −72} dbu alongside 12 M4 polygon end resizes at magnitudes 24 and 72 dbu. In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, 20 instances were moved by deltas exclusively from {+48, −48, +96, −96} dbu with no polygon resize_end ops. All observed values are integer multiples of 24 dbu, confirming that all vertical adjustments to M4-hosting geometry in the unit_gate channel must be integer multiples of 24 dbu to remain on-grid per M4.AUX.1.

Step size selection depends on the individual instance's current offset from the nearest legal routing track position, not a uniform pitch. trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06 and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 both show that different instances within the same batch receive different step magnitudes. Do not apply a single uniform step to all instances in a batch without checking each instance's pre-move y-coordinate modulo 24 dbu; the step must land the instance on a legal track position.

## M4 Vertical Width Forbidden Values Under M4.W.3 and M4.W.4

M4.W.3 forbids vertical widths that are even-integer multiples of the 24 nm minimum width (48, 96, 144, 192, 240, 288, 336, 384, 432, 480 nm). M4.W.4 additionally forbids widths of 72, 168, 264, 360, and 456 nm. When a resize_end operation adjusts a M4 polygon's y-extent, the resulting height must be verified against both forbidden lists. The resize_end deltas of +24 and +72 dbu applied to individual polygon ends in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06 altered the vertical extent of M4 wires; a +24 dbu resize on a polygon whose pre-move height was, for example, 24 nm would produce a 48 nm result, which M4.W.3 forbids. The trial was gated_in (26 new violations introduced within crop, none outside) precisely because such post-resize heights may land on forbidden values. Always compute the post-operation bbox height of every M4 polygon touched by a resize_end and reject steps that produce values in {48, 72, 96, 144, 168, 192, 240, 264, 288, 336, 360, 384, 432, 456, 480} nm.

## X-Axis Instance and Polygon Moves: Channel Distinction Matters

Four unit_gate trials and one cu_pool trial in this history have applied x-axis displacements to M4-touching geometry. In the unit_gate channel, all observed x-moves introduced new violations: trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.03 moved a single instance by −16 dbu and introduced 2 new in-crop violations; trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 applied polygon moves at +32 dbu and −16 dbu to 15 instances and 2 polygons and introduced 26 new violations; trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 moved a single instance (i0193) by −16 dbu in x (touching M3, M4, V3) and introduced 1 new in-crop violation. All three unit_gate x-move trials were accepted as gated_in with conn_preserved.

In the cu_pool channel, trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.00 applied identical x-move deltas (+32 and −16 dbu) to polygons and instances and achieved a net violation reduction of −14 (applied, not gated_in). The cu_pool decision path evaluates net violation delta across window pairs and accepts operations that reduce violations overall; unit_gate evaluates connectivity and accepts even when violations increase in-crop. The same x-delta values that always introduce violations in unit_gate can produce net reductions in cu_pool when the full repair group offsets them with complementary y and via-shape corrections.

Neither −16 dbu nor +32 dbu is a multiple of 40 dbu (M4.S.2 horizontal spacing) or of 44 dbu (M4.W.5 minimum horizontal width). In unit_gate, all observed x-displacements have introduced new M4 compliance violations in every case; the smallest count was 1 (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00, single instance, M3/M4/V3 stack). When V3 is in the touched stack, V3.M4.EN.2 enclosure margins and V3.M4.AUX.2 coincidence-edge alignment are primary risks. When V3 is absent (M4/M5/M6/V4/V5 stack), V4.M4.EN.1 and M4.S.2 are primary x-axis checks.

## M4 Connectivity Preservation as Gating Criterion

All unit_gate trials in this history (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.03, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00) were accepted under the `gated_in` decision path with `conn_preserved: true` as the stated reason, even though they introduced 2, 26, 35, 26, and 1 new violations respectively within the crop window. No unit_gate trial in this layer's history was rejected for introducing violations in-crop when connectivity was preserved. This confirms that the unit_gate channel treats connectivity preservation as a sufficient condition for acceptance independent of the in-crop violation delta, and that responsibility for clearing newly introduced violations falls on subsequent iterations.

The cu_pool channel uses a different criterion: trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01 and trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.00 were both accepted as "applied" (not gated_in) with positive net violation reductions (−52 and −14 respectively). In both cu_pool cases conn_preserved was also true, but the acceptance label and the positive net delta signal that cu_pool requires measurable violation reduction rather than merely connectivity preservation.

## M4.AUX.2 Track Alignment and Batch Y-Move Coordination

M4.AUX.2 requires minimum-width M4 tracks to have their centerlines at positions satisfying (CL − 48 dbu) mod 192 dbu = 0, i.e., at y = 48, 240, 432, 624, … dbu. The polygon resize_end operations in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06 modified the y-extents of 12 M4 polygons, with resize magnitudes of 24 and 72 dbu applied at both the high and low ends of distinct polygons. Moves applied to the low end versus the high end shift the centerline by half the delta in opposite directions. A +72 dbu resize at the high end and a −72 dbu resize at the low end of the same polygon leave the centerline unchanged; resizes applied to only one end shift the centerline. The step magnitudes 48 and 96 dbu observed in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 are also multiples of 24 dbu; a one-sided 48 dbu step shifts the centerline by 24 dbu and a one-sided 96 dbu step shifts it by 48 dbu, both integer dbu values. To maintain M4.AUX.2 compliance for minimum-width wires, one-sided resize_end deltas must equal an even multiple of 24 dbu so that the resulting centerline displacement (delta / 2) is an integer dbu value, and the new centerline must land on the 192 dbu track grid with 48 dbu offset.

## M4.AUX.3 Non-Bend Constraint

M4.AUX.3 forbids any M4 polygon that contains a corner with interior angle between 0 and 90 degrees (exclusive), i.e., M4 may not bend or have L-shaped geometry. All M4 polygon operations observed in this history (resize_end in y on trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06; x-resize on trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01; direct polygon x-moves on trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 and trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.00; single instance x-move on trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00) operate on individual axis-aligned ends or translate polygons rigidly and cannot introduce bends when applied to rectilinear polygons. Instance moves are rigid translations that preserve polygon shape. Do not merge or subtract M4 polygons during repair in a way that could produce non-Manhattan geometry; M4.AUX.3 violations from bends cannot be fixed by further resizing and require polygon splitting or deletion.

## M4.S.1 and Batch Vertical Moves

M4.S.1 requires a minimum vertical (y-axis projection) spacing of 24 nm between M4 shapes. When multiple instances are moved in y simultaneously, the relative y-displacement between M4 segments on adjacent instances changes. An instance moved by +48 dbu and an adjacent instance not moved closes any pre-existing vertical gap by 48 dbu; if the pre-move gap was exactly 24 dbu, the result is a −24 dbu overlap. The 26 new violations in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06 (steps ±24, ±72 dbu) and the 35 new violations in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 (steps ±48, ±96 dbu, 20 instances) are consistent with this gap-closure mechanism, with larger step magnitudes producing proportionally more spacing violations. When applying asymmetric batch y-moves (some instances up, some down), verify that no pair of M4 segments on adjacent moving or non-moving instances closes to below 24 dbu vertical separation. Larger step sizes (96 dbu) in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 correlate with the highest observed in-crop violation count (35) across all unit_gate trials in this history.
