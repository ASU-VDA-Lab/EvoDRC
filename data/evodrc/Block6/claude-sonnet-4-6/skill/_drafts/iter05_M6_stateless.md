Vertical endpoint adjustments on M6 polygons must be made in multiples of 16 dbu to preserve alignment with M6.AUX.1 horizontal-edge grid requirements (32 nm pitch). In trial:i01.ug.leaf_0020.09 both p2107 and p2106 received asymmetric y-axis resize_end operations (96, 32, 16, and 112 dbu respectively), all multiples of 16 dbu, while delivering 55 new in-crop violations and zero out-of-crop violations with connectivity preserved. Do not apply y-axis resize_end deltas that are not multiples of 16 dbu on minimum-width M6 tracks, as M6.AUX.1 mandates a 32 nm horizontal-edge grid and M6.AUX.2 mandates 256 dbu centerline pitch with 64 dbu offset from origin (base 128 dbu) for narrow (1x) tracks.

When repositioning V5 via shapes under M6 to satisfy V5.M6.AUX.2 (V5 width must exactly match M6 width perpendicular to M6 length) and V5.M6.EN.2 (minimum 11 nm enclosure on two opposite sides), apply both a lateral move and a symmetric resize on the same axis. In trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02 all four V5 shapes were moved ±116 dbu on x and each resized by +320 dbu on x, reducing DRC violations by 8 in each of two units. Never resize V5 on x without the paired ±move to re-center the shape under the M6 stripe; the AUX.2 check requires the via edges to be coincident with the M6 edges, so a resize without re-centering shifts one side into violation while correcting the other.

Moving an M6 polygon horizontally requires co-moving every associated cell instance by the identical delta. In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 p1683 moved +32 dbu on x with 8 instances co-moved +32 dbu, and p1682 moved -16 dbu on x with 8 instances co-moved -16 dbu; connectivity was preserved and 26 in-crop violations were cleared. In trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.00 the same pairing was repeated (p1685 +32 with 7 instances, p1684 -16 with 6 instances), reducing total violations by 14. Omitting instance co-movement breaks connectivity regardless of DRC improvement.

When M6 tracks must shift vertically, the V5 via array spanning those tracks must be resized on y by an amount that covers the full new vertical extent. In trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.00 p2109 moved -64 dbu and p2108 moved -112 dbu on y while all four V5 shapes were each resized +512 dbu on y and moved ±132 dbu on y to re-balance the array. The combined operation satisfied V5.M6.EN.2 (11 nm enclosure) and V5.M6.AUX.2 after the track displacement. Apply the via y-resize in the same grouped operation as the M6 y-move; splitting them into separate trials risks intermediate states that violate enclosure rules.

Extending the high-x end of an M6 polygon provides enclosure margin for V5 and V6 vias and is the correct first response when V5.M6.EN.2 or V6.M6.EN.1 violations appear at the terminal end of an M6 stripe. In trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01 three M6 polygons received high-x end extensions (p2086 +168 dbu, p1946 +132 dbu, p2046 +100 dbu), clearing 22 in-crop violations across M1, M2, M5, M6, V1, and V5 with connectivity preserved. The extension deltas are not uniform (100, 132, 168 dbu), indicating that the required margin depends on the specific via placement and must be calculated per polygon rather than set to a single fixed value. Always verify that the extended end does not create an M6.S.2 (40 nm horizontal spacing) violation with an adjacent M6 stripe after applying the extension.

M6.W.3 and M6.W.4 together impose a strict constraint on vertical width: widths that are even integer multiples of 32 nm (64, 128, 192, 256, 320, 384, 448, 512, 576, 640 nm per M6.W.3) and widths that span an even number of routing tracks (96, 224, 352, 480, 608 nm per M6.W.4) are both forbidden. When a y-axis resize_end operation is applied, verify that the resulting vertical dimension does not land on any of these forbidden values. The asymmetric deltas seen in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0020.09 (low 96 + high 32 on p2107; low 16 + high 112 on p2106) result in net height changes of -64 and +96 dbu respectively; the net change arithmetic must account for both ends before checking M6.W.3 and M6.W.4 compliance.

M6.AUX.3 prohibits any corner whose interior angle falls between 0 and 90 degrees, meaning M6 must be purely rectilinear with no bends. All resize_end and move operations in the history act on rectangular polygons and produce rectangular results. Never apply a polygon operation that would produce a non-axis-aligned edge or an L-shaped polygon on M6; such a shape would simultaneously violate M6.AUX.3 and the GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL block rule.

M6.S.1 mandates 32 nm minimum vertical spacing, and M6.S.5 mandates that two M6 polygons on adjacent tracks sharing a parallel run length must have at least 44 nm of parallel overlap. When moving M6 polygons on x to resolve M6.S.2 (40 nm horizontal spacing) or track-alignment violations, check that the resulting x-positions do not reduce parallel run length below 44 nm for any pair that previously shared a run length, which would trigger M6.S.5.
