## Repair Operation Types

Every accepted trial in this history applies only two operation types to V1-touching regions: `move_instance` and `resize_end`. Both operation types produce strictly orthogonal geometry. All move deltas take the form `[N, 0]` — X-axis displacement only, Y component zero — and every `resize_end` specifies `axis:x` (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.06, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00). No Y-axis move or resize and no operation that would introduce a non-orthogonal edge appears in any accepted trial across all three iterations.

## Coordinated Multi-Layer Edits

In every accepted trial the `touched_layers` field records `["M1","M2","V1"]` — never V1 alone (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row8.03, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0011.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0018.07, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row7.01, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row8.02, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.04, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00). V1.AUX.1 requires every V1 polygon to reside inside M1 ∩ M2, and V1.M2.AUX.2 requires V1 width to equal M2 width perpendicular to the M2 track; both rules tie V1 position tightly to the enclosing M1 and M2 geometry. Move V1 together with its enclosing M1 and M2 shapes using a matching delta (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00 moves three instances by [72,0] together; trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row8.03 and trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row7.01 each move a single instance while M1/M2 are also marked touched). Do not edit V1 geometry in isolation from M1 and M2, as no accepted trial demonstrates that pattern (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0011.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0018.07, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.00 — single-op moves that nonetheless touch all three layers).

## Delta Magnitude: Use the Smallest Sufficient Value

Move deltas range from 4 dbu (trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row7.01) to 112 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02). Resize deltas range from 48 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.06) to 132 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.04, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00). The 4 dbu correction in trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row7.01 followed a prior 104 dbu move on the same unit (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02), demonstrating that a residual violation after a large move requires only a small incremental correction rather than another large shift. All accepted trials record `n_new_in_crop:0` and `n_new_out_of_crop:0`, confirming that the chosen delta in each case cleared the target violation without generating secondary violations. Apply the smallest delta sufficient to meet the minimum spacing or enclosure requirement; over-correction risks creating new V1.S.1/V1.S.2/V1.S.3/V1.S.4 violations against neighboring instances (trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row7.01 illustrates fine-tuned correction after an over-large first pass).

## Resize Direction: Always Extend at the High End

Every `resize_end` in the history uses `end:high` — the upper-X boundary of the polygon — to extend the M2 or M1 shape (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01 extends p2072 by 92 dbu; trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02 extends p1903 by 124 dbu and p1920 low end by 56 dbu from `end:low` — note p1920 uses `end:low`, the only exception; trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.04 extends p2016 by 132 dbu; trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.06 extends p1923 by 48 dbu; trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00 extends p2020 by 132 dbu). The dominant pattern is `end:high` extension; the single `end:low` case in trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02 accompanies a concurrent `end:high` resize on a separate polygon in the same trial, indicating that bidirectional extension may be used when two M2 shapes bound the violation region from opposite sides. Never resize on `axis:y`; no accepted trial does so (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01 through trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00).

## Connectivity Preservation

All thirteen accepted trials record `conn_preserved:true` and `decision:gated_in`. Multi-instance move operations achieve this by applying the same delta to every instance in the electrically connected group: trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00 moves i0471, i0324, and i0481 by identical [72,0]; trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01 moves four instances by [36,0] while simultaneously extending one M2 polygon end. Move all instances in a net together when relocating a V1 via instance move, so that intra-group relative positions are unchanged and no new spacing violation opens within the group (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.06, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row8.02).

## Rule-Specific Observations

**V1.W.1 (minimum width 18 nm along M2 length):** Width violations along the M2 track are addressed by `resize_end` operations that increase the X extent of the enclosing M2 polygon. Observed resize deltas of 48–132 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.06 through trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.04) bracket the correction magnitudes that produce zero new violations.

**V1.S.1 / V1.S.2 / V1.S.3 / V1.S.4 (spacing):** Spacing shortfalls between V1 instances are resolved by `move_instance` operations shifting one group in the +X direction. The corrected spacing between groups after a 4 dbu increment (trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row7.01) confirms that a small residual shortfall needs only a proportionally small move. Trials covering the same unit across iterations (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02 then trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row7.01) show that iterative fine-tuning converges without over-shooting into the V1.S.x spacing band on the far side.

**V1.M1.EN.1 / V1.M2.EN.2 (enclosure):** When M1 or M2 underencloses V1, `resize_end` at `end:high` extends the enclosing polygon to satisfy the 5 nm / 2 nm (M1) and 5 nm / 5 nm or 5 nm / 0 nm (M2) requirements. The concurrent instance move in the same trial ensures the V1 polygon stays geometrically inside the extended M1/M2 boundary, satisfying V1.AUX.1 (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.02, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00).

**V1.M2.AUX.2 (width matching):** Resize operations on M2 that extend only the X-axis end preserve the Y-axis dimension of M2, keeping M2 width equal to V1 width in the perpendicular direction. No accepted trial resizes M2 in Y (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.01, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.04, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row4.00).
