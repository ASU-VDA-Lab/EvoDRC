**Repair Approach for V3 DRC Violations**

All accepted trials modified V3 implicitly by moving the M3/M4 instances that enclose it rather than editing V3 polygons directly (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.03, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00). **Horizontal (x-axis) moves address on-track V3.S.1 violations**

V3.S.1 requires ≥18 nm between V3 instances on the same M4 track (projection measure). Accepted x-axis instance moves used −16 dbu: in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.03, moving i0358 by −16 dbu resolved 2 new violations; in trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00, moving i0193 by −16 dbu resolved 1 new violation. Both were accepted with conn_preserved: true. Apply x-axis instance moves of −16 dbu to close on-track spacing violations; the move magnitude must produce a resulting projected V3 edge separation of ≥18 nm.

**Vertical (y-axis) moves address inter-track spacing and corner-to-corner rules**

V3.S.1 (parallel tracks, not aligned: ≥27 nm projection), V3.S.2 (corner-to-corner, both with 5 nm M4 end-cap: ≥23 nm Euclidean), V3.S.3 (both without end-cap: ≥30 nm Euclidean), and V3.S.4 (mixed end-cap: ≥27 nm Euclidean) are all resolved by adjusting inter-track separation. Both were accepted. Use balanced push/pull pairs—some instances moved +Δ, others −Δ—to open inter-track spacing symmetrically and avoid pushing one side into a new enclosure violation.

**Paired resize_end operations on M4 polygons maintain V3.M4.EN.2 after y-moves**

V3.M4.EN.2 requires M4 to enclose V3 by ≥11 nm on at least two opposite sides. In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.06, 12 `resize_end` operations on M4 polygons (p1826, p1833, p1831, p1792, p1786, p1806, p1845, p1844, p1857, p1734, p1757, p1749) applied y-axis delta_dbu values of +72, +24, −24, −72, matching the move magnitudes used for the companion instance moves in the same trial. Failure to apply the paired resize would narrow the enclosure on the trailing side of the displaced V3. The x-axis moves in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.03 and trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 required no M4 resize_end operations, confirming that resize_end is only necessary when the displacement direction is along the M4 length axis (y).
