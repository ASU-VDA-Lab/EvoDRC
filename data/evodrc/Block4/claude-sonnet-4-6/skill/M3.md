## M3 Repair Behavior: Observations from Measured Trials

### cu_pool Channel: Via-Shape Resizes on M3 Are Consistently Ineffective

Both cu_pool attempts that directly resized M3 via-shapes in the y-axis were rejected before reaching the design state. A y-axis `resize_via_shape` of −40 dbu on M3 in cell `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` produced a net delta of zero (no violation reduction) and was rejected (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). A y-axis `resize_via_shape` of −64 dbu on M3 in `VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52`, applied together with −24 dbu shrinks on two V3 shapes, produced a net delta_total of +34 (34 additional in-crop violations) and was also rejected (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.01). Do not apply cu_pool y-axis `resize_via_shape` directly to M3 shapes in either of these via cells; both outcomes confirm zero or negative improvement.

The assemble_drops record in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0026.12 confirms these two cu_pool ops were evaluated and discarded before the unit_gate pass proceeded, establishing that the cu_pool gate saw them first and found no benefit.

### cu_pool Channel: x-Axis Move+Resize of V2 Is the Effective Pattern for M2/M3/V2 Contexts

The only cu_pool trial that reached `applied` status in this history targeted the V2 layer exclusively, touching M2, M3, and V2 without directly resizing M3 polygons. The operation moved V2 shape 0 by −144 dbu in x, expanded it by +288 dbu, moved shape 2 by +144 dbu in x, expanded it by +288 dbu, and expanded shape 1 by +288 dbu — an asymmetric bilateral x-axis enlargement of the V2 metal in `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36`. This reduced in-crop violations by 51 (trial:i05.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). When M3/V2 interactions produce violations, expand V2 in x via move+resize rather than shrinking M3 in y.

### unit_gate Channel: y-Axis Bulk Polygon Shrinks on M3 Pass When Connectivity Is Preserved

Five M3 polygons (p1406–p1410) were each shrunk by −64 dbu in y in a single unit_gate trial and accepted with `gated_in`. The two new in-crop violations introduced were on M1 (M1.A.1 and V1.M1.EN.1), not on M3, and connectivity was preserved (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0026.12). Bulk y-axis shrinks of M3 polygons at the −64 dbu scale do not in themselves generate new M3 DRC violations; any new violations appear on underlying layers.

### unit_gate Channel: x-Axis Resize-End and Instance Moves on M3 Are Accepted

A `resize_end` operation on M3 polygon p1410 of −8 dbu in x, combined with instance moves, was accepted with `gated_in` and connectivity preserved (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02). An x-axis `move` of M3 polygon p1341 by +32 dbu, also with accompanying instance moves, was similarly accepted (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01). Both trials touched M3 alongside other metal layers; in both cases no new M3-specific violations were reported. Small x-axis endpoint trims and positive x translations of M3 shapes are viable unit_gate strategies.

### Enclosure Rules V2.M3.EN.2 and V3.M3.EN.1: Directional Sensitivity

The effective cu_pool fix for `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` (M2/M3/V2 context) resolved violations by expanding V2 in x rather than adjusting M3, consistent with rule V2.M3.EN.2 requiring 5 nm enclosure of V2 by M3 on two opposite sides. The two previously attempted y-axis M3 shrinks failed (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.01), confirming that reducing M3 coverage in y does not satisfy V2.M3.EN.2 or V3.M3.EN.1 enclosure requirements. Enclosure compliance for V2 within M3 is achieved by growing the via metal (V2) laterally rather than by trimming the enclosing M3 shape.
