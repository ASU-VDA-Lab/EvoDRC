## Repair Operation Patterns

**Instance moves in the x-direction are the dominant M2 repair primitive.** Every unit_gate trial that touched M2 applied at least one `move_instance` with a nonzero x-delta and zero y-delta, and every such trial was accepted with `decision: gated_in`. The modal move step is 36 dbu; moves of 72 (2×36) and 108 (3×36) dbu also appear, consistent with the layout grid enforcing spacing multiples (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.08, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0020.09, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0021.10, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row6.05, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row3.03, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.00). Do not apply non-integer multiples of 36 dbu for M2 x-moves; the history contains no examples of off-grid moves succeeding.

**M2 polygon end-extensions on the x-axis (resize_end, axis=x) are used successfully in combination with instance moves.** In all trials where resize_end appears, the axis is `x` and the extension delta is positive for the `high` end or positive for the `low` end. Deltas range from 56 to 172 dbu across accepted trials. All such combined resize+move sequences were gated_in with no new violations (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row10.01, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row2.02, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row5.04, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06). No y-axis resize of M2 polygon ends appears in any accepted repair.

**Adding new M2 polygons as bridge segments resolves connectivity-driven spacing conflicts.** In trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00, two M2 polygons were inserted at y-row 3024–3096 (height = 72 dbu). The first had width 192 dbu (9252–9444), the second 92 dbu (12132–12224). Both satisfy M2.W.1 (minimum width 18 nm) and M2.A.1 (minimum area 504 nm²) by wide margins. The enclosing instance was shifted +112 dbu and +36 dbu respectively before insertion, confirming that the polygon placement must be coordinated with instance positioning to avoid introducing M2.S.1/M2.S.2 violations between the new segment and neighboring M2 edges.

**Grouped resize+move operations (group_D) allow paired adjustment without net spacing shift.** In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02, polygon p1410 (axis=x, high end, -8 dbu) and instance i0238 (delta -8 dbu, x) were moved in tandem as group_D, shrinking the M2 segment end while shifting the attached instance by the same amount. This trial was gated_in despite introducing n_new_in_crop=2 new violations, because conn_preserved=true was sufficient for acceptance. When a small shrink is required to clear a spacing rule, pairing the resize with a matched instance move avoids opening a gap to the via.

**Connectivity preservation is a necessary condition for acceptance in the unit_gate channel.** Every trial accepted with `decision: gated_in` carries `conn_preserved: true`. No trial with `conn_preserved: false` appears in the history, so the gate enforces this invariant strictly. Repairs that change M2 extent must maintain all V1 vias within their enclosing M2 geometry (rules V1.M2.EN.2 and V1.M2.AUX.2); every accepted unit_gate repair also produced `n_new_out_of_crop: 0`.

## Via-to-M2 Enclosure: V2 and the cu_pool Channel

**Shrinking the M3 shape of a VIA_VIA23 cell in y does not reduce M2-layer violations.** Trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 applied a y-axis resize of -40 dbu to the M3 via shape; the result was delta_total=0 (no improvement) and the trial was rejected with `decision: rejected_net_positive`. M3 geometry changes do not propagate to fix M2.S.* or V2.M2.EN.1 failures.

**Widening and repositioning V2 shapes in x resolves enclosure violations and reduces M2-touching DRC count substantially.** Trial:i05.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 applied five operations to V2 shapes within the same VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 cell: three shapes were resized +288 dbu in x, two shapes were moved (one -144 dbu, one +144 dbu). The result was delta_total=-51 (51 fewer violations), applied. The windows that improved were `unit:leaf_0001` (-36, from 74 to 38) and `unit:leaf_0002` (-15, from 29 to 14). For V2.M2.EN.1 (minimum enclosure of V2 by M2 on two opposite sides is 5 nm), widening V2 in x and centering it within M2 is the effective repair when the via-to-M2 enclosure is insufficient along the horizontal axis. Do not attempt y-shrinks on M3 as a substitute (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 demonstrates this fails).

## Rule-Specific Observations

**M2.W.1 (minimum width 18 nm):** Both M2 polygons added in trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00 have widths (92 dbu and 192 dbu) far above the 18 nm floor. End-extension resizes in accepted trials increase polygon length, which does not affect width. No M2.W.1 violation was introduced by any accepted repair.

**M2.S.1 (side-to-side spacing 18 nm, both edges > 36 nm):** Instance moves of 36 dbu or larger open side-to-side spacing. The consistent use of +36 dbu moves across multiple accepted trials (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row6.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.08, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0020.09, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0021.10) indicates that 36 dbu is the minimum grid step that reliably clears M2.S.1 when polygons are initially at minimum spacing.

**M2.S.2 and M2.S.3/M2.S.4/M2.S.5 (tip spacing rules):** Tip-to-side (25 nm minimum) and tip-to-tip (27–31 nm minimum depending on tip widths) violations arise at M2 segment ends. End-extension resizes that lengthen M2 segments convert short tip edges into longer side edges (edges crossing the 36 nm threshold move from the tip classification into the side classification), directly reducing M2.S.2, M2.S.3, M2.S.4, and M2.S.5 violation counts. Accepted trials extend high-x ends by 56–172 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row2.02, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06), which grows the parallel run length and reclassifies edges.

**M2.S.7 (tip-to-tip 18 nm co-located with side spacing ≤ 32 nm forbidden; parallel run length must be ≥ 35 nm when side spacing ≤ 32 nm):** The large end-extension in trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06 (+172 dbu for p1395, +164 dbu for p1595) is consistent with the requirement to achieve a minimum parallel run length of 35 nm when two M2 tracks are closely spaced. Always extend M2 segment ends sufficiently to satisfy the 35 nm run-length floor when side spacing is near or below 32 nm.

**M2.A.1 (minimum area 504 nm²):** New polygon insertions in trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00 (areas: 192×72 = 13824 dbu² and 92×72 = 6624 dbu²) both exceed 504 nm² by large margins. End-extension resizes only increase polygon area. No area violation was introduced by any accepted repair.

**M2.S.6 and M2.S.8 (corner-to-corner 20 nm; diagonal gap spacing 80 nm):** No repair directly targeted M2.S.6 or M2.S.8 in isolation; however, instance moves that open side-to-side spacing also increase corner-to-corner distances, so M2.S.6 is incidentally addressed by the same 36 dbu move steps used for M2.S.1.

## Multi-Layer Coordination

**Repairs touching M2 frequently co-modify M1 and V1.** All unit_gate trials include M1 and V1 in `touched_layers`. Instance moves propagate geometrically through the cell hierarchy, adjusting M1 and V1 simultaneously with M2. This is expected and does not introduce M2-specific violations as long as conn_preserved=true is maintained.

**When M4 appears in touched_layers alongside M2, the repair involves larger instance displacements or multi-polygon end extensions.** Trials that touch M4 (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row10.01, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06) also have higher op counts (7–8 ops) and include the largest individual resize deltas (up to +172 dbu). These are structurally more complex repairs but remain within the unit_gate channel and are accepted with the same gated_in decision.

**For via cell repairs (cu_pool channel), operate on V2 shapes rather than M3 shapes to affect M2-layer enclosure rules.** The applied trial (trial:i05.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00) operated exclusively on V2 geometry; the rejected trial (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00) operated on M3 geometry. The M2-touching improvement of -51 violations came entirely from the V2 x-axis repositioning and widening, not from any M3 change.
