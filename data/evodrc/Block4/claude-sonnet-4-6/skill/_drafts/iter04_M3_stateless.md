## Channel Routing and Decision Patterns

The repair engine routes M3 candidates through two channels: `cu_pool` and `unit_gate`. All three cu_pool trials touching M3 in this history were rejected (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.01); all three unit_gate trials touching M3 were accepted (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0026.12, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01). The cu_pool rejection criterion is `rejected_net_positive`: the operation is dropped when the aggregate DRC violation count across tracked windows increases or, in the zero-delta case, fails to decrease (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 had delta_total=0 and was still dropped; trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.01 had delta_total=+34 and was dropped). The unit_gate criterion is `conn_preserved` with no new out-of-crop violations; all three accepted trials record `n_new_out_of_crop: 0` and `reason: conn_preserved` (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0026.12, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01).

## assemble_drops: cu_pool Rejections Excluded Before unit_gate Assembly

When a cu_pool candidate is rejected, the harness records its constituent ops in the `assemble_drops` list of the subsequent unit_gate trial for the same locus. Trial:i01.ug.leaf_0026.12 carries four assemble_drops entries corresponding exactly to the two cu_pool trials rejected immediately before it (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 and trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.01). The unit_gate trial then committed only the five remaining M3 polygon resizes (p1406–p1410, y-axis, -64 dbu each) without those dropped via-cell ops. Do not re-propose ops that appear in a trial's assemble_drops list for any subsequent attempt in the same locus; they have already been screened out as net-positive by the cu_pool filter (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0026.12).

## V2.M3.EN.2 and V2.M3.AUX.2 Enclosure Constraints in Via Cell Resizes

The cu_pool trial for VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00) touched layers M2, M3, and V2 simultaneously. The operation shrank M3 shape_index 0 by -40 dbu on the y-axis inside the via cell definition. The delta_total was 0, meaning no net DRC improvement resulted, and the trial was rejected. V2.M3.EN.2 requires M3 to enclose V2 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides; V2.M3.AUX.2 requires V2 to match M3 width along the perpendicular direction. Shrinking M3 within a via cell in y without a commensurate V2 adjustment risks violating these enclosure rules, and the zero-delta outcome confirms this shrink did not resolve any pre-existing violations (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). Do not shrink an M3 shape inside a via cell in a direction that reduces enclosure margin for the enclosed via without verifying that the resulting geometry satisfies both V2.M3.EN.2 and V2.M3.AUX.2 (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00).

## V3.M3.EN.1 Enclosure Constraints in VIA34 Cell Resizes

The cu_pool trial for VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52 (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.01) touched M3, M4, and V3. It shrank M3 shape_index 0 by -64 dbu on y and simultaneously shrank V3 shape_index 0 and shape_index 1 each by -24 dbu on y. The result was a net increase of 34 DRC violations. V3.M3.EN.1 requires M3 to enclose V3 by at least 5 nm on at least two opposite sides. Shrinking M3 in y by -64 dbu while shrinking V3 shapes by only -24 dbu on the same axis reduces the M3-to-V3 enclosure margin on that axis and is insufficient to resolve existing violations when the combined geometry still fails V3.M3.EN.1 or related spacing rules (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.01). The co-shrink of both M3 and V3 in the same direction within a via34 cell definition does not guarantee enclosure compliance and produced a worse outcome than no change (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.01).
