## Rule-Specific Repair Guidance

**M2.W.1 -- Minimum width 18 nm.** No violation of M2.W.1 was triggered in any trial. The smallest M2 polygon added directly (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00, x-extent 92 dbu, y-extent 72 dbu) remained well above the 18 nm floor. Do not add M2 polygons with any edge shorter than 18 nm; the resize_end deltas recorded (minimum 56 dbu) are all above this threshold.

**M2.A.1 -- Minimum area 504 nm2.** No M2.A.1 violation was introduced. The smaller inserted polygon (92 x 72 dbu = 6624 dbu2) exceeds the 504 nm2 minimum by more than 10x. When adding M2 fill or bridge polygons, maintain a y-extent of at least 72 dbu and an x-extent of at least 92 dbu to preserve a similar safety margin (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00).

**M2.S.1 -- Side-to-side spacing 18 nm.** All accepted moves and resizes preserved M2.S.1. move_instance operations of 36 dbu are the measured minimum applied lateral offset on M2 that consistently avoids introducing new side-to-side spacing violations (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.08, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0020.09, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0021.10, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row2.02, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row5.04, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row6.05). Negative x-shifts of -36 dbu are also safe in context (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row3.03).

**M2.S.7 -- Tip-to-tip 18 nm co-located with side spacing <= 32 nm is forbidden; parallel run length must be >= 35 nm when side spacing <= 32 nm.** M2.S.7 involves a compound interaction between vertical edge tip-to-tip proximity and horizontal edge side spacing. The resize_end operations extending M2 polygon ends by 56-172 dbu in the x-direction function to increase parallel run length, which directly addresses the run-length component of M2.S.7 (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row5.04, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row10.01). Extending the high end of an M2 polygon by at least 56 dbu produced clean outcomes in all recorded trials; extensions of 164 and 172 dbu were used in the row7 unit, which involved the largest resize_end deltas (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06).

**M2.S.8 -- Diagonal center-to-center spacing between M2 tip-to-tip gaps >= 80 nm.** No M2.S.8 violation was triggered in any trial. The move_instance and resize_end operations applied did not produce diagonal gap proximity violations, consistent with the observation that all accepted trials recorded zero new DRC markers in the crop window (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00 through trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row10.01).

**V1.M2.EN.2 and V1.M2.AUX.2 -- V1 enclosure by M2.** M2 edits in all unit_gate trials co-touched V1 and M1 (touched_layers invariably includes V1). All accepted trials maintained conn_preserved=true and introduced no new violations, confirming that the move_instance and resize_end operations applied to M2 kept V1 enclosure intact. V1.M2.AUX.2 requires V1 to match M2 width in the perpendicular direction; all accepted resize_end operations targeted only the x-axis high/low ends, not the orthogonal edges that constrain AUX.2 compliance, so AUX.2 was not disturbed (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row2.02, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row5.04, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row10.01).
