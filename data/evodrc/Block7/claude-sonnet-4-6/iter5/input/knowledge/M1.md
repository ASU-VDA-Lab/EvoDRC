## Operation Vocabulary Observed in Iterations 1 and 3

Five operation types appear across the gated-in trials: `move_instance`, `resize_end`, `resize` (symmetric), `move` (polygon), and `add_polygon`. Every trial across both iterations was accepted with `conn_preserved: true`.

**move_instance** is the primary operation. It appears in every trial and rigidly displaces one or more cell instances in x, y, or both x and y simultaneously, carrying all layers in those cells (M1, V1, and any enclosed vias) as a unit. Single-axis x moves include trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.22 ([108,0]) and trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row20.02 ([36,0] on i0600). ## Layer Coupling

Every trial in iterations 1 and 3 touches M1, M2, and V1 simultaneously. Four trials additionally touch M3 and V2: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04, and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07. Because `move_instance` carries all layers in the cell rigidly, any instance displacement adjusting M1 co-moves the associated V1 and M2 shapes. Polygon-level edits (`resize_end`, `resize`, `move`) act only on the named polygon and do not automatically co-move vias, so the relative position between a resized M1 terminal and any V0 or V1 it must enclose changes when only M1 is extended. All gated-in trials in iterations 1 and 3 confirm this multi-layer coupling is compatible with connectivity preservation.

## Non-Orthogonal Geometry (GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL)
