## Repair operation palette

The history for this layer contains five distinct operation types across 45 accepted trials: `move_instance`, `resize_end`, `resize`, `move` (polygon translate), and `add_polygon`. Every accepted trial carries `conn_preserved:true`; the gating channel rejects any op sequence that breaks nets, so connectivity preservation is a hard acceptance condition.

`move_instance` along X is the dominant primitive and appears in virtually every trial (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row6.18, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row22.05, trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row13.01).
