## Repair Strategy: Symmetric Y-axis Move + Resize on V5 Shapes

The only validated repair on V5 used paired `move_via_shape` and `resize_via_shape` operations along the Y axis, applied to all four V5 shapes within a single via-cell definition. In trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02, shapes 0 and 1 were moved -132 dbu on Y and resized +512 dbu on Y; shapes 2 and 3 were moved +132 dbu on Y and also resized +512 dbu on Y. The net effect is symmetric outward expansion: each shape extends further toward its respective outer edge while the center-of-mass of each pair stays consistent. This produced a net reduction of 80 DRC violations across four affected windows (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02).

Apply Y-axis resize (+512 dbu or equivalent) together with an outward Y-axis move (magnitude 132 dbu) whenever V5.M5.EN.1 or V5.M6.EN.2 violations are detected for shapes within a via-array cell. The move component is necessary to prevent the resize from shifting the shape's edge asymmetrically inward (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02). Do not apply resize without the paired move on the same axis, or the enclosure gain on one side will be offset by an enclosure loss on the other.

A 2-op partial attempt on `VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58` that resized only shape_index 0 of M6 (+128 dbu, Y) and shape_index 0 of M5 (-96 dbu, X) -- omitting all other shape indices and V5 itself -- increased violations by 234 and was rejected (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.01). This directly contradicts any strategy of single-shape or partial-cell repair on this cell.

## Multi-Shape Atomicity

All four shapes in cell `VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58` were operated on within the same 8-op group (`group: v56-fix`), and connectivity was preserved (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02, `conn_preserved: true`). ## Layer Co-Modification: M5, M6, and V5 Together -- Consistent Axis Required

The repair in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02 touched M5, M6, and V5 simultaneously using the same axis (Y) for all shape operations. V5.AUX.1 requires V5 to remain inside both M5 and M6; V5.M6.AUX.2 requires V5 to be exactly the same width as M6 in the direction perpendicular to M6 length. Any Y-axis resize of V5 shapes that changes their extent in the M6-perpendicular direction must be accompanied by a matching change to M6 (and potentially M5) to avoid introducing V5.AUX.1 or V5.M6.AUX.2 violations. Do not apply mixed-axis resizes across M5 and M6 in the same repair group. M5 and M6 co-modification must use the same axis as the V5 repair axis; cross-axis resizes on enclosing metals without corresponding V5 adjustment break V5.AUX.1 and V5.M6.AUX.2 constraints simultaneously.
