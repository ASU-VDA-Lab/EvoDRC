## Repair Strategy: Symmetric Y-axis Move + Resize on V5 Shapes

The only validated resize-based repair on V5 used paired `move_via_shape` and `resize_via_shape` operations along the Y axis, applied to all four V5 shapes within a single via-cell definition. In trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02, shapes 0 and 1 were moved −132 dbu on Y and resized +512 dbu on Y; shapes 2 and 3 were moved +132 dbu on Y and also resized +512 dbu on Y. The net effect is symmetric outward expansion: each shape extends further toward its respective outer edge while the center-of-mass of each pair stays consistent. This produced a net reduction of 80 DRC violations across four affected windows (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02).

Apply Y-axis resize (+512 dbu or equivalent) together with an outward Y-axis move (magnitude 132 dbu) whenever V5.M5.EN.1 or V5.M6.EN.2 violations are detected for shapes within a via-array cell. The move component is necessary to prevent the resize from shifting the shape's edge asymmetrically inward (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02). Do not apply resize without the paired move on the same axis, or the enclosure gain on one side will be offset by an enclosure loss on the other.

A 2-op partial attempt on `VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58` that resized only shape_index 0 of M6 (+128 dbu, Y) and shape_index 0 of M5 (−96 dbu, X) — omitting all other shape indices and V5 itself — increased violations by 234 and was rejected (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.01). This directly contradicts any strategy of single-shape or partial-cell repair on this cell.

## Instance-Level Move Repairs (move_instance)

The 15-op repair structure — 9 `move_instance` operations on via/metal instances combined with 6 axis-separated polygon moves (3 X-only, 3 Y-only) touching M5, M6, and V5 — has been confirmed across two distinct unit_gate leaf units. In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0013.09 (unit leaf_0013) it was accepted (gated_in) with connectivity preserved but introduced 217 new in-crop violations. In trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04 (unit leaf_0005) the same structural form was accepted (gated_in) with connectivity preserved and zero new in-crop or out-of-crop violations, demonstrating that this repair pattern can achieve a clean outcome when delta magnitudes are appropriately sized to the target geometry.

Instance moves used per-instance [x,y] delta vectors in both accepted trials. In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0013.09, X components were +32, −16, −64 dbu and Y components were +32, −16, +64 dbu across nine instances. In trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04, X components were +32, −16, +32 dbu and Y components were −64, +16, −32 dbu across nine instances. The specific magnitudes differ between leaf units; the 3×3 grouping structure (three distinct X deltas, three distinct Y deltas, each instance receiving one X and one Y component) is the consistent pattern across both trials.

Polygon moves in both trials were axis-separated (X-only or Y-only per polygon). Mixed-axis per-instance deltas (non-zero both X and Y in a single `move_instance`) are permitted and were used in both accepted repairs. This differs from polygon-level moves, which were kept axis-separated in both cases.

`move_instance` is a valid operation type for V5 via-array repair distinct from `move_via_shape`.

## Multi-Shape Atomicity

All four shapes in cell `VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58` were operated on within the same 8-op group (`group: v56-fix`), and connectivity was preserved (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02, `conn_preserved: true`). Repair all V5 shapes in a via-array cell together in one atomic operation group. Repairing a subset of shapes in a via-array risks introducing V5.S.1, V5.S.2, or V5.S.3 spacing violations between the moved shapes and the unmodified shapes in the same cell. A 2-op attempt touching only shape_index 0 of M6 and M5 in the same cell generated +234 net violations and was rejected (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.01), confirming that partial-shape repair is strictly harmful for this cell geometry.

## Acceptance Gate: Connectivity Preservation vs. Violation Count

A repair that introduced 217 net new in-crop DRC violations and zero new out-of-crop violations was nonetheless accepted (gated_in) because conn_preserved was true (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0013.09). A repair that introduced zero new in-crop violations and zero new out-of-crop violations was also accepted with conn_preserved true (trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04). Connectivity preservation is a primary gating criterion that can override a positive net violation delta. Do not treat violation-count reduction alone as the acceptance criterion; a repair that holds connectivity while increasing in-crop violations can be gated in. The inverse — a violation-reducing repair that breaks connectivity — has not been observed accepted.

## Layer Co-Modification: M5, M6, and V5 Together — Consistent Axis Required

The resize-based repair in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02 touched M5, M6, and V5 simultaneously using the same axis (Y) for all shape resize operations. V5.AUX.1 requires V5 to remain inside both M5 and M6; V5.M6.AUX.2 requires V5 to be exactly the same width as M6 in the direction perpendicular to M6 length. Any Y-axis resize of V5 shapes that changes their extent in the M6-perpendicular direction must be accompanied by a matching change to M6 (and potentially M5) to avoid introducing V5.AUX.1 or V5.M6.AUX.2 violations. Do not resize V5 in isolation from its enclosing metal layers.

Do not apply mixed-axis resizes across M5 and M6 in the same repair group. A repair that resized M6 on Y (+128 dbu) and M5 on X (−96 dbu) for the same cell produced +234 net violations (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.01). M5 and M6 co-modification must use the same axis as the V5 repair axis; cross-axis resizes on enclosing metals without corresponding V5 adjustment break V5.AUX.1 and V5.M6.AUX.2 constraints simultaneously.

The mixed-axis restriction applies to resize operations. Instance-level `move_instance` ops with mixed [x,y] deltas were used and accepted for M5, M6, and V5 simultaneously in move-only repairs (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0013.09, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04); the cross-axis prohibition is specific to resize, not to pure translation.

## Width and Spacing Floor

V5.W.1 sets a 24 nm minimum width along M6 length. V5.S.1, V5.S.2, and V5.S.3 all enforce a 33 nm minimum spacing (projection and Euclidean) between V5 instances. The 512 dbu Y-axis resize applied in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02 increased via extent; verify post-repair that the expanded shapes do not encroach within 33 nm (projection) of adjacent V5 instances on either the same or different nets, and that corner-to-corner Euclidean distance remains ≥ 33 nm (V5.S.3). This check is especially relevant in dense via arrays where multiple via-cell definitions abut. The instance-move repair in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0013.09 introduced 217 new in-crop violations, consistent with spacing violations arising from translated instances; the structurally identical repair in trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04 introduced zero new violations, indicating that delta magnitude selection controls whether spacing floor violations are triggered. Post-move spacing checks against adjacent V5 instances remain mandatory.

## Orthogonality

All V5 edges must remain orthogonal (GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL rule). The move and resize operations in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02 operated exclusively on axis-aligned Y coordinates, preserving orthogonality. Polygon moves in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0013.09 and trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04 were also axis-aligned (X-only or Y-only per polygon). Use only axis-aligned (X or Y) move and resize deltas for V5 polygon-level shape repair; diagonal or rotated transforms will trigger GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL violations. Instance-level `move_instance` deltas may combine X and Y components in a single op (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0013.09, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04) because instance placement translation does not alter edge angles of the instantiated shapes.

## Enclosure Rule Summary (Operational)

- **V5.M5.EN.1**: M5 must enclose V5 by ≥ 11 nm on at least two opposite sides. The 512 dbu Y resize in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02 increased V5 extent; M5 was co-modified on the same axis to maintain this enclosure.
- **V5.M6.EN.2**: M6 must enclose V5 by ≥ 11 nm on two opposite sides. Same co-modification applies (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.02).
- **V5.M6.AUX.2**: V5 width perpendicular to M6 length must exactly match M6 width in that direction. A resize that changes V5 in the M6-perpendicular axis must be mirrored exactly in M6 or it introduces a new V5.M6.AUX.2 violation. Cross-axis M6/M5 resize modifications without V5 co-modification on the same axis break this constraint (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58.01).
- **V5.AUX.1**: V5 must remain inside both M5 and M6 after any repair. Instance moves that translate V5 instances must translate the enclosing M5 and M6 regions by the same vector, or enclosure violations result. Both trial:i03.ug.leaf_0013.09 and trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04 touched all three layers (M5, M6, V5) together in 15-op move-only repairs with connectivity preserved.
