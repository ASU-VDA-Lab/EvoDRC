**Width and Area Minimums**

M2.W.1 sets a minimum polygon width of 18 nm. M2.A.1 sets a minimum polygon area of 504 nm². The M2 stub inserted in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row20.10 has dimensions 56 x 72 dbu = 4032 nm², well above both limits, and was gated_in with conn_preserved. When inserting new M2 polygons, use dimensions that comfortably exceed both minimums; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row20.10 confirms that a 56 x 72 dbu rectangle is a safe stub geometry.

**Side-to-Side Spacing (M2.S.1)**

M2.S.1 requires 18 nm side-to-side spacing between edges longer than 36 nm. The dominant repair across all measured iterations is a lateral x-axis move_instance shift that simultaneously repositions all M2, M1, and V1 geometry inside a cell. Pure move_instance repairs with no polygon resize are consistently accepted: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01 (two move_instances, +36 dbu each, gated_in, zero new in-crop errors), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 (five move_instances, +40 dbu each, gated_in, zero new in-crop errors), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row6.18 (six move_instances, +28 to +36 dbu each, gated_in, zero new in-crop errors). Uniform lateral shifts that keep all wires on the same relative spacing correct side-to-side violations without creating new violations on adjacent tracks, as demonstrated by trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row6.18.

**Tip Spacing Rules (M2.S.2, M2.S.3, M2.S.4, M2.S.5)**

M2.S.2 requires 25 nm tip-to-side separation when one edge is 36 nm or shorter and the other is longer. M2.S.3 requires 27 nm tip-to-tip spacing when both edges are 24–36 nm. M2.S.4 requires 31 nm tip-to-tip spacing when both edges are narrower than 24 nm. M2.S.5 requires 31 nm tip-to-tip when one edge is 24–36 nm and the other is narrower than 24 nm. The repair pattern seen in the history is to extend M2 ends in x, which grows short tip edges into side edges (longer than 36 nm), reclassifying them under the less restrictive M2.S.1. In trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19, polygon p3317 was extended +128 dbu on the high-x end; gated_in, zero new in-crop errors. In trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row5.17, two polygons (p3737, p3746) were each extended +92 dbu on their high-x ends; gated_in, one new in-crop error, no out-of-crop errors. Extend M2 ends rather than retract them when addressing tip-spacing violations; retraction would shrink polygon width toward the M2.W.1 floor, whereas the measured end-extension approach in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row5.17 grows the tip edge clear of the tip-spacing rules.

**Run-Length Rule (M2.S.7)**

M2.S.7 forbids an 18 nm tip-to-tip gap co-located with a side-to-side spacing of 32 nm or less; the parallel run length must be at least 35 nm when side spacing is 32 nm or less. The measured fix is an end extension on one M2 polygon. In trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row9.06, polygon p3683 was extended +112 dbu on the high-x end alongside a +56 dbu move_instance on i1150; gated_in, zero new in-crop errors. In trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14, three polygons (p3058 +136 dbu high-x, p3635 +120 dbu high-x, p3564 +128 dbu high-x) were extended; gated_in, two new in-crop errors, no out-of-crop errors. The single-end extension magnitudes observed for M2.S.7 repair range from +56 dbu low-x end (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row8.20, polygon p3430) to +308 dbu high-x end (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00, polygon p3286). Extend the deficient M2 polygon end by enough dbu to bring the run length above 35 nm; the values above bound the range of corrections that were accepted through unit_gate.

**Corner-to-Corner Spacing (M2.S.6)**

M2.S.6 requires a minimum Euclidian corner-to-corner spacing of 20 nm between any two M2 polygons. No trial in the measured history produced a new M2.S.6 violation after repair. The lateral move steps of 36–108 dbu applied in the majority of trials (e.g., trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 with steps of 36–108 dbu on multiple instances, gated_in; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05 with six move_instances at 36 dbu each, gated_in) are large relative to the 20 nm spacing requirement. The minimum observed successful move step of 3 dbu (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0022.10, gated_in) also produced no M2.S.6 regression, confirming that even sub-grid moves do not generate corner violations when surrounding geometry is not already marginal.

**Diagonal Gap Spacing (M2.S.8)**

M2.S.8 requires a minimum Euclidian 80 nm center-to-center distance between 18 nm tip-to-tip gaps on different tracks. No M2.S.8 violation was reported or introduced in any measured trial. End-extension repairs applied for M2.S.7 reduce the total number of 18 nm tip-to-tip gaps and therefore reduce M2.S.8 exposure; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 and trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row9.06 both demonstrate end extensions that closed tip-to-tip gaps without generating M2.S.8 violations.

**Y-Axis M2 Operations and Cross-Layer Coupling**

Small y-axis M2 moves on individual units are accepted through the unit_gate channel when connectivity is preserved. Trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08 moved polygon p3515 by −12 dbu on y with two instances (same layers); gated_in. Trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row15.03 moved instance i1062 by −84 dbu on y and resized polygon p3592 by +96 dbu on y; gated_in. Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0007.05 resized polygon p3592 by +72 dbu on the low-x end and −48 dbu on both the low-y and high-y ends; gated_in. These results establish that y-axis edits at magnitudes up to 96 dbu are accepted per-unit through unit_gate when the cell touches M2 together with V1, M3, or V2 layers.

Diagonal move_instance operations that combine x and y components in a single op are also accepted through unit_gate. Trial:i05.ug.leaf_0004.03 moved instance i0177 by [−96, −48] dbu (x and y simultaneously, touching M1, M2, V1); gated_in, one new in-crop error, zero out-of-crop errors. This extends the range of accepted y-axis magnitudes to 48 dbu for diagonal co-moves through unit_gate.

Large-batch y-axis M2 shrink operations must be submitted through the cu_pool channel as a combined operation with the coupled via-definition edit, not as standalone or unit_gate submissions. Trial:i04.ug.leaf_0008.07 submitted 73 ops (symmetric y-axis resize_end shrinks across 36 M2 polygons, plus one resize_via_shape on VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 in M3) through unit_gate and was rejected with decision "gated_out" and reason "empty_or_missing_patch." The identical 73-op set submitted as a pool-level edit in trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 was accepted ("applied") and reduced V2.M3.AUX.2 violations from 356 to 284 (−72), with connectivity preserved. Submitting the resize_via_shape on VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 alone—without the accompanying M2 y-shrinks—is also rejected: trial:i05.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 submitted only the single resize_via_shape (y-axis, −40 dbu on M3) through cu_pool and was rejected with decision "rejected_net_positive" because unit:leaf_0006 increased from 130 to 194 errors (+64 delta). Apply the M2 y-shrinks and the coupled resize_via_shape on the VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 via definition as a single combined operation; trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 records the accepted form and trial:i05.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 confirms that the via-def edit alone causes net regressions.
