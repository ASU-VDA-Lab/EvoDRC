## Rule Interactions Grounded in Measured Operations

**V2.AUX.1 and joint-layer movement.** V2.AUX.1 requires V2 to reside inside the intersection of M2 and M3. All accepted trials that moved V2 instances also co-moved the enclosing M2 and M3 geometry. In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.07, only M2, M3, and V2 were touched; instance `i1643` and polygon `p3383` received delta `[0, -57]`. In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.03 the same instance and polygon were moved by `[0, 21]` on the y-axis touching the same three layers, accepted with the same pattern. In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08 instance moves and polygon `p3515` all received `[0, -12]` on the y-axis. In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07 and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04, which touched M1 and V1 in addition to M2, M3, and V2, the M2 and M3 layers were co-moved in each accepted outcome. In trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01, polygon `p2916` and instances `i2005` and `i1790` were all moved by `[8, 0]` on the x-axis; in trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02, polygon `p2877` and instances `i1706` and `i1738` were moved by `[8, 0]`. Both x-axis co-movement trials were accepted with zero new violations, confirming that co-movement of M3 and V2 satisfies V2.AUX.1 along either axis.

**Non-uniform instance deltas within a repair group.** Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07 moved instance `i0177` by `[-16, -48]` and instance `i0184` by `[0, -48]`; the y-components match but the x-components differ by 16 dbu. Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04 moved instance `i1117` by `[68, 0]` and instance `i1168` by `[172, 0]`; the y-components match but the x-components differ by 104 dbu. Both trials were accepted with zero new violations, establishing that instances within one repair group do not carry an identical-delta requirement across all axes, provided the enclosing M2 and M3 geometry is also adjusted to maintain V2.AUX.1 coverage.

**V2.M3.AUX.2 and bilateral symmetric resize_end on M3 stubs.** V2.M3.AUX.2 requires V2 to exactly match M3's width in the direction perpendicular to M3 length. Trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 fixed 72 V2.M3.AUX.2 violations in a single applied edit by applying bilateral `resize_end` shrinks to 36 M3 stub polygons (most received -32 dbu on both the low-y and high-y ends; six received -32 dbu low / -41 dbu high) alongside a `resize_via_shape` operation on the via cell definition. This is the first measured record identifying V2.M3.AUX.2 as the targeted rule and confirming that symmetric or asymmetric bilateral shrinking of M3 stubs—applied simultaneously to both ends on the y-axis—resolves V2.M3.AUX.2 at scale when the via landing shape is also adjusted.

**V2.M3.EN.2 and V2.M3.AUX.2 width/enclosure coupling.** M3 must enclose V2 by 5 nm on at least one pair of opposite sides and must exactly match V2's width perpendicular to M3 length. In trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26, M3 end-cap polygons `p2432` and `p3537` were extended on their high-y ends (by 68 dbu and 48 dbu respectively) while their associated V2-containing instances were moved by `[0, 48]`. In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07, polygon `p3537` was shrunk on its high-y end by 48 dbu via `resize_end` while instances were moved by differing deltas; the trial was accepted with zero new violations. Extending or shrinking an M3 end-cap via `resize_end` alongside co-movement of the associated via instance satisfies V2.M3.EN.2 and preserves V2.M3.AUX.2 when the flush condition on the perpendicular edges is maintained.

**V2.M2.EN.1 via instance co-movement.** M2 must enclose V2 by 5 nm on at least two opposite sides. All trials that touched V2 also touched M2 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.07, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.03, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01), indicating that the enclosure margin is set at placement and preserved by co-movement of the via with its parent metal stack or by adjustment of the via cell shape.

**V2.S.1 spacing and mask context.** The spacing rule classifies via pairs by their M3 track relationships (same track: 18 nm; parallel not aligned: 27 nm; parallel aligned: 18 nm) using a mask derived from M3-coincident edges and end-cap extensions. Moving an M3 polygon jointly with its V2 (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.07, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.03, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02) changes the effective mask and resolves spacing violations without altering via size.

**resize_end on M3 as an enclosure and width repair tool.** Trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26 applied `resize_end` on the high-y ends of `p2432` (+68 dbu) and `p3537` (+48 dbu), extending M3 past V2 to satisfy the 5 & 0 nm enclosure variant of V2.M3.EN.2. Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07 applied `resize_end` on the high-y end of `p3537` by -48 dbu (shrink), accepted with zero new violations. Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04 applied `resize_end` on `p2720` (y high, +20 dbu) and `p3300` (x low, -88 dbu), alongside two instance moves and one `add_polygon` operation, also accepted with zero new violations. Trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 applied bilateral symmetric `resize_end` shrinks (both low and high ends of the y-axis) to 36 M3 stub polygons, eliminating 72 V2.M3.AUX.2 violations. All `resize_end` trials were accepted, confirming that one-sided, asymmetric, or bilateral symmetric M3 end-cap adjustment is a valid repair path for V2.M3.EN.2 and V2.M3.AUX.2 when paired with via instance co-movement or via cell shape adjustment.

**add_polygon as a multi-layer repair operation.** Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04 added a new M3 polygon with corners at `[11664,11756], [11664,11828], [11908,11828], [11908,11756]` (72 dbu tall, 244 dbu wide), alongside two instance moves and two `resize_end` operations, touching M1, M2, M3, V1, and V2. The trial was accepted with zero new violations. Adding an M3 polygon as part of a multi-operation repair is a confirmed viable operation within gated_in outcomes.

**resize_via_shape as a via cell definition repair tool.** Trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 applied a `resize_via_shape` operation on cell `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` (layer M3, shape_index 0, delta -40 dbu on y-axis) as part of the 73-op set that fixed 72 V2.M3.AUX.2 violations. This operation shrinks the M3 landing geometry inside the via cell definition rather than moving or resizing polygons in the top-level layout. The cu_pool channel `applied` decision with `conn_preserved: true` confirms that `resize_via_shape` is a valid repair operation for V2.M3.AUX.2 when combined with bilateral `resize_end` on the corresponding M3 stub polygons.

**cu_pool channel vs unit_gate channel.** The unit_gate channel evaluates repairs on individual unit crops and requires a non-empty spatial patch (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0008.07, `gated_out`, reason `empty_or_missing_patch`). The cu_pool channel applies repairs pool-order on the full block assembly and records `decision: applied`; it targets via cell definitions via `resize_via_shape` and is the correct channel for block-wide V2.M3.AUX.2 repairs that modify via cell shapes. Identical operation sets may be rejected in unit_gate (empty patch) while succeeding in cu_pool (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0008.07 vs trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01).

## What the History Does Not Cover

All accepted trials used `move`, `move_instance`, `resize_end`, `add_polygon`, or `resize_via_shape` operations on M1–M3 and V1–V2. No trial attempted resizing V2 directly or splitting/merging via instances. The single gated_out (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0008.07) was rejected for `empty_or_missing_patch`, not for producing new DRC violations; no rejection on DRC grounds has been recorded. The cu_pool trial (trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01) is the first measured record explicitly attributing a repair to a specific rule (V2.M3.AUX.2); prior gated_in trials provide no breakdown of which rule triggered their original violations. The single case of new in-crop violations (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0001.03, n_new_in_crop:3) provides no breakdown of which rule generated those violations.
