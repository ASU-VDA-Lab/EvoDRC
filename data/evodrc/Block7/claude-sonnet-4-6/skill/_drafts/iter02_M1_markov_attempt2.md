## Operation Vocabulary Observed Through Iteration 2

Five operation types appear across all gated-in trials: `move_instance`, `resize_end`, `resize` (symmetric), `move` (polygon), and `add_polygon`. Every trial was accepted with `conn_preserved: true`.

**move_instance** is the primary operation. It appears in every trial and rigidly displaces one or more cell instances in x or y, carrying all layers in those cells (M1, V1, and any enclosed vias) as a unit. A single `move_instance` of 108 dbu was the sole correction in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.22. Five `move_instance` ops with no polygon edits sufficed in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21. ## Layer Coupling

Every trial in iterations 1 and 2 touches M1, M2, and V1 simultaneously. Three trials additionally touch M3 and V2: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26, and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08. Because `move_instance` carries all layers in the cell rigidly, any instance displacement that adjusts M1 co-moves the associated V1 and M2 shapes. Polygon-level edits (`resize_end`, `resize`, `move`) act only on the named polygon and do not automatically co-move vias, so the relative position between a resized M1 terminal and any V0 or V1 it must enclose changes when only M1 is extended. All gated-in trials across both iterations confirm this multi-layer coupling is compatible with connectivity preservation.
