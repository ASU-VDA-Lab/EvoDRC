## Operation Patterns

**Instance moves are the primary V1 repair action.** All 48 trials in this history use `move_instance` as at least one operation, shifting one or more cell instances along x, y, or both axes. The most common x-axis step sizes are 36 dbu and its multiples (72, 108, 136 dbu), consistent with the routing grid (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 — five instances each +40 dbu; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05 — six instances each +36 dbu; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 — moves of 36, 36, 108, -36, 72, 72, 72, 52 dbu). Non-multiple step sizes (28, 40, 52, 56, 64, 68 dbu) also occur when finer alignment is required (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row6.18 — delta 28 dbu on i1473; trial:i01.ug.leaf_0022.12 — delta 4 dbu on i0132).

**M2 resize_end accompanies instance moves to maintain enclosure and width matching.** When a V1-bearing instance is repositioned, the connected M2 polygon is frequently resized at its high or low x-axis end independently to preserve V1.M2.EN.2 enclosure and V1.M2.AUX.2 width matching. The resize amount does not always match the move amount — trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 pairs a +52 dbu instance move with a +308 dbu resize_end high on p3286, and trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row9.06 pairs a +56 dbu instance move with a +112 dbu resize_end high on p3683. Apply resize_end to the end of the M2 polygon that is leaving V1 exposed, not to both ends indiscriminately (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row8.20 — resize_end low only on p3430; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03 — resize_end low then high independently on p3526).

**Bidirectional x moves spread adjacent V1 sites apart.** When two neighboring instances both contribute to a spacing violation, they are moved in opposite x directions within the same trial. Apply negative and positive deltas to the respective instances rather than shifting both in the same direction (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06 — instances i0928 and i0407 at -36 dbu, i0320 at +36 dbu; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09 — instances i0026 at -72 dbu, i0294 at +36 dbu, i0418 at +72 dbu; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row18.08 — instance i0428 at -72 dbu, others at +72 dbu).

**Uniform group moves resolve row-level spacing.** When a cluster of instances on the same row all need the same positional adjustment, move all of them by the same delta in the same direction (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 — five instances each +40 dbu; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row6.18 — five instances each +36 dbu; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05 — six instances each +36 dbu; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01 — two instances each +36 dbu). Do not apply a uniform group shift when violations are between instances within the group — use bidirectional moves instead (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 — mixed deltas within a group of eight instances).

**Y-axis moves are valid and required for certain geometry configurations.** Moves along the y-axis address spacing or enclosure constraints that cannot be resolved by x-axis displacement alone. Apply y-axis instance moves and paired y-axis polygon resizes when the violation involves vertical M2 end-cap geometry (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26 — two instances at [0,+48] dbu with resize_end y high +68 on p2432 and +48 on p3537; trial:i03.ug.leaf_0007.05 — instance i1062 at [0,+84] dbu with y-axis resize_end adjustments on p3592; trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08 — two instances at [0,-12] dbu with polygon y move of -12 dbu on p3515).

**Diagonal instance moves (simultaneous x and y) are valid.** A `move_instance` with non-zero components on both axes does not produce non-orthogonal polygon edges in rectangular standard cells; it only repositions the cell origin. Use a diagonal move when both x and y enclosure or spacing margins are deficient simultaneously (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07 — i0177 moved [-16,-48] dbu; trial:i05.ug.leaf_0004.03 — i0177 moved [-96,-48] dbu). Both trials were accepted with conn_preserved=true.

**M2 add_polygon fills an absent or insufficient end-cap.** When there is no existing M2 polygon to resize, insert a new M2 rectangle aligned to the V1 position to satisfy V1.M2.EN.2 enclosure. In trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row20.10, a 56 × 72 dbu M2 rectangle was added at coordinates [5992,22824]–[6048,22896] alongside a -36 dbu instance move; the added polygon supplies the required M2 coverage where none previously existed. The add_polygon coordinates must be axis-aligned to avoid the NONORTHOGONAL violation.

**Uniform polygon resize (op:"resize") adjusts all sides simultaneously.** Three trials use the `resize` op rather than `resize_end`: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05 (p3586, +160 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09 (p3619, +40 dbu; p3654, -72 dbu; p3523, +72 dbu). Use uniform resize when the M2 polygon needs symmetric growth or shrink on both length ends, rather than one-sided adjustment.

## Repeat-Visit Patterns

**Partial repairs require follow-up iterations on the same unit.** Several units recur across consecutive iterations because an earlier fix resolved some violations but left others. Block7_union_row12 recurred at iter 1 and iter 2 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row12.01). Block7_union_row13 recurred across iter 1, 2, and 3 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row13.02, trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row13.01). Block7_union_row20 recurred across iter 1, 2, and 3 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row20.10, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row20.04, trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row20.02). Block7_union_row9 recurred at iter 1, 2, and 3 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row9.06, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04 covers same spatial vicinity). Apply a targeted, smaller move on the residual violation in follow-up iterations rather than repeating the full prior repair operation (iter 1 row20: large M2 add_polygon + -36 dbu move; iter 2 row20: three-instance row adjustment; iter 3 row20: single instance +36 dbu move).

**Instance i0177 required adjustments in four separate trials across three iterations.** trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26 (+48 y), trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07 ([-16,-48]), trial:i04.ug.leaf_0007.06 (+48 y), trial:i05.ug.leaf_0004.03 ([-96,-48]). When an instance recurs this frequently, the V1/M2 geometry at that site is constrained in multiple directions simultaneously; moves must address both axes and the connected polygon resizes at each pass.

## Multi-Layer Interactions

**V1 repair always co-modifies M1 and M2.** All 48 trials list M1 and M2 in touched_layers alongside V1. No trial modifies V1 geometry without also adjusting the M1 landing and M2 wire. This is required to maintain V1.AUX.1 (V1 inside both M1 and M2), V1.M1.EN.1 (5 & 2 nm M1 enclosure), V1.M2.EN.2 (5 & 5 nm or 5 & 0 nm M2 enclosure), and V1.M2.AUX.2 (V1 width equals M2 width perpendicular to M2 length direction).

**Six trials also touched M3 and V2, indicating upward cascades.** trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0007.06 all include M3 and V2 in touched_layers. Each of these trials involves a y-axis component or an M3 add_polygon (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04 adds an M3 polygon at [11664,11756]–[11908,11828] to restore connectivity after repositioning below). When a V1 fix involves y-axis movement or the M2 endpoint geometry changes in ways that affect the M2/V2/M3 stack, include the higher layers in the repair operation.

## Violation Count Behavior

**n_new_in_crop > 0 does not block acceptance when connectivity is preserved.** Multiple trials introduced new in-crop violations yet were accepted under the gated_in decision: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03 (1 new), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11 (9 new), trial:i04.ug.leaf_0006.05 (173 new, full-design locus [1728,2068]–[28728,28172]), trial:i04.ug.leaf_0007.06 (48 new, near-full locus [1728,3148]–[28728,27092]). The acceptance criterion in this history is conn_preserved=true, not zero new violations. Global-scope single-instance moves (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0006.05 — i0235 at -108 dbu; trial:i04.ug.leaf_0007.06 — i0177 at +48 y dbu) can simultaneously open many new violations across the design while still being gated in.

**Small locus trials with n_new_in_crop=0 are the norm.** The majority of trials — 35 of 48 — produce zero new in-crop violations. These are localized, tightly constrained repairs affecting a single row or leaf cell. Prefer smaller locus repairs that keep n_new_in_crop at zero; reserve global-scope moves for cases where localized adjustment cannot satisfy the constraint.

## Geometry Bounds from DRC Rules

**V1 width along the M2 length direction must remain at or above 18 nm (V1.W.1).** Resize operations on M2 polygons that increase M2 length support this constraint by expanding the region over which V1 is enclosed. Operations in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 (resize_end +128 on p3317) and trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row9.06 (resize_end +112 on p3683) extend M2 without shrinking the underlying V1 footprint.

**V1.M2.AUX.2 requires V1 width to equal M2 width in the direction perpendicular to M2 length.** All resize_end operations in the history act on the x-axis ends (length axis) of M2 polygons, leaving the y-dimension (perpendicular width) unchanged. This preserves the V1/M2 width match without requiring additional adjustment (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03 — both resize_end ops on x-axis of p3526; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14 — three resize_end high ops on x-axis of p3058, p3635, p3564).

**V1.M2.EN.2 requires M2 to enclose V1 on two opposite sides by at least 5 & 5 nm or 5 & 0 nm.** The second variant (5 & 0 nm) corresponds to a V1 edge coincident with the M2 edge (full-flush); the DRC deck checks for this via `v1_en2_ep_zero`. When M2 end-cap is being provided only on one side (flush on the other), resize_end operations that increase M2 overhang on the non-flush side resolve enclosure violations without needing to add material on the flush side (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row5.17 — resize_end +92 high on p3737 and p3746).

**V1.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V1 on two opposite sides by 5 nm on one axis and 2 nm on the other.** Instance moves that shift V1 out of M1 enclosure coverage must be paired with M1 adjustments to maintain the 5 & 2 nm requirement. All trials that move V1-bearing instances also touch M1, confirming that M1 is always co-adjusted.

**V1.S.1 minimum spacing (18 nm same-track, 27 nm parallel not-aligned, 18 nm parallel aligned) is enforced through the mask geometry that extends 5 nm beyond V1 for wec-type vias.** Bidirectional instance moves that spread adjacent V1 instances apart resolve V1.S.1 by increasing the mask-space separation (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06 — -36 dbu and +36 dbu on adjacent instances; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09 — negative and positive moves on instances in the same region).

**V1.S.2, V1.S.3, and V1.S.4 govern corner-to-corner Euclidean spacing based on end-cap type.** These rules check whether the extended mask geometry (wec_mask or nec_mask) violates 16.4 nm, 16.12 nm, or 17.11 nm Euclidean separation respectively. Instance moves that increase the physical distance between V1 centers resolve these violations. The relevant repair is the same as for V1.S.1 — reposition instances to increase inter-via distance — with the distinction that the required delta may be larger when the violation is corner-to-corner (diagonal approach) rather than edge-to-edge (projection approach).

**Non-orthogonal polygon edges are prohibited on V1 (and all drawing layers).** All resize_end, resize, move, and add_polygon operations in this history produce axis-aligned results. The add_polygon in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row20.10 uses exclusively 90-degree corners. Diagonal instance move vectors (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0004.03) do not produce non-orthogonal edges because standard cell geometry is rectangular; only the resulting edge angles matter for the NONORTHOGONAL check, not the move vector direction.
