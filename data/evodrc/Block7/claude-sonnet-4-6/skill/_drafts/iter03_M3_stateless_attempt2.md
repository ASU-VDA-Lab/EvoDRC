**Spacing rule hierarchy for sizing repair moves**

M3.W.1 sets 18 nm as the minimum M3 width. M3.S.1 requires 18 nm side-to-side spacing between edges each longer than 36 nm. M3.S.2 raises the requirement to 25 nm for tip-to-side pairs (tip ≤36 nm, side >36 nm). M3.S.3 requires 27 nm between two tips both in the 24–36 nm range. M3.S.5 requires 31 nm when one tip is in the 24–36 nm range and the other is below 24 nm. M3.S.4 requires 31 nm between two narrow tips both below 24 nm. M3.S.6 adds a 20 nm euclidean minimum that fires on corner-to-corner proximity not captured by projection-based checks. The accepted insertion in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04 (zero new violations, 72 dbu × 244 dbu polygon) shows that keeping both polygon spans above the 36 nm edge-length threshold restricts exposure to M3.S.1 only (18 nm), the least demanding spacing rule in the set.
