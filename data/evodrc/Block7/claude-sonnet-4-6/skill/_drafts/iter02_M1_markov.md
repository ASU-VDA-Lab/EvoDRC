## Operation Vocabulary Observed in Iterations 1–2

Five operation types appear across all gated-in trials: `move_instance`, `resize_end`, `resize` (symmetric), `move` (polygon), and `add_polygon`. Every trial across both iterations was accepted with `conn_preserved: true`.

**move_instance** is the primary operation. It appears in every trial and rigidly displaces one or more cell instances in x or y, carrying all layers in those cells (M1, V1, and any enclosed vias) as a unit. A single `move_instance` of 108 dbu was the sole correction in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.22. Five `move_instance` ops with no polygon edits sufficed in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21. Three `move_instance` ops at 36 dbu each sufficed in trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row12.01.

**resize_end** adjusts one end (high or low) of a named M1 polygon along one axis by a signed delta. High-end x extensions appear in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 (+308 dbu, p3286), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row5.17 (+92 dbu, p3737 and p3746), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 (+128 dbu, p3317), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14 (+136, +120, +128 dbu on p3058, p3635, p3564), trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row20.04 (+56 dbu, p3048), trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row22.05 (+92 dbu, p3527), and trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row9.06 (+112 dbu, p3683). Low-end x adjustments appear in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15 (+56 dbu, p3384), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row17.07 (+56 dbu, p3187), and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row8.20 (+56 dbu, p3430). Use `resize_end` when only one M1 terminal requires adjustment while the opposite end must remain fixed.

**resize** (symmetric) adjusts both ends equally. In iteration 1 it targeted the x-axis only: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05 (+160 dbu on p3586, x-axis) and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09 (+40 dbu on p3619, -72 dbu on p3654, +72 dbu on p3523, all x-axis). Iteration 2 adds a y-axis symmetric resize: trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row15.03 (+96 dbu on p3592, y-axis), accepted with zero new violations. Apply `resize` when both ends of an M1 segment require equal adjustment; the axis may be x or y depending on the deficiency direction.

**move** (polygon) translates an entire polygon without touching its parent instance. In trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06, polygon p3516 was moved -12 dbu in y; in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26, polygons p2432 and p3537 were moved +68 dbu and +48 dbu in y; in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08, polygon p3515 was moved -12 dbu in y — all accepted.

**add_polygon** was not used on M1 in iterations 1 or 2. The single `add_polygon` observed (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row20.10) targeted layer M2.

## Layer Coupling

Every trial across both iterations touches M1, M2, and V1 simultaneously in `touched_layers`. Three trials additionally touch M3 and V2: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26, and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08. Because `move_instance` carries all layers in the cell rigidly, any instance displacement that adjusts M1 co-moves the associated V1 and M2 shapes. Polygon-level edits (`resize_end`, `resize`, `move`) act only on the named polygon and do not automatically co-move vias, so the relative position between a resized M1 terminal and any V0 or V1 it must enclose changes when only M1 is extended. All gated-in trials confirm this multi-layer coupling is compatible with connectivity preservation.

## V0 Enclosure by M1 (V0.M1.EN.1)

V0.M1.EN.1 requires that V0 be enclosed by M1 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides (either a 5 & 5 nm pair or a 5 & 0 nm pair using the projection-based check). The rule also fires if any M1 enclosure edge is less than 1 dbu. `resize_end` on the M1 high or low x-end is the observed mechanism for extending M1 past V0 to achieve required enclosure margins. All such operations extended the M1 end outward and were accepted: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 (+308 dbu high-x p3286), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15 (+56 dbu low-x p3384), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 (+128 dbu high-x p3317), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row5.17 (+92 dbu high-x p3737 and p3746), trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row20.04 (+56 dbu high-x p3048), trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row22.05 (+92 dbu high-x p3527), trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row9.06 (+112 dbu high-x p3683). Move the appropriate M1 end outward by `resize_end` to provide the missing enclosure on the deficient side while leaving the opposite terminal in place.

## V0 Width Coincidence (V0.M1.AUX.3)

V0.M1.AUX.3 requires that any V0 edge not coincident with an M1 edge must be absent in both horizontal and vertical projections — equivalently, V0 width exactly equals M1 width along the direction perpendicular to M1 length. All `resize_end` operations across both iterations targeted the x-axis only (the M1 length direction), leaving the y-dimension of M1 polygons affected by that operation type unchanged. A y-axis `resize` (+96 dbu on p3592) does appear in trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row15.03 and was accepted with zero new violations, establishing that y-axis symmetric resize is compatible with AUX.3 when applied to M1 polygons in that context. The two iterations' polygon-translate operations (`move`) also applied on the y-axis without introducing AUX.3 violations: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06 (y-axis move on p3516), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26 (y-axis move on p2432 and p3537), trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08 (y-axis move on p3515). Avoid applying `resize_end` in the y-axis direction, as no such operation appears in any accepted trial; all y-axis polygon modifications use `move` (translation) or `resize` (symmetric), both of which keep M1 edge positions aligned with V0 edges when the coincidence condition was pre-satisfied.

## V1 Enclosure by M1 (V1.M1.EN.1)

V1.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V1 by at least 5 nm on one side and 2 nm on the opposite side. V1 appears in `touched_layers` for every trial, always together with M1. The `resize_end` pattern used for V0.M1.EN.1 (extending M1 ends outward in x) addresses V1 enclosure as well, since V1 and V0 enclosure both depend on M1 terminal positions. All trials applying `resize_end` on M1 x-ends were accepted with conn_preserved=true: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row5.17, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row20.04, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row22.05, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row9.06, and others.

## Width and Spacing Rules (M1.W.1, M1.S.1 through M1.S.6)

M1.W.1 mandates 18 nm minimum width. M1.S.1 requires 18 nm side-to-side spacing when both opposing edges exceed 36 nm. M1.S.2 through M1.S.6 impose tip-to-side and tip-to-tip spacings of 25–31 nm depending on tip edge length. No trial in either iteration applied a `resize_end` or `resize` that shrank an M1 polygon in the dimension relevant to spacing — every polygon resize moved M1 ends outward. The minimum resize_end delta observed is +16 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, p3539 high-x); the maximum is +308 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00, p3286 high-x). Spacing violations are addressed by moving the relevant instance away from its neighbor by `move_instance` (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21: five instances each +40 dbu in x; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01: two instances each +36 dbu in x; trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row12.01: three instances each +36 dbu in x) or by retracting one M1 end using `resize_end` with a positive delta on the appropriate low or high end away from the violating neighbor (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row17.07: +56 dbu low-x on p3187; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row8.20: +56 dbu low-x on p3430).

## Area Rule (M1.A.1)

M1.A.1 requires a minimum polygon area of 504 nm². No `add_polygon` on M1 was used in any trial across either iteration. Area increases are achieved through `resize_end` or `resize` operations that enlarge existing M1 polygons: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05 (`resize` +160 dbu on p3586 x-axis) and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09 (`resize` +72 dbu on p3523 x-axis). No trial deleted or reduced an M1 polygon in a way that risked M1.A.1.

## Redundant Island Rule (M1.R.0)

M1.R.0 flags an M1 polygon that encloses exactly one small V0 and sits within 400 nm of a large empty M1 region (>= 500 nm wide, area > 2.5 µm²). No trial in either iteration removed an M1 polygon or severed a via connection; all gated-in trials preserved or extended M1 coverage with conn_preserved=true.

## Non-Orthogonal Geometry (GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL)

Every polygon operation across both iterations produced only axis-aligned edge movements. All `move_instance` displacements are strictly x or y components (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.22: [108,0]; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06: [0,-12]; trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row15.03: [-96,0] and [0,-84]; trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08: [0,-12]; trial:i02.ug.leaf_0022.10: [3,0]). All `resize_end` deltas are single-axis x. The one y-axis `resize` in trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row15.03 (+96 dbu, p3592) is also single-axis. No diagonal or non-90° delta appears in any accepted trial across either iteration. Restrict all M1 edits to x or y axis components to avoid GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL violations, as borne out by the absence of any non-orthogonal operation in the full accepted set.
