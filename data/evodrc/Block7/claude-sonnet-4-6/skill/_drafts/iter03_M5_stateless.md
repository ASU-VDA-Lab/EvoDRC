## M5 Width and Track Constraints Summary

The following width values are prohibited by M5.W.3 and M5.W.4 and must never result from any resize operation: 48, 72, 96, 144, 168, 192, 240, 264, 288, 336, 360, 384, 432, 456, 480 nm (even multiples of the 24 nm minimum and track-spanning widths). The minimum allowed horizontal width is 24 nm (M5.W.1) and maximum is 480 nm (M5.W.2); no resize should produce a width outside [24, 480] nm or equal to any prohibited value. Vertical (y-axis) width minimum is 44 nm (M5.W.5). M5.AUX.3 prohibits any bend: all polygons must remain rectangular; no operation may introduce a corner angle other than 90 degrees. M5.AUX.2 requires minimum-width (24 nm horizontal) M5 tracks to be centered on vertical routing tracks at 192 dbu pitch with 48 dbu offset; x-axis moves applied to minimum-width segments must snap to this grid. M5.AUX.4 prohibits wide M5 polygon outside edges from coinciding with routing track edges; resizes that widen an M5 polygon must verify the resulting edge does not land on a track boundary. The history records do not yet provide a direct measured citation for violations of M5.W.3, M5.W.4, M5.W.5, M5.AUX.3, or M5.AUX.4 independently, so those rules are noted here as constraints to verify analytically against any proposed geometry rather than from a repair-outcome citation.
