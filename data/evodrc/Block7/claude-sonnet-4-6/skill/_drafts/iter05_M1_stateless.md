## Repair Primitives

**move_instance is the primary M1 DRC repair primitive.** Every one of the 46 accepted trials across iterations 1-5 includes at least one `move_instance` operation. Instance moves are applied almost exclusively along the x-axis; y-axis instance moves appear in only a handful of trials (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0007.05, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0005.04, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0007.06, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0004.03). Moving instances along x shifts M1 wire stubs and interconnect segments simultaneously, correcting spacing and enclosure violations across the full cell hierarchy without requiring explicit per-polygon edits.

**resize_end is the secondary M1 repair primitive.** It adjusts a single end of an M1 polygon along one axis, used to correct enclosure shortfalls after an instance move leaves a via partially exposed. Observed high-end x extensions include deltas of 52, 56, 60, 72, 92, 100, 112, 120, 128, 136, 160, 192, and 308 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 uses +308 on p3286 high end and +180 then +56 on p3751; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 uses +128 on p3317 high end; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14 uses +136, +120, +128 on p3058, p3635, p3564 respectively). Low-end adjustments use deltas of 56, 92 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row8.20, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row17.07, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row18.08). Apply `resize_end` only on the specific end that violates the enclosure rule; do not touch the compliant end.

**resize (whole-polygon translate) is distinct from resize_end.** The `resize` operation translates the entire polygon along one axis, as in `{"axis":"x","delta_dbu":160,"op":"resize","polygon_id":"p3586"}` (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05), `{"axis":"x","delta_dbu":40,"op":"resize","polygon_id":"p3619"}` and `{"axis":"x","delta_dbu":-72,"op":"resize","polygon_id":"p3654"}` (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09), and the y-axis example `{"axis":"y","delta_dbu":96,"op":"resize","polygon_id":"p3592"}` (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0007.05). Use `resize` when the entire M1 polygon must shift to track a moved via rather than extending only one end.

**move (polygon translate) appears for individual polygon repositioning** at the polygon level rather than the instance level, as in `{"axis":"y","delta_dbu":-12,"op":"move","polygon_id":"p3516"}` (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08) and `{"axis":"y","delta_dbu":-12,"op":"move","polygon_id":"p3515"}` (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08). This form appears exclusively for y-axis adjustments in the recorded history.

## Step Granularity

**36 dbu is the dominant instance-move quantum.** It appears as the sole or primary delta in the majority of trials: for example, all six instances in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 move +36 or +52 in x; all five instances in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 move +40; the two-op trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01 moves both instances +36; trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row12.01, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row13.02, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row22.05 use uniform 36-dbu shifts. The multiples 72 and 108 are the next most common (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row18.08, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.22, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24). Use 36 dbu as the default step and escalate to 72 or 108 when the violation gap exceeds one step.

**Sub-grid moves are valid and accepted.** Trial:i02.ug.leaf_0022.10 moves instance i0100 by exactly 3 dbu (+3 x), resulting in 3 new in-crop violations but still gated in because connectivity was preserved. Trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row22.12 uses a 4-dbu move of i0132. These are not preferred (they produce more secondary violations) but are not rejected. Use them only when a full 36-dbu step overshoots a constraint on the other side.

**Large moves (>108 dbu) occur for severe spacing violations.** Trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 moves i1180 by +288 dbu and i1140 by +52 dbu, with a +308 resize_end on p3286. Trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03 moves i0507 and i0482 by +136 dbu and extends p3525 by +192. Trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14 moves i0215 by +136. Apply large moves when the measurement gap to the nearest DRC-clean position is confirmed to exceed 108 dbu.

## Multi-Layer Coupling

**Every accepted M1 trial simultaneously touches M1, M2, and V1.** No trial in the history repairs M1 alone. The `touched_layers` field shows `["M1","M2","V1"]` in the majority of trials, with a subset also touching M3 and V2 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26). Never move an M1 polygon or instance without also repositioning the attached V0/V1 vias and the connected M2 segments. V1.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V1 by 5 nm on one axis and 2 nm on the other; instance moves that correct M1.S.1 or M1.S.2 spacing violations will also shift V1 landing positions relative to M1, so the enclosure must be re-verified on M1 after every instance move.

**add_polygon is used for M2/M3 connectivity restoration, not for M1 creation.** Only two `add_polygon` ops appear in the dataset: one adds a 56x72 dbu M2 rectangle at (5992,22824)-(6048,22896) in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row20.10 after an instance move broke an M2 connection, and one adds a 244x72 dbu M3 rectangle in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04. No M1 polygon is ever added in the recorded history. Do not create new M1 polygons; instead resize existing ones or move the instances that contain them.

## Connectivity Preservation as Acceptance Gate

**Connectivity preservation overrides DRC count.** All 46 trials are accepted with `conn_preserved: true` regardless of the `n_new_in_crop` value. Trial:i04.ug.leaf_0006.05 introduces 173 new in-crop violations and is accepted. Trial:i04.ug.leaf_0007.06 introduces 48 new in-crop violations and is accepted. Trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03 and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09 each introduce 1 new in-crop violation and are accepted. Conversely, no trial in the history is rejected, so the history provides no negative examples of connectivity breaks. Never apply an op sequence that severs a net even if it would produce zero DRC violations in the local crop window.

**Wide-locus single-op moves carry high secondary DRC risk.** Trials with loci spanning the near-full design (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0006.05 locus 1728-28728 x, 2068-28172 y; trial:i04.ug.leaf_0007.06 locus 1728-28728 x, 3148-27092 y) involve only a single `move_instance` operation but produce 173 and 48 new in-crop violations respectively. These are accepted only because connectivity holds. Prefer narrow-locus, multi-op sequences over single wide-locus moves when the goal is net DRC reduction.

## Enclosure Rule Repair (V0.M1.EN.1, V1.M1.EN.1)

**V0.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V0 by 5 nm on two opposite sides (or 5 nm on one side with 0 nm on the other via the endpoint-zero path).** The two-sided check uses `m1.enclosing(v0, 5.nm, projection)` along horizontal and vertical independently. A via fails when neither the horizontal nor the vertical pair of enclosing edges satisfies the 5 nm requirement on both sides. The endpoint-zero path (`v0_en1_ep_zero` using 1.dbu) captures the asymmetric case. Repair by extending the M1 polygon end that is deficient using `resize_end`, ensuring the resulting enclosure on the short axis reaches at least 5 nm. The observed resize_end deltas of 52-308 dbu are consistent with correcting deficits that started below 5 nm and require overshoot for margin (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row18.08, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14).

**V1.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V1 by 5 nm on one axis and 2 nm on the other.** The rule checks for good enclosure in horizontal and vertical independently: a V1 is satisfied if it is fully enclosed in one orientation (`v1_m1en1_hgood` = not_interacting with bad2h and interacting with has5h; `v1_m1en1_vgood` analogously). The asymmetry (5 nm vs. 2 nm) means M1 stripe extensions of as little as 2 nm cover the short-axis requirement. The y-axis resize_end in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26 (+68 high on p2432, +48 high on p3537) and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07 (-48 high on p3537) address V1.M1.EN.1 in the y dimension by adjusting M1 polygon extents.

**V0.M1.AUX.3 requires V0 to be exactly the same width as M1 in the direction perpendicular to M1 length.** The rule fires on vias whose edges are not coincident with M1 edges. When an instance move shifts M1 but leaves a V0 inside wider than M1, correct by resizing the M1 end to match the V0 dimension rather than shrinking the V0.

## Spacing Rule Context (M1.S.1 through M1.S.6)

**M1.S.1 (18 nm side-to-side) and M1.S.2 (25 nm tip-to-side) govern the most common repair scenarios.** The edge-length thresholds in M1.S.1/S.2 (36 nm boundary) distinguish "side" edges (>36 nm) from "tip" edges (<=36 nm). Instance moves that shift cells by 36 dbu or more push M1 wire edges apart by that amount, directly resolving side-to-side violations above the 18 nm minimum. The prevalence of 36-dbu moves across nearly all rows of Block7 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01 through trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14, and their iter-2/3 follow-ups) confirms that the dominant layout error is sub-18 nm M1 side-to-side spacing correctable by a single grid step.

**M1.S.3 (27 nm tip-to-tip for edges 24-36 nm) and M1.S.4/S.5 (31 nm tip-to-tip for edges <24 nm) require larger clearance than M1.S.1.** When a resize_end reduces a tip below 36 nm, the applicable spacing rule escalates from M1.S.1 (18 nm) to M1.S.2 (25 nm) or M1.S.3 (27 nm). Resize_end deltas substantially larger than 36 dbu (e.g., +92, +112, +128, +136, +192 dbu seen across multiple trials) are used when the tip clearance violation gap exceeds the baseline 36-dbu step.

**M1.S.6 (20 nm corner-to-corner) is a tighter constraint than M1.S.1 and must be satisfied simultaneously.** Corner proximity can remain after a side-to-side spacing fix; verify corner clearance independently after each instance move or resize_end.

## Width and Area Constraints

**M1.W.1 (18 nm minimum width) must be verified after every resize_end.** Resizing one end of an M1 polygon along the narrow axis could reduce width below 18 nm. The observed resize_end operations are exclusively along the length axis (high/low ends of the wire), not across the width axis, consistent with preserving wire width. Do not apply resize_end in the width direction.

**M1.A.1 (504 nm-sq minimum area) constrains how much an M1 polygon can be shortened.** A polygon whose initial dimensions are near-minimum in both axes (e.g., 18 nm wide x 28 nm long = 504 nm-sq) cannot have its length reduced at all. The add_polygon path for M2 in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row20.10 (adding a 56x72 = 4032 dbu-sq M2 patch) is not an M1 area fix but indicates that downstream connectivity restoration may require injecting new metal on upper layers.

## Iteration Convergence Patterns

**Units are re-entered across multiple iterations with progressively smaller loci and deltas.** Block7_union_row10 is first repaired in iter 1 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00, locus 9576-19280 x, 11 ops, large moves and resize_end +308) and re-entered in iter 3 (trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row10.00, locus 9576-12672 x, 2 ops, move +56 and resize_end +52). Block7_union_row20 is repaired in iter 1 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row20.10, 2 ops), iter 2 (trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row20.04, 3 ops), and iter 3 (trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row20.02, 1 op). This demonstrates that large initial corrections leave residual violations requiring fine-grained follow-up. Each successive pass operates on a narrower locus and applies smaller deltas.

**Leaf cells are also re-entered across iterations.** leaf_0002 is repaired in iter 1 (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23, 1 op, +36 x) and iter 3 (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04, 5 ops, larger cross-layer adjustment including M3 add_polygon). leaf_0095 appears in iter 1 (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26, 5 ops, y-axis moves and resize_end) and its y-coordinate region is revisited in iter 3 (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07, 3 ops, y-axis fine correction). Leaf cell re-entry indicates that row-level fixes in iter 1 can shift leaf geometry enough to expose new leaf-level violations.

**Single-op trials are common in later iterations.** Iter 3 includes trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row20.02 (1 op: move_instance +36 x) and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0008.06 (1 op: move_instance +108 x). Iter 4 includes trial:i04.ug.leaf_0004.03 (1 op), trial:i04.ug.leaf_0005.04 (1 op), trial:i04.ug.leaf_0006.05 (1 op), trial:i04.ug.leaf_0007.06 (1 op). Iter 5 includes trial:i05.ug.leaf_0004.03 (1 op: move_instance -96 x, -48 y, with 1 new in-crop violation accepted). The trend from multi-op to single-op across iterations confirms that the algorithm narrows toward residual isolated violations.

## Geometry Directionality

**Y-axis corrections are required for M1 polygons that run in the x-direction (horizontal wires).** Y-direction moves and resize_ends appear in trials involving M3/V2 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06 moves two instances by [0,-12]; trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08 moves two instances by [0,-12]; trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26 moves two instances by [0,48]; trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07 moves two instances by [0,-48]). Y-axis corrections are smaller in magnitude (12 and 48 dbu dominate vs. 36-108 for x-axis) and always appear alongside multi-layer V2/M3 involvement, suggesting y-axis M1 violations are associated with via stacking in the vertical routing stack.

**Diagonal moves are not used.** No trial applies a simultaneous nonzero x and y delta to a polygon resize or move operation; the sole exception is `move_instance` in trial:i05.ug.leaf_0004.03 with `delta_dbu: [-96, -48]` on instance i0177, which is a combined x and y displacement on the instance (not a polygon resize). This confirms that M1 polygon reshaping is strictly Manhattan (orthogonal) throughout, consistent with the NONORTHOGONAL block rule that rejects any non-90-degree edges.
