## Repair Strategy Overview

Across all 48 measured repairs to layer V1 in case Block7, channel unit_gate (iterations 1 through 5), every trial was accepted with decision "gated_in" and conn_preserved=true (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 through trial:i05.ug.leaf_0004.03). The three primary repair operations observed are `move_instance` (translating cell instances so embedded V1 geometry shifts relative to neighboring shapes), `resize_end` (moving one endpoint of an M1 or M2 polygon along a single axis), and `resize` (uniform polygon shift on one axis). An `add_polygon` operation was used once to introduce a new M2 stub (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row20.10).

## Touched Layers and Co-Movement

All repairs touch at minimum M1, M2, and V1 simultaneously. V1 geometry is contained inside cell instances; translating an instance via `move_instance` shifts V1 together with the surrounding M1 region and the M2 track as a rigid body. This co-movement is the consistent pattern across the history (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, and all remaining iter-1 row trials). A subset of repairs also touched M3 and V2 when vertical routing adjustments were required alongside the V1 repair (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.08, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.04, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07).

## Dominant Direction of Movement

The dominant repair axis across the history is x. The vast majority of `move_instance` operations carry a non-zero x component and zero y component, as in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01 (both instances moved [36,0]), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14. Mixed x+y moves appear in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09 (instance i0949 moved [0,-48] while other instances moved along x) and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0011.07 (instance i0177 moved [-16,-48], instance i0184 moved [0,-48]).

## resize_end Operations on M2 Polygons

`resize_end` with end="high" and positive delta_dbu extends the high-x edge of an M2 polygon rightward, increasing its length in that direction. This was used in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 (polygon p3273, x, +56), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row5.17 (polygons p3737 and p3746, x, +92 each), and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14 (polygons p3058 +136, p3635 +120, p3564 +128).

`resize_end` with end="low" and positive delta_dbu moves the low-x edge rightward, shortening M2 from the left. This appears in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15 (polygon p3384, x, +56), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row17.07 (polygon p3187, x, +56 end="low"), and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row8.20 (polygon p3430, x, +56 end="low").

Simultaneous resize_end on both ends of the same polygon shifts the polygon without changing its length when the two deltas are equal, or changes length when they differ. In trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, polygon p3526 received end="low" +56 and end="high" +100: the low edge moved right by 56 dbu and the high edge by 100 dbu, yielding a net rightward shift with a 44-dbu length increase.

Y-axis `resize_end` and `resize` operations correct vertical enclosure margins. Trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row15.03 resized polygon p3592 on y by +96 dbu (op="resize") alongside moving instance i1062 by [0,-84]. Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0007.05 applied three resize_end operations on polygon p3592: end="low" on x by +72, end="low" on y by -48, and end="high" on y by -48, after moving instance i1062 by [0,+84].
