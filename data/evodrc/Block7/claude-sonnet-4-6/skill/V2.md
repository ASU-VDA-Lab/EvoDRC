**V2.M3.AUX.2 — Width-match violation: root cause and proven fix**

V2.M3.AUX.2 fires when a V2 instance is not exactly the same width as its enclosing M3 segment along the axis perpendicular to M3's length. The full-deck repair confirmed at trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 reduced violations from 356 to 284 (−72, all attributed to V2.M3.AUX.2) by combining 72 M3-stub `resize_end` operations (y-axis, −32 dbu or −41 dbu on both low and high ends of the affected polygons) with a single `resize_via_shape` on the VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 cell definition (axis y, delta_dbu −40 on the M3 landing shape). This combo was applied at block:full-deck scope in the cu_pool channel and connectivity was preserved.

Do not apply the VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 M3-land shrink as a standalone pool edit. trial:i05.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 issued that single `resize_via_shape` operation in isolation and was rejected (decision: rejected_net_positive, delta_total +64): unit:leaf_0006 moved from 130 to 194 violations. The M3-stub `resize_end` set must accompany the cell-definition shrink; without it, the via land reduction widens the width mismatch for instances whose stubs have not yet been trimmed.

The identical 73-operation set (72 M3-stub resizes + 1 via-shape resize) was first attempted through the unit_gate channel at trial:i04.ug.leaf_0008.07 and was gated out with reason `empty_or_missing_patch` (locus null). The operations themselves were not defective; the gate rejection was a channel-routing artifact. The same op-set applied through the cu_pool channel at trial:i04.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 succeeded, confirming that the repair content is correct and that the appropriate channel for block-wide cell-definition edits is cu_pool, not unit_gate.
