## Operation Vocabulary Observed in Iteration 1

Five operation types appear across the 27 gated-in trials: `move_instance`, `resize_end`, `resize` (symmetric), `move` (polygon), and `add_polygon`. Every trial was accepted with `conn_preserved: true`.

**move_instance** is the primary operation. It appears in every trial and rigidly displaces one or more cell instances in x or y, carrying all layers in those cells (M1, V1, and any enclosed vias) as a unit. A single `move_instance` of 108 dbu was the sole correction in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.22. Five `move_instance` ops with no polygon edits sufficed in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21.

**resize_end** adjusts one end (high or low) of a named M1 polygon along one axis by a signed delta. High-end x extensions appear in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 (+308 dbu, p3286), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row5.17 (+92 dbu, p3737 and p3746), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 (+128 dbu, p3317), and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14 (+136, +120, +128 dbu on p3058, p3635, p3564). Use `resize_end` when only one M1 terminal requires adjustment while the opposite end must remain fixed, as confirmed by all of the above gated-in trials.

**resize** (symmetric) adjusts both ends equally and appears in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05 (+160 dbu on p3586, x-axis) and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09 (three polygons: +40 dbu on p3619, -72 dbu on p3654, +72 dbu on p3523, all x-axis). ## Layer Coupling

Every trial in iteration 1 touches M1, M2, and V1 simultaneously (all 27 trials list these three in `touched_layers`). Two trials additionally touch M3 and V2: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06 and trial:i01.ug.leaf_0095.26. Because `move_instance` carries all layers in the cell rigidly, any instance displacement that adjusts M1 co-moves the associated V1 and M2 shapes. Polygon-level edits (`resize_end`, `resize`, `move`) act only on the named polygon and do not automatically co-move vias, so the relative position between a resized M1 terminal and any V0 or V1 it must enclose changes when only M1 is extended.
