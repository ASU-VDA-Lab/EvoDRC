## Repair Operation Patterns

**Extending the high-x M1 edge (resize_end axis:x end:high) paired with move_instance is the established zero-violation repair pattern for enclosure violations.** Every trial in iterations 1 and 2 that combined move_instance with resize_end(axis:x, end:high) produced n_new_in_crop:0 and n_new_out_of_crop:0 (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row5.02, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0007.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0012.08, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01). Resize deltas on the high-x end across those trials ranged from 56 to 192 dbu, demonstrating tolerance across a wide range of extension magnitudes without introducing new violations.

**Pure move_instance ops with positive x-deltas also produce zero new violations** when applied without resize companions, as measured in trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row2.01 (two instances, delta +36 dbu each) and trial:i01.ug.leaf_0006.04 (two instances, delta +36 dbu each).

**Negative x-direction moves on large loci introduce violations when instance i0099 is the moved cell.** In trial:i01.ug.leaf_0013.09 (instance i0023, delta -36 dbu, locus [6696,9376,8048,9612]) and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.00 (instance i0233, delta -36 dbu, locus [7276,2268,7704,3132]), negative moves of identical magnitude produced n_new_in_crop:0. By contrast, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 (instance i0099, delta -36 dbu, locus [1728,3148,11016,9812]) produced n_new_in_crop:10. The distinguishing factors are the instance identity (i0099) and the large locus spanning most of the design. Do not apply a negative x-move to i0099 within the locus [1728,3148,11016,9812] without a compensating high-x resize on affected M1 polygons.

**Do not use resize_end(axis:x, end:low) to repair violations introduced by a negative instance move.** In trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00, a move_instance of -76 dbu on i0099 combined with resize_end(axis:x, end:low, delta:132) on p1207 left n_new_in_crop:10 — the same count as inherited from trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02. The end:low resize (repositioning the low-x boundary of p1207) did not reduce the violation count. Every zero-violation resize in the recorded history used end:high, never end:low (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row5.02, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0007.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0012.08, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01).

## Enclosure Rules (V0.M1.EN.1, V1.M1.EN.1)

V0.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V0 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides (or 5 & 0 nm on opposite sides via the projection check). V1.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V1 by 5 nm on one axis and 2 nm on the opposite side. All 14 recorded trials touch M1 and V1 simultaneously, making both rules continuously active. The consistent application of resize_end(axis:x, end:high) in all zero-violation trials (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00 through trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01) establishes that extending the M1 high-x edge is the correct compensating operation when an instance is moved in the positive-x direction. When an instance is moved in the negative-x direction (as in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 and trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 for i0099), the displaced M1 edge must be extended on the high-x side — not the low-x side — to maintain the required two-sided enclosure; trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 demonstrates that a low-x resize does not satisfy this requirement.

## Width and Spacing Rules (M1.W.1, M1.S.1 through M1.S.6)

M1.W.1 requires minimum M1 width of 18 nm. M1.S.1 requires minimum side-to-side spacing of 18 nm when both opposing edges exceed 36 nm. M1.S.2 requires 25 nm tip-to-side separation. Move magnitudes used in zero-violation trials — including 36, 72, 108, and 136 dbu steps (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row2.01, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0006.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0009.07, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03) — are all consistent with grid positions that avoid sub-minimum width and spacing. Larger move magnitudes (up to 136 dbu) in trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00 and trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03 also produced zero new violations, indicating that the magnitude alone does not trigger M1.S rules when the move direction is positive-x. The 10 new violations that appeared in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 and persisted through trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 are associated with uncompensated negative-x movement of i0099, not with the move magnitude per se.

## Large-Locus Sensitivity

The locus [1728,3148,11016,9812] spans the bulk of the design. Both iter:3 and iter:4 operations on this locus produced n_new_in_crop:10 (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00). All iter:1 trials used tighter, localized loci and achieved n_new_in_crop:0. When operating within this large locus, any negative-x displacement of i0099 must be paired with a resize_end(axis:x, end:high) on the M1 polygon(s) whose enclosure is affected. The end:low resize applied to p1207 in trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 does not constitute such compensation and left the violation count unchanged.

One trial applied resize_end(axis:y, end:high) on polygon p1159 with delta 20 dbu, simultaneously touching M1, M2, M3, and V1 (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.06). This produced n_new_in_crop:0.
