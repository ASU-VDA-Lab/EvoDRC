## Operation Patterns

**Paired move + resize is the dominant M2 repair primitive.** Every unit_gate repair in which a DRC fix was attempted on M2 used at least one `move_instance` alongside at least one `resize_end` on a polygon when the violation involved a polygon endpoint; trials that touched only instances without any polygon reshaping occurred only when no endpoint adjustment was required (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row2.01, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0006.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0009.07). Apply a paired move + resize strategy for any M2 spacing or enclosure error that implicates a polygon tip or end.

**Extend the high-x end of an M2 polygon to fix spacing violations on that side.** All observed shape resizes in the unit_gate channel used `resize_end` with `end="high"` and a positive `delta_dbu` along the x-axis, indicating that the standard correction for M2 polygon length deficiency is to extend the high-x terminus outward (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row5.02, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0007.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0012.08, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01). The single observed low-end resize (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00, `end="low"`, delta_dbu=132) was paired with a larger-magnitude negative x-move (delta_dbu=[-76,0]), which repositioned the whole unit before trimming the polygon's low end; do not apply a low-end resize without a compensating negative x-move.

**Move magnitude and resize delta are not identical; the resize must exceed the move.** In every multi-operation repair the resize delta consistently exceeds the instance move delta: trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00 move=136 vs. resize=192; trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03 moves=72–108 vs. resizes=92–164; trial:i01.ug.leaf_0012.08 move=72 vs. resize=108. Do not copy the move delta directly into the resize delta; the resize must absorb both the repositioning offset and any additional shortfall required by the enclosure or spacing rule.

**36 dbu is the minimum observed step for instance moves.** All `move_instance` operations use deltas that are multiples of 36 dbu or near-multiples (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row2.01: 36; trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row5.02: 36; trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00: 136; trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03: 72 and 108; trial:i01.ug.leaf_0012.08: 72; trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00: 76). Do not use sub-36 dbu moves for M2 unit repositioning.

**Both positive and negative x-moves are used.** Move direction depends on which neighbor is causing the violation: positive x-moves dominate in iter 1 (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row5.02, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03), while negative x-moves appear where spacing on the high-x side is too tight (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0013.09: delta=[-36,0]; trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.00: delta=[-36,0]; trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02: delta=[-36,0]; trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00: delta=[-76,0]).

**y-axis resizing is rare and paired with x-axis operations.** Only trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.06 used a y-axis `resize_end` (axis=y, end=high, delta=20), and it occurred together with an x-axis `resize_end` and two instance moves touching M1, M2, M3, and V1. Do not issue a y-axis resize in isolation; accompany it with x-axis correction and check for propagation into M3 routing.

**Every M2 unit_gate operation also modifies V1 and M1.** All eleven unit_gate trials that touched M2 list `touched_layers` as at least `["M1","M2","V1"]` (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00 through trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00). ## Acceptance Criteria

**Connectivity preservation is the primary gate for unit_gate decisions.** All thirteen unit_gate trials that were gated_in carry `conn_preserved: true` (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00 through trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00). Never propose an M2 repair that severs connectivity; gated_in status depends on this condition without exception.

**Out-of-crop new violations are a hard disqualifier.** Every gated_in trial records `n_new_out_of_crop: 0` (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00 through trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00). Do not issue moves or resizes whose extent reaches across the repair window boundary; contain all geometric changes within the stated locus.

**New in-crop violations are accepted when connectivity is preserved and out-of-crop count stays at zero.** Trial:i03.ug.leaf_0003.02 and trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 both record `n_new_in_crop: 10` yet remain gated_in. Do not treat new in-crop violations as disqualifying on their own; the binding constraint is out-of-crop violations, not in-crop count.

**The cu_pool channel uses net-violation-delta acceptance.** Trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 reached `decision: applied` with `delta_total: -27`. The cu_pool gate fires on a net reduction in the violation count, not on zero new violations.
