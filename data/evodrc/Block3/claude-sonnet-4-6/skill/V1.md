**Coordinated Move + Resize Pattern**

Ten of the fourteen trials pair each move_instance with a resize_end (axis: x) on the associated M2 polygon. Nine of those ten use end="high" and all nine produced 0 new violations (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row5.02, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0007.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0012.08, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.01, and the per-instance resize pairs within multi-instance trials).
