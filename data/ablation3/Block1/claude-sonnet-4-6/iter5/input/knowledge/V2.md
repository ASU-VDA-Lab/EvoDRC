## Rule Mechanics

**V2.W.1** flags any V2 shape whose width along the M3 length direction falls below 18 nm.

**V2.S.1** constructs a per-via mask before measuring spacing. Each V2 is first classified by edge coincidence with M3: a via whose entire edge set coincides with M3 edges is labelled NEC (no-end-cap); all others are WEC (with-end-cap). The NEC mask extends the via body by 5 nm beyond each M3-coincident edge on all four sides plus 5 nm perpendicular; the WEC mask extends the via's M3-coincident edges by 5 nm laterally, then applies a +5 nm / -5 nm size cycle to round corners. The combined mask minus M3 is used for the actual spacing measurements: horizontal non-M3 edges are checked at 17 nm projection, all non-M3-touching edges at 1 nm projection, V2-internal mask edges at 18 nm projection, and the full mask at 1 nm projection. The resulting effective minimums between V2 instances are 18 nm on the same M3 track, 27 nm on parallel tracks (not aligned), and 18 nm on parallel tracks (aligned).

**V2.S.2** computes a 16.4 nm euclidean spacing check on the WEC mask alone. The geometric equivalent for designers is a 23 nm corner-to-corner clearance between any two WEC vias.

**V2.S.3** computes a 16.12 nm euclidean spacing check on the NEC mask, then subtracts violations that are also caught by a 16.12 nm projection check (i.e., keeps only pure diagonal violations). The geometric equivalent is a 30 nm corner-to-corner clearance between any two NEC vias.

**V2.S.4** computes a 17.11 nm euclidean separation between the WEC mask and the NEC mask, again filtering out projection-coincident violations. The geometric equivalent is a 27 nm corner-to-corner clearance between one WEC and one NEC via.

**V2.M2.EN.1** evaluates enclosure by eroding M2 independently along each axis: `m2.sized(-5.nm, 0)` removes 5 nm of M2 in x only; `m2.sized(0, -5.nm)` removes 5 nm of M2 in y only. A V2 is flagged when it protrudes beyond the x-eroded M2 AND beyond the y-eroded M2 simultaneously. Consequently, a via passes EN.1 when M2 provides at least 5 nm enclosure on both members of at least one opposite-side pair in x, or at least 5 nm on both members of at least one opposite-side pair in y; satisfying only one axis direction is not sufficient.

**V2.M3.EN.2** uses a two-pass enclosure check. First, `m3.enclosing(v2, 5.nm, projection)` collects all edges where M3 falls short of 5 nm enclosure around V2. A V2 fails when it has no axis (horizontal or vertical) along which both opposite sides clear the 5 nm threshold. A zero end-cap on one side of a pair is permitted only when the other side of the same pair carries >=5 nm. The second sub-check (`enclosing(v2, 1.dbu, projection)`) catches the degenerate case of a flush or zero enclosure on any edge not already caught by the 5 nm check.

**V2.AUX.1** flags any V2 shape not fully contained within the Boolean intersection of M2 and M3.

**V2.M3.AUX.2** flags a V2 when fewer than two of its edges are coincident with M3 edges (checked via `v2_aux2_coinc` and `interacting(..., 2)`). This enforces that V2's dimension perpendicular to M3 length equals M3's width exactly at that location; any narrower or wider via body is flagged.

**NONORTHOGONAL** flags edges on V2 (and every other drawing layer) whose angle falls in the ranges 1deg-89deg, 91deg-179deg, -179deg--91deg, or -89deg--1deg. Only edges at exactly 0deg, 90deg, 180deg, or 270deg are permitted.
