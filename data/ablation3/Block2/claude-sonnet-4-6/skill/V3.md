### Family B: M4 on-grid reshape and V3 height matching (M4.AUX.1, V3.M4.AUX.2)

These two rules co-fired on the same M4 polygons in the reference-design block; reshaping the same 7 M4 polygons cleared all 7 M4.AUX.1 violations and all 7 V3.M4.AUX.2 violations simultaneously (seed, reference-design-verified). M4.AUX.1 fires because horizontal edges (y-coordinates) are off the 24 nm (96 dbu) grid; V3.M4.AUX.2 fires because the M4 y-extent does not match the V3 via y-height. The co-firing is specific to that block's off-grid M4 geometry and is not a universal invariant of the rule set (seed, reference-design-verified).

Family B in three verified facts (seed, reference-design-verified):
1. Seven M4 polygons were reshaped or narrowed (an eighth M4 reshape at center 432 belongs to Family D (-> see M4.md), not this family).
2. Six of the seven then needed a VIA34 y-move to the new M4 center; the seventh (center 2160) was already on a legal track, so its VIA34 stayed -- but the M4 itself was STILL reshaped (y-height 184 -> 96 with the center fixed at 2160, plus x-narrowing); its VIA45 moved in x only, following M5.
3. Every repaired M4 center satisfies y mod 192 == 48: both the edge-grid constraint (center at k*96+48) and the track-parity constraint (center_y mod 192 == 48) held at every repaired site.

Rule semantics:
- M4.AUX.1: M4 y-edges were at multiples of 96 dbu at all 7 cleared sites; every violation site had edges off the 96-dbu grid (seed, reference-design-verified).
- V3.M4.AUX.2: the V3 cut's y-extent is 96 dbu = 24 nm (via-cell geometry: the cut is 72x96); V3.W.1 separately requires a minimum V3 width of 18 nm along the M4 length; after repair, M4 y-height is also 96 dbu in every cleared case (seed, reference-design-verified).

Recipe -- M4 reshape to on-grid height + coordinated via repositioning:

Step 1. For each off-grid M4 polygon, choose the target y for the new M4 CENTER. Two grid constraints apply simultaneously, confirmed at all seven Family-B sites (seed, reference-design-verified):
  (1a) EDGE grid: both new y-edges on the 96-dbu grid; with a 96-dbu height the center is at k*96 + 48.
  (1b) TRACK grid: the center on a legal horizontal routing track; all legal track centers satisfy y mod 192 == 48 -- every k*96+48 with y mod 192 == 144 is an illegal track even though its edges land on the 96-dbu grid.
  Table of all seven Family-B sites (seed, reference-design-verified):
