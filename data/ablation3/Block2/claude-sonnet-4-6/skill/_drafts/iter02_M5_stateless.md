Trial trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01 applied three `resize_via_shape` operations—two on V4 and one on M4, all axis x, delta +152 dbu—inside cell `VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58`, reducing total violations from 68 to 52 (−16). The operation group label "V4M5_fix" confirms this pattern targets V4/M5 interaction rules (V4.M5.EN.2, V4.M5.AUX.2). When a via cell is used to carry M5 connectivity, the correct repair point for V4.M5.EN.2 and V4.M5.AUX.2 violations is the via cell definition itself, not individual M5 polygon edits.

The +152 dbu x-axis expansion of V4 in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01 is consistent with bringing V4 into compliance with V4.M5.AUX.2 (V4 must exactly match M5 width perpendicular to M5 length) and V4.M5.EN.2 (≥11 nm M5 enclosure on two opposite sides in x). A delta smaller than 22 dbu (2 × 11 nm enclosure) would be insufficient for both rules simultaneously; 152 dbu provides substantial margin and was verified clean.

## M5 Polygon Horizontal Extension (resize_end, high end)

Extending M5 polygon right ends (axis x, end high) by a positive delta while simultaneously moving associated via instances in +x preserves M5 track coverage and introduces zero new M5 violations. Trial trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00 applied `resize_end` (axis x, end high) to eight M5 polygons (p958–p965) each by +32 dbu, and to p1065 by +116 dbu and p1036 by +80 dbu, alongside `move_instance` operations shifting groups of via instances by [32, 0] dbu or [72, 0] dbu in x. The gate decision was `gated_in` with `n_new_in_crop: 0` and `n_new_out_of_crop: 0`, confirming no new violations. Connectivity was preserved (`conn_preserved: true`).

The 32 dbu horizontal extension increment aligns with M5.AUX.1 (M5 vertical edges must lie on a 24 nm grid): 32 dbu is not a multiple of 24 dbu, so it produces a valid grid position only when applied to a polygon whose existing edge is already on a grid-aligned coordinate that, after the shift, lands on 24 dbu modulus. Verify that both the original and resulting edge x-coordinates satisfy `x % 24 == 0` before applying any `resize_end` delta; trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00 passed this check for all modified polygons.

The +116 dbu and +80 dbu deltas on p1065 and p1036 in trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00, combined with instance moves of +72 dbu x, indicate that polygons serving instances displaced further in x require proportionally larger end extensions. The extension must at minimum cover the instance displacement so that via enclosure rules (V4.M5.EN.2, V5.M5.EN.1) remain satisfied after the move.

## M5 Vertical Position Adjustments

M5 polygons may be moved in y (±48, ±72, ±96 dbu) in conjunction with x-axis resizes without violating M5.W.5 (min vertical width 44 nm) or M5.S.2 (min vertical spacing 40 nm), provided the overall layout coordinates remain valid. Trial trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00 applied y-moves to eight M5 polygons: p964 (−48 dbu), p963 (−96 dbu), p962 (+48 dbu), p961 (+72 dbu), p960 (−72 dbu), p959 (+72 dbu), p958 (−72 dbu), each paired with a +32 dbu x resize_end. All moves were gated in with zero new violations. The 48 and 96 dbu y increments correspond to one and two units of vertical track pitch (48 nm), and the ±72 dbu moves span 1.5 units. None of these y-moves violated M5.S.2 (40 nm vertical spacing) or M5.W.5 (44 nm vertical width), confirming that the layout's pre-existing vertical clearances were sufficient to absorb these shifts.

M5 polygons p937 and p938 in trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00 were moved +32 dbu in x only (no y move, no resize_end). This establishes that a pure x-translation of an M5 polygon—without changing its width—is a valid operation when the destination coordinates satisfy M5.AUX.1 (24 nm x-grid) and M5.AUX.2 (min-width tracks on routing track centers at 192 dbu pitch, 48 dbu offset). The fact that no M5.AUX.1 or M5.AUX.2 violation was introduced for p937/p938 confirms that both polygons' original and shifted x-coordinates were consistent with these constraints.

## M5.AUX.2 Track Grid Compliance

M5.AUX.2 requires minimum-width M5 tracks to lie on vertical routing tracks at a pitch of 192 dbu with a 48 dbu offset from origin (i.e., valid centerline x-coordinates satisfy `(cl − 48) % 192 == 0`). The horizontal instance displacement of +32 dbu used in trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00 for most via groups is not itself a multiple of 192 dbu; instead, it shifts all associated geometry uniformly so that the M5 polygon centerlines, after extension, remain on valid tracks. When planning horizontal shifts to repair V4.M5 violations, ensure the resulting M5 polygon centerlines (midpoint of left and right x-edges) satisfy the AUX.2 modular constraint; trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00 confirms that +32 dbu shifts are track-grid-safe in at least one layout context.

## M5.AUX.3 and M5.AUX.4 Non-Bending and Wide-Polygon Constraints

No operations in either trial created new M5 corners or caused M5 polygons to bend (M5.AUX.3). All resize and move operations in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.01 and trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00 were applied to straight, axis-aligned M5 segments, and neither trial introduced M5.AUX.3 violations. Similarly, no M5.AUX.4 violations (wide M5 polygon outside edge touching a routing track edge) were generated, indicating that the wide-polygon boundaries were not shifted to coincide with routing track edges by these operations.
