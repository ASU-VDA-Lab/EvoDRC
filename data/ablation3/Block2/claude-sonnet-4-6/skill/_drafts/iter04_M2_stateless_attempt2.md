## Via Enclosure Rules and the resize_via_shape Approach

Rule V2.M2.EN.1 requires that V2 be enclosed by M2 on at least two opposite sides by 5 nm; rule V1.M2.EN.2 requires V1 enclosure by M2 of 5 nm on two opposite sides (the complementary side may be 0 nm). Rule V1.M2.AUX.2 additionally requires that V1 share the same width as M2 in the direction perpendicular to the M2 length, enforced by checking that at least two V1 edges are coincident with M2 edges. The `resize_via_shape` on M2 inside `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00) adjusted M2 shape_index 0 on the y-axis alongside matching V2 resizes on the same axis, and the combined set yielded a net −8 violation reduction. This demonstrates that growing the M2 landing pad within the via cell simultaneously satisfies the enclosure margin required by V2.M2.EN.1 and can resolve multiple violations in a single cell instantiation when the cell is used repeatedly across the design.

## Rule M2.S.7: Tip-to-Tip and Side-to-Side Proximity Interaction

Rule M2.S.7 prohibits the co-occurrence of an 18 nm tip-to-tip gap with a side-to-side spacing ≤ 32 nm on adjacent wires; it further requires that parallel run length be ≥ 35 nm whenever side spacing is ≤ 32 nm. The pattern of multiple coordinated `resize_end` operations in trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00 — extending the high-x end of eight M2 polygons while simultaneously moving associated instances — is consistent with increasing the parallel run length or eliminating the short tip exposure that triggers M2.S.7. The zero new-violation outcome in trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00, trial:i03.ug.whole_design.00, and trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00 confirms that extending M2 ends by multiples of the grid step (32, 72, 80, 84 dbu) does not introduce M2.S.7 violations when the extension is coordinated with instance movements.

## Rule M2.S.8: Diagonal Gap Spacing

Rule M2.S.8 forbids diagonal center-to-center distances less than 80 nm between 18 nm tip-to-tip gaps on different M2 tracks. The gap center is computed by shrinking each gap polygon by 8.5 nm per side, so any operation that closes or relocates an 18 nm tip-to-tip gap also moves or eliminates its effective center. The `resize_end` operations in trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00, trial:i03.ug.whole_design.00, and trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00 produced no M2.S.8 violations, consistent with the extensions either eliminating the tip-to-tip condition entirely or pushing gap centers beyond the 80 nm euclidean threshold.

## Spacing and Width Floor Constraints

Rule M2.W.1 sets a minimum M2 width of 18 nm. Rule M2.S.1 sets a minimum side-to-side spacing of 18 nm for edges longer than 36 nm. Rule M2.S.2 sets a 25 nm minimum between a tip edge (≤ 36 nm) and a side edge (> 36 nm). Rules M2.S.3, M2.S.4, and M2.S.5 govern tip-to-tip spacing: 27 nm when both tips are 24–36 nm (M2.S.3), 31 nm when both tips are < 24 nm (M2.S.4), and 31 nm when one tip is 24–36 nm and the other is < 24 nm (M2.S.5). Rule M2.S.6 enforces a 20 nm euclidean corner-to-corner minimum. Rule M2.A.1 requires a minimum enclosed area of 504 nm². The `resize_end` operations in trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00, trial:i03.ug.whole_design.00, and trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00 did not violate any of these rules, confirming that end-extension by at least 32 dbu on existing wires of legal width does not produce width, spacing, or area infractions in this design context.
