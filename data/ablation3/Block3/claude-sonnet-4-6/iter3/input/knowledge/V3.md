### Family B: M4 on-grid reshape and V3 height matching (M4.AUX.1, V3.M4.AUX.2)

These two rules co-fired on the same M4 polygons in the reference block (seed, reference-design-verified). In the reference design, all 7 M4.AUX.1 violations and all 7 V3.M4.AUX.2 violations cleared from the same 7 M4 polygons. An eighth M4 reshape at center 432 belongs to Family D (-> see M4.md) and sits on a legal track (seed, reference-design-verified).

Family B in three verified facts:
1. Seven M4 polygons were reshaped or narrowed (seed, reference-design-verified).
2. Six of the seven then needed a VIA34 y-move to the new M4 center; the
   seventh (center 2160) was already on a legal track, so its VIA34 stayed --
   but the M4 itself was STILL reshaped (y-height 184 -> 96 with the center
   fixed at 2160, plus x-narrowing); its VIA45 moved in x only, following M5.
3. Every repaired M4 center satisfies y mod 192 == 48; all seven Family-B sites
   confirm that both the edge-grid constraint and the track-parity constraint are
   satisfied together at the same center coordinate (seed, reference-design-verified).

Rule semantics:
- M4.AUX.1: M4 horizontal edges that are not on the 96-dbu grid produce
  violations; worked example B1 before repair has y_bottom=988 (988 mod 96 = 28)
  and y_top=1172 (1172 mod 96 = 20), both off-grid; after repair y_bottom=1152
  (12*96) and y_top=1248 (13*96), both on-grid, with no residual error (seed, reference-design-verified).
- V3.M4.AUX.2: V3 y-extent must exactly equal M4 y-extent (both perpendicular to
  M4 length, which runs in x). The V3 cut's y-extent is 96 dbu = 24 nm
  (via-cell geometry: the cut is 72x96); the deck's V3.W.1 separately requires
  a minimum V3 width of 18 nm along the M4 length. After repair, M4 y-height is
  also 96 dbu (seed, reference-design-verified).

Recipe -- M4 reshape to on-grid height + coordinated via repositioning:

Step 1. For each off-grid M4 polygon, choose the target y for the new M4
  CENTER. Two grid constraints stack:
  (1a) EDGE grid (M4.AUX.1): both new y-edges on the 96-dbu grid; with a
       96-dbu height the center is at k*96 + 48 -- confirmed by all seven
       repaired M4 polygons whose bottom edges equal multiples of 96 and whose
       top edges equal the bottom plus 96 (seed, reference-design-verified).
  (1b) TRACK grid (M4.AUX.2): legal track centers satisfy y mod 192 == 48;
       the k*96+48 candidate with y mod 192 == 144 is off-parity: the 1080-site
       nearest-legal candidate at 1104 satisfies 1104 mod 96 == 0, center 1104
       would be (1104-48)=1056 and (1104+48)=1152, but 1104 mod 192 == 144 --
       that candidate was NOT chosen; the selected center 1200 satisfies
       1200 mod 192 == 48; every one of the seven final centers satisfies y mod
       192 == 48 (seed, reference-design-verified).

  Measured centers (old center = old VIA34 y; new center verified at each site):

    old center | new center | VIA34 dy | VIA45 (dx, dy)
    -----------|------------|----------|----------------
       1080    |    1200    |   +120   | (-88, +120)  <- NOT the nearest legal
       2160    |    2160    |     0    | (-88,   0)   <- center already legal;
                                                         M4 still reshaped
                                                         184->96 + x-narrowed
       3240    |    3312    |    +72   | (-88,  +72)
       4320    |    4272    |    -48   | (-88,  -48)
       5400    |    5424    |    +24   | (-88,  +24)
       6480    |    6576    |    +96   | (-88,  +96)  <- distance tie, upward
       7560    |    7536    |    -24   | (-88,  -24)

  The DEFAULT candidate is the NEAREST center with y mod 192 == 48; that default
  matches 6 of the 7 sites above. The single exception is the 1080-site, which
  resolved to 1200 (+120) rather than 1008 (-72): among legal tracks the nearest
  can conflict with other geometry; the next track at +/-192 from the nearest
  legal candidate is the fallback, applied when the nearest produces a new
  violation in preview (seed, reference-design-verified).

Step 2. Derive the edges from the chosen center:
  y_bottom_new = center_y - 48, y_top_new = center_y + 48
