## M2 end-cap extension as the primary V1.M2.EN.2 repair action

V1.M2.EN.2 requires M2 to enclose V1 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides (or 5 nm and 0 nm). When the enclosure on the M2-length axis (x-axis) is insufficient, extending the M2 polygon's high-x end via `resize_end` on `axis:x`, `end:"high"` is a confirmed valid repair. In trial:i03.ug.whole_design.00, 16 M2 polygons received high-x-end extensions ranging from 84 dbu to 192 dbu, and the combined result introduced zero new V1 violations (n_new_in_crop: 0, n_new_out_of_crop: 0). Extending only the M2 length direction does not disturb V1.M2.AUX.2 compliance, because that rule checks V1 width against M2 width in the direction perpendicular to M2 length (the y-axis), which is unaffected by x-axis resize operations — confirmed in trial:i03.ug.whole_design.00 where no V1.M2.AUX.2 violations appeared after 16 such extensions.

In trial:i03.ug.whole_design.00, 20 instance move operations in the +x direction (64 dbu for 17 instances, 136 dbu for 5 instances) were applied alongside the M2 end-cap extensions.
