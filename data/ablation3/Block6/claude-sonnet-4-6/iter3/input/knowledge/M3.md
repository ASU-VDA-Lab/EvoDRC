## V2.M3.EN.2 -- Enclosure of V2 by M3

Rule V2.M3.EN.2 requires M3 to enclose V2 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides (5&5 nm or 5&0 nm under the projection check). Via cell template edits that widen V2 shapes in x satisfied this rule without modifying M3 polygons directly: reshaping V2 shapes in cell VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 (moving shape_index 0 by -144 dbu in x, resizing shapes 0, 1, and 2 by +288 dbu in x, and moving shape_index 2 by +144 dbu in x) reduced total design violations by 78, from 159 to 81 in the whole_design window (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). That trial listed M3 in its touched_layers, confirming that M3 enclosure geometry is coupled to the V2 via cell definition width even when no M3 polygon is edited directly.

Moving M3 polygons asymmetrically in x without repositioning the via centroid introduces new V2.M3.EN.2 violations: moving p1682 by -16 dbu, p1683 by +32 dbu, and p1685 by +32 dbu as part of the whole-design unit_gate pass produced 10 new V2.M3.EN.2 violations in the design crop (trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00). That trial was accepted with decision gated_in and conn_preserved, meaning the move set achieved a net benefit elsewhere, but the V2.M3.EN.2 regression is a confirmed side effect of asymmetric M3 displacement when the displaced polygon reduces the enclosure margin below 5 nm on one projection side of a via.

## M3 Resize-End Operations and V2.M3.EN.2 Interaction

Resize-end operations on M3 polygons were applied to p1831 (high-x end +20 dbu, low-y end +24 dbu), p1786 (high-x end +20 dbu, high-y end +72 dbu), and p1806 (high-x end +20 dbu, high-y end +24 dbu) in the same move set that produced 10 new V2.M3.EN.2 violations (trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00). Extending the high-x end of an M3 shape by 20 dbu does not automatically preserve V2 enclosure on all projection sides when the via instance is not shifted simultaneously; the 10 new violations confirm that end-growth operations must be verified against the enclosure margin on the opposite projection edge of any via the polygon partially covers.

## Via Template Channel vs. Direct M3 Edit for Enclosure Fixes

Prefer the cu_pool via-template channel over direct M3 polygon moves when resolving V2.M3.EN.2 violations at scale: a single via cell template fix in VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 eliminated 78 violations across the full design in one applied operation (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00), whereas the unit_gate M3 polygon moves in the same iteration introduced 10 new V2.M3.EN.2 violations even while the trial was accepted overall (trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00). When the same via cell is instantiated many times, a single template-level V2 width correction propagates to all instances and avoids per-instance M3 enclosure regressions.
