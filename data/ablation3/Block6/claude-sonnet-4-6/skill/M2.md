## V1.M2.EN.2 and V1.M2.AUX.2: Paired M2 End-Extension with Instance Translation

Trials i03.ug.whole_design.00, i04.ug.whole_design.00, and i05.ug.whole_design.00 all touch both M2 and V1, and all three were accepted with conn_preserved=true and n_new_in_crop=0. In trial:i03.ug.whole_design.00, seven M2 polygon high-x ends were each extended (resize_end, axis=x, end=high) by either +116 dbu (polygons p2020, p1946, p1903, p1991) or +152 dbu (polygons p2071, p2072, p2086), while the paired instances were translated +96 dbu in x. No low-x end was trimmed in the same commit. Use resize_end on the far (translation-direction) tip rather than a bilateral resize when an instance moves in one axis direction, to avoid shortening the opposite tip into a new M2.S.2 or M2.S.4 violation (trial:i03.ug.whole_design.00).
