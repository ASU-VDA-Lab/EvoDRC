## Via-cell metal resize on adjacent layers propagates to M4 DRC

In trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.00, a single resize of VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58 on M5 by −88 dbu (y-axis) cleared 56 total violations while M4 was listed as a touched layer. V4.M4.EN.1 requires 11 nm enclosure of V4 by M4 on at least two opposite sides; an over-tall via-cell metal pad on M5 forces a compensating M4 geometry that violates V4.M4.EN.1 on the opposing end. Shrinking the via-cell metal in y from inside the cell definition resolved the violation without any direct M4 polygon edit. When V4.M4.EN.1 or V3.M4.EN.2 violations appear on a VIA cell instance, apply the fix inside the via cell definition rather than stretching the flat M4 shape, as confirmed by trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.00.

## M4 polygon x-end resizes must be balanced; ±20 dbu confirmed safe

In trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00, polygons p1831, p1786, and p1806 each had their high-x end extended by +20 dbu. In trial:i05.ug.whole_design.00, the same three polygons had their high-x end retracted by −20 dbu. Both operations gated in with zero new M4 violations. The +20 dbu extension remained within M4.W.5 (minimum horizontal width 44 nm) and M4.S.2 (minimum horizontal spacing 40 nm) tolerances, and the intermediate extended state was stable across one full iteration. Do not extend a M4 polygon's x-end beyond an amount that can later be retracted without triggering M4.S.2 or M4.W.5 on neighboring shapes; the safe net magnitude observed in these two trials is ±20 dbu.

## Instance y-displacement must be multiples of 24 dbu to comply with M4.AUX.1

Trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00 moved all via and M4-touching instances by y-deltas drawn from {0, ±24, ±48, ±72, ±96} dbu — all multiples of 24 dbu. Trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00 and trial:i05.ug.whole_design.00 likewise used only y-deltas in that same set. All three trials gated in with zero new M4 violations. M4.AUX.1 requires M4 horizontal edges to lie on a 24 nm grid; non-multiple-of-24 y-displacements applied to instances that carry M4 shapes move those edges off-grid and fire M4.AUX.1. Always snap instance y-moves to 24 dbu increments.

## M4.AUX.2 track alignment is preserved automatically by 24 dbu y-move steps

M4.AUX.2 places minimum-width M4 tracks at y = 48 + 192×N dbu. Because 192 = 8×24, any y-displacement that is a multiple of 24 dbu maps every valid track center to another valid position modulo 192. The uniform use of 24 dbu multiples for all instance y-moves across trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00, trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00, and trial:i05.ug.whole_design.00 confirms this property held and no M4.AUX.2 violations were introduced in any of those trials. Moves in 24 dbu y-steps are sufficient to maintain M4.AUX.2 compliance without additional per-track verification.

## M4 x-moves up to ±96 dbu and fractional offsets (13, 37, 48, 80 dbu) are individually safe

Trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00 shifted instances and M4 polygons by ±96 dbu and +80 dbu on the x-axis with no new M4 violations. Trial:i05.ug.whole_design.00 moved M4 polygons by +13 dbu (p2072, p1903, p2020, p1946, p2071, p2086, p2019), +37 dbu (p2050, p1911, p2049, p1914, p1907, p2046, p1927, p1910, p2067, p2030), +48 dbu (p2047), and −72 dbu (p1999). All gated in under M4.S.2 (minimum horizontal spacing 40 nm) and M4.W.5 (minimum horizontal width 44 nm). The fractional offsets 13 and 37 dbu are not multiples of 44 nm but are legal because M4.W.5 is a per-polygon minimum-width check, not a fixed x-pitch constraint. Do not assume M4 x-positions must be on a fixed horizontal grid; the binding constraints are per-shape horizontal width (≥44 nm) and per-pair horizontal edge spacing (≥40 nm), both confirmed safe across trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00 and trial:i05.ug.whole_design.00.

## M4.AUX.3 bend prohibition requires axis-separated resize ops

M4.AUX.3 forbids bends (corners with interior angles 0–90°). All resize operations across trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00, trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00, and trial:i05.ug.whole_design.00 used resize_end and move operations that specify a single axis per op (distinct "axis":"x" and "axis":"y" fields), preserving axis alignment of each edge. No new M4.AUX.3 violations were introduced in any of those trials. When resizing M4 polygon endpoints, move only one axis per operation; do not combine x and y displacement into a single diagonal op on a shared endpoint, as that creates a non-orthogonal edge and fires M4.AUX.3.

## V3.M4.AUX.2 width matching survives simultaneous instance and polygon moves

V3.M4.AUX.2 requires V3 to exactly match M4 width along the direction perpendicular to M4's length. Trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00 touched both V3 and M4 via 69 mixed instance-move and polygon-resize ops, and zero new violations of V3.M4.AUX.2 were introduced. Resizing M4 horizontally (x-axis resize_end on p1831, p1786, p1806, each +20 dbu) while simultaneously moving via instances maintains V3.M4.AUX.2 compliance provided via cell dimensions already match the starting M4 width and the resize does not alter the M4 dimension perpendicular to V3's orientation. Never resize M4 in the direction perpendicular to an enclosed V3 without a corresponding update to the V3 shape; trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00 confirms that horizontal M4 end extensions (parallel to the M4 length) leave V3.M4.AUX.2 unaffected.

## M4.W.3 and M4.W.4 forbidden vertical widths: avoid direct M4 y-resizes

M4.W.3 forbids vertical widths that are even integer multiples of 24 nm (48, 96, 144, 192, 240, 288, 336, 384, 432, 480 nm). M4.W.4 additionally forbids widths 72, 168, 264, 360, and 456 nm. No direct M4 y-axis resize operations were applied across trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00, trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00, or trial:i05.ug.whole_design.00; all M4 vertical dimension changes were inherited indirectly through the via-cell shrink in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA45_1_2_58_58.00 (which operated on M5). The complete absence of direct M4 y-resize ops in three consecutive gated-in unit_gate trials reflects a repair strategy that avoids touching M4 vertical widths in the flat layer, consistent with the large number of forbidden widths in M4.W.3 and M4.W.4. When a vertical width change on M4 is unavoidable, compute the target width explicitly and confirm it is not equal to 48×k nm for any positive integer k, and not in the set {72, 168, 264, 360, 456} nm, before applying.

## M4.S.1 vertical spacing and M4.AUX.1 grid share the same 24 dbu pitch: treat them jointly

M4.S.1 sets a 24 nm minimum vertical spacing between M4 polygon edges. M4.AUX.1 sets a 24 nm grid for all M4 horizontal edges. Both constraints operate at the same 24 dbu increment. In trial:i02.ug.whole_design.00, trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00, and trial:i05.ug.whole_design.00, all y-moves were multiples of 24 dbu, ensuring M4 shapes remained on-grid and inter-shape vertical gaps were preserved at valid multiples of 24 nm. A fractional y-displacement on an M4 shape is simultaneously an M4.AUX.1 off-grid violation and a potential M4.S.1 spacing underrun; because both rules fire at the same grid, a single 24 dbu alignment check covers both.

## M4.S.5 parallel run length (≥44 nm) is not impacted by x-moves of 13–37 dbu

M4.S.5 requires a minimum parallel run length of 44 nm between adjacent-track M4 polygons. Trial:i05.ug.whole_design.00 moved multiple M4 polygons by +13 dbu and +37 dbu on the x-axis, and gated in with zero new violations. These small x-displacements shift polygon endpoints without reducing run length below 44 nm on shapes that already satisfy the rule, confirming that incremental x-adjustments of this magnitude do not trigger M4.S.5 in this design context. Before applying any x-move that could shorten a shared parallel edge, verify the post-move overlap length remains ≥44 nm; trial:i05.ug.whole_design.00 confirms moves up to 37 dbu are safe when the pre-move run length is sufficiently above the minimum.
