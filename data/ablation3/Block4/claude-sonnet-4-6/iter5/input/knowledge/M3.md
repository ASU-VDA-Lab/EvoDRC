## M3 End-Extension Repair via resize_end

The unit_gate channel resolves M3 spacing and V3 enclosure violations by extending or retracting the ends of M3 wire polygons. In trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00, three resize_end operations on M3 polygons were applied: polygon p1374 extended +140 dbu at its high-x end, p1395 extended +172 dbu at its high-x end, and p1382 retracted 176 dbu at its low-x end. All three operations passed the full DRC check with zero new violations inside or outside the crop window and with connectivity preserved.

The range of end-extension deltas in trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00 (140-176 dbu in x) spans the critical edge-length thresholds that determine which spacing rule applies to the modified edge under M3.S.1 through M3.S.5:

- Side edges (length > 36 nm) are governed by M3.S.1 (18 nm side-to-side spacing).
- Wide tip edges (24 nm <= length <= 36 nm) are governed by M3.S.3 (27 nm tip-to-tip) and M3.S.5 (31 nm wide-tip-to-narrow-tip).
- Narrow tip edges (length < 24 nm) are governed by M3.S.4 (31 nm tip-to-tip) and M3.S.5.
- Any tip edge (length <= 36 nm) facing a side edge (length > 36 nm) falls under M3.S.2 (25 nm tip-to-side).

Extending an M3 end past the 36 nm boundary reclassifies the edge from tip to side and relaxes the applicable minimum spacing from 25-31 nm to 18 nm. Retracting an M3 end below 24 nm reclassifies it from wide-tip to narrow-tip and tightens the tip-to-tip and tip-to-side requirements. Trial:i04.ug.whole_design.00 confirmed that end-extension operations in the 140-176 dbu range applied alongside instance moves produce a clean DRC result on M3 when the geometry satisfies the post-move spacing rules.
