## Repair Operation Patterns

All 18 trials across iterations 1–4 used exclusively x-axis operations. Every `move_instance` delta across those iterations is either a vector of the form `[dx, 0]` with `dy = 0` or a scalar with `axis: "x"` explicitly set, and every `resize_end` specifies `axis: "x"` targeting either the `"high"` or `"low"` end (trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row3.03, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row4.04, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row5.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0020.10, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0031.11, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.01, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0004.03).

In iteration 5, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04 included a `move` op with `axis: "y"`, `delta_dbu: -96` on polygon `p1561` (cell `VIA_VIA56_2_2_66_58`, layer M6). This op appeared in `ops` but was dropped by the assembler under `assemble_drops` with reason `cu_pool:rejected_net_positive`. The y-axis op was not applied to the design. Do not issue y-axis displacements for V1, M1, or M2 geometry; the measured record confirms that y-axis ops submitted to the assembler are rejected as net-positive by the cu_pool and have no effect on the repair outcome (trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04).

The scalar `move_instance` form (`axis: "x"`, scalar `delta`) is confirmed alongside the vector form (`delta_dbu: [dx, 0]`) in trial:i04.ug.leaf_0004.03. Both forms target the x-axis and are accepted by the harness.

The symmetric `resize` operation (no `end` field) was used once, in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0031.11, and introduced 4 new violations in crop. Every other resize operation uses `resize_end` with an explicit `"high"` or `"low"` end specifier and avoids this outcome (trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row3.03, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row5.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0020.10, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.01). ## Movement Magnitude and Connectivity Risk

The minimum delta for a clean (zero new violation, connectivity preserved) repair across all measured accepted trials is 36 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row10.01, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row3.03, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row4.04, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row5.05, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row9.08, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0004.09, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00). Larger single-step deltas of 52, 72, 92, and 108 dbu also appear in accepted clean trials (trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row8.07). A sub-36 dbu delta of 4 dbu was issued in trial:i04.ug.leaf_0004.03 and produced 80 new in-crop violations. Do not apply displacements smaller than 36 dbu; the measured record confirms that sub-36 dbu moves introduce extreme violation counts.

A 72 dbu delta is not universally safe: trial:i05.ug.leaf_0001.00 moved inst_id `i0453` at `[72, 0]` and broke connectivity, producing the only `gated_out` result in the record. The 36 dbu move on the same instance in trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.00 was clean. Large deltas can sever connections even when they produce zero new DRC violations; verify connectivity sensitivity on persistent recurrence units before escalating delta magnitude.

In iteration 3, the dominant delta is 72 dbu (trial:i03.ug.Block1_union_row3.00, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.01, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0005.02), doubling the base 36 dbu step used heavily in iteration 1. Units that persisted to iteration 3 received larger per-instance displacements than they had in earlier iterations. Escalating magnitude is not a reliable strategy past the first persistence: leaf_0001 was broken by 72 dbu in iteration 5 after a clean 36 dbu repair in iteration 4.
