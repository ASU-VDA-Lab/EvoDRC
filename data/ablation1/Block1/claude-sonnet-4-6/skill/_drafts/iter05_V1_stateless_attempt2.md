## Rule Summary

**V1.W.1** sets a minimum V1 width of 18 nm along the M2 length direction. **V1.S.1** sets minimum projection-measured spacing between V1 masks: 18 nm on the same M2 track, 27 nm between non-aligned parallel tracks, and 18 nm between aligned parallel tracks. **V1.S.2** sets a minimum Euclidean corner-to-corner spacing of 23 nm (16.4 nm check distance) for two via instances both classified as with-end-cap (WEC). **V1.S.3** sets a minimum Euclidean corner-to-corner spacing of 30 nm (16.12 nm check) for two no-end-cap (NEC) via instances. **V1.S.4** sets a minimum Euclidean corner-to-corner spacing of 27 nm (17.11 nm check) between one WEC and one NEC via instance. **V1.M1.EN.1** requires M1 to enclose V1 by at least 5 nm on one pair of opposite sides and 2 nm on the other pair. **V1.M2.EN.2** requires M2 to enclose V1 by 5 nm on one axis and either 5 nm or 0 nm on the other, with no partial enclosure allowed. **V1.AUX.1** requires V1 to reside inside both M1 and M2. **V1.M2.AUX.2** requires V1 width in the direction perpendicular to M2 length to match M2 width exactly. The GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL rule fires on any V1 edge whose angle falls outside 0° and 90°.

## WEC vs NEC Classification

The spacing rules V1.S.1 through V1.S.4 depend on whether each V1 instance is classified as WEC or NEC. A V1 is NEC when all its edges coincide with M2 edges (the via is flush with M2 on every side). A V1 is WEC when at least one edge is not coincident with M2, receiving a 5 nm end-cap extension along M2 in the mask computation. The distinction directly controls which spacing threshold applies between any pair of vias.

## V1.M1.EN.1 Violations Observed in Trial History

In trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04, a batch of four move_instance operations (each [36, 0] dbu in x) combined with one polygon y-shift generated 24 new in-crop DRC violations, of which 15 were attributed to V1.M1.EN.1. The trial was accepted because connectivity was preserved across all nets. This is the only trial in this history with a per-rule breakdown attributing violations directly to a V1 rule. The V1.M1.EN.1 checker isolates horizontal (0°) and vertical (90°) V1 edges separately: it requires that on at least one horizontal axis M1 encloses V1 by 5 nm with no horizontal V1 edge failing the 5 nm check, and by 2 nm on the complementary horizontal side, and applies the same condition independently on the vertical axis. Avoid instance move combinations that shift M1 away from V1 without simultaneously verifying residual enclosure on both the 5 nm and 2 nm sides (trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04).

## Connectivity as the Primary Gating Criterion

Every rejected trial in this history failed on connectivity, not on DRC count. Trial:i05.ug.leaf_0001.00 is the sole rejected trial and its decision was `gated_out` with reason `conn_broken`. By contrast, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04 introduced 24 new in-crop violations including 15 V1.M1.EN.1 violations and was still accepted because connectivity was preserved. Operations that reduce DRC violations while breaking net connectivity are rejected regardless of their geometric correctness. Always confirm that any move, resize, or via substitution that touches V1 preserves connectivity across all nets in the crop before committing (trial:i05.ug.leaf_0001.00, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0005.04).

## Multi-Layer Coupling

Every trial in this history that touched V1 also touched M1 and M2 simultaneously. No V1-only edit has been observed. Because V1.AUX.1 requires V1 to be inside both M1 and M2, and V1.M2.AUX.2 requires V1 width to match M2 width exactly in the direction perpendicular to M2 length, edits to V1 position or size require co-adjustment of the enclosing M1 and M2 geometries. The resize operations on M2 polygons observed in trial:i01.ug.Block1_union_row1.00 and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.01 directly address V1.M2.EN.2 and V1.M2.AUX.2 compliance for their associated V1 shapes.
