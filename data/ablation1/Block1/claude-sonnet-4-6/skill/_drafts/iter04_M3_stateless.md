## Resize and Move Operations on M3: What the Measured Trials Show

### Via-shape resize on M3 (VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36, y-axis, -40 dbu) is rejected by cu_pool

The cu_pool channel rejected the resize_via_shape operation targeting the M3 shape inside cell `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` with `delta_dbu=-40` on the y-axis (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). The rejection reason was `rejected_net_positive`: delta_total=0 across both measured windows (leaf_0014: 175 violations before and after; leaf_0015: 113 violations before and after). Because this operation touches M2, M3, and V2 simultaneously yet produces no net reduction, do not apply a y-axis shrink of -40 dbu to the M3 shape in `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` through the cu_pool channel.

The same operation was consequently dropped from the unit_gate assembly for leaf_0014 (assemble_drops in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.05, reason: `cu_pool:rejected_net_positive`). An operation rejected by cu_pool is excluded from subsequent unit_gate assembly; redundant resubmission of this resize_via_shape through unit_gate will not succeed.

### Bulk y-axis resize of M3 polygons (-64 dbu) is connectivity-preserving but introduces cross-layer violations

Twelve simultaneous y-axis resize operations on M3 polygons, each at delta_dbu=-64, were accepted by unit_gate as `gated_in` with `conn_preserved=true` (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.05). The operation introduced 82 new in-crop violations, all on layers other than M3: M1.A.1 (39 violations), V1.M1.EN.1 (38 violations), M4.W.5 (3 violations), M4.S.4 (1 violation), M4.S.5 (1 violation). No new M3 violations were generated by this resize batch, and no out-of-crop violations were introduced.

The y-axis shrink of M3 polygons is not itself a source of M3 rule violations at the -64 dbu magnitude measured in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.05, but it propagates violation count into M1, V1, and M4. Any repair sequence that shrinks M3 in y must account for downstream impact on those layers.

### Instance move touching M3 (-36 dbu x-axis) introduces zero new violations

Moving instance `i0452` by delta_dbu=[-36, 0] (x-axis only) was accepted as `gated_in` in iteration 4 (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01). The touched layers are M2, M3, and V2; the result was n_new_in_crop=0 and n_new_out_of_crop=0. This instance-level x-move produces a clean state change with no new violations on any rule, including all M3 rules (M3.W.1, M3.S.1–M3.S.6, M3.A.1, V2.M3.EN.2, V2.M3.AUX.2, V3.M3.EN.1).

### V2 enclosure and via-shape sizing

The V2.M3.EN.2 rule requires M3 to enclose V2 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides (either 5&5 or 5&0 on horizontal and vertical pairs). V2.M3.AUX.2 requires the V2 width along the axis perpendicular to M3 length to exactly match the M3 width in that direction. The rejected resize_via_shape in trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 touched V2 along with M3; because the operation produced delta_total=0, shrinking the M3 via-shape in y by 40 dbu inside `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` does not resolve V2.M3.EN.2 or V2.M3.AUX.2 violations in that cell under the conditions measured at iteration 2.

### M3 spacing rule reference values (from rules, applied to measured context)

The measured trials do not directly exercise M3 spacing or width violations; all new violations from M3 operations in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.05 landed on M1, V1, and M4. The relevant M3 thresholds for future operations are: minimum width 18 nm (M3.W.1); side-to-side spacing 18 nm for edges >36 nm (M3.S.1); tip-to-side 25 nm when tip edge ≤36 nm and side edge >36 nm (M3.S.2); tip-to-tip 27 nm for both edges 24–36 nm (M3.S.3); tip-to-tip 31 nm when one edge is 24–36 nm and the other is <24 nm (M3.S.5); tip-to-tip 31 nm for both edges <24 nm (M3.S.4); corner-to-corner 20 nm euclidean (M3.S.6); minimum area 504 nm² (M3.A.1). The -64 dbu y-resize in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.05 did not trigger any of these rules, confirming that the resized shapes remained above minimum width, area, and spacing thresholds after the shrink.
