## Via Enclosure Constraints

V2.M3.EN.2 requires M3 to enclose V2 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides (5 & 5 or 5 & 0 pattern). V2.M3.AUX.2 additionally requires that V2 width perpendicular to the M3 length direction exactly matches the M3 width along that direction. Any y-axis resize of M3 polygons hosting V2 vias risks violating V2.M3.EN.2 on the shrunk edge. In trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, the `resize_via_shape` operation on M3 (axis `y`, `-40 dbu`) touched layers M2, M3, and V2 simultaneously and produced zero net violation change, indicating the enclosure headroom was not sufficient to convert violations after shrink. Avoid y-direction M3 shrinks near V2 vias when the available enclosure margin on the shrunk edge is at or near the 5 nm minimum (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00).

X-axis instance moves on cells spanning M2, M3, and V2 do not disturb the via enclosure relationship when the via moves with the instance as a rigid unit. Both trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01 (delta `[-36, 0]` dbu, instance i0452) and trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01 (delta `[+36, 0]` dbu, instance i0451) produced zero new V2.M3.EN.2 or V2.M3.AUX.2 violations (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01).

V3.M3.EN.1 requires M3 to enclose V3 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides. The measured trials do not record V3.M3.EN.1 violations as a result of M3 ops; this rule has no repair evidence in the current history.

## Channel and Assembly Behavior

The unit_gate and cu_pool channels evaluate the same ops under different criteria. In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.05, the twelve M3 resize polygon ops cleared unit_gate on the `conn_preserved` basis despite adding 82 foreign-layer violations. The `resize_via_shape` on M3 was excluded from that assembly (dropped, reason: `cu_pool:rejected_net_positive`) before the unit_gate decision, confirming that cu_pool pre-screening filters ops before assembly. An op that produces zero net violation delta is dropped by cu_pool regardless of connectivity outcome (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.05 assemble_drops).

In trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01 and trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01, single `move_instance` ops on leaf_0002 cleared unit_gate with zero new violations and zero dropped ops. The two trials differ only in instance id (i0452 vs i0451) and displacement sign (-36 vs +36 dbu on the x-axis); both produced identical outcomes. Instance-level x-axis moves on cells spanning M2, M3, and V2 pass the unit_gate channel cleanly on both connectivity and zero-violation grounds when the touched layers shift as a rigid unit. The unit_gate `conn_preserved` criterion is satisfied by both zero-violation and nonzero-violation assemblies; the violation count itself is not gating (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0014.05, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.01).
