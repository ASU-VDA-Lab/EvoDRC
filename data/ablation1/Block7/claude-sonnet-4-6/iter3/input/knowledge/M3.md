## Spacing Rule Sensitivities on M3

**Tip-to-side spacing (M3.S.2, 25 nm) is the most frequently triggered M3 spacing rule under y-axis shrink operations.** In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20, a bulk symmetric y-resize of 39 M3 polygons each by -64 dbu generated 10 new M3.S.2 violations and 1 new M3.S.4 violation within the crop window. No M3.S.1 or M3.S.3 violations were created by the same operation, confirming that the critical constraint in dense M3 vertical-shrink scenarios is the tip-to-side gap (25 nm) and the narrow-tip-to-narrow-tip gap (31 nm), not the side-to-side gap (18 nm).

**Shrinking M3 polygon tips toward neighboring side edges converts a previously safe side-to-side geometry into a tip-to-side geometry**, which is governed by the stricter M3.S.2 rule (25 nm) rather than M3.S.1 (18 nm). A tip edge is any edge with length <= 36 nm. After a -64 dbu symmetric y-resize, polygons that previously had long horizontal edges (length > 36 nm, side class) can acquire short tip edges (length <= 36 nm, tip class), changing the applicable spacing rule from 18 nm to 25 nm without any movement of the neighboring polygon (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20).

**Narrow tips (edge length < 24 nm) trigger M3.S.4 (31 nm tip-to-tip) rather than M3.S.3 (27 nm).** The single M3.S.4 violation produced in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20 during the same bulk y-resize confirms that polygons whose horizontal extent falls below 24 nm after shrinking must satisfy the most restrictive tip-to-tip budget (31 nm projection). Avoid resize operations that drive M3 polygon width below 24 nm unless 31 nm clearance to opposing tips is verified.

## Via Enclosure Constraints (V2.M3.EN.2, V2.M3.AUX.2, V3.M3.EN.1)

**Shrinking the M3 enclosure of VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 in the y-axis by -40 dbu was rejected as net-positive.** The cu_pool trial trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 applied a single resize_via_shape (axis y, delta -40 dbu) to the M3 layer of that via cell and was rejected with reason "rejected_net_positive", meaning the operation increased the total DRC count across the monitored windows. Correspondingly, the assemble_drops field of trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20 records that the same op (resize_via_shape, axis y, delta -40 dbu on VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 M3 shape index 0) was dropped during assembly because it had already been rejected in cu_pool. Do not apply negative y-axis resize to M3 enclosure shapes of VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36; the enclosure margin is already at or near the V2.M3.EN.2 minimum (5 nm on two opposite sides) and any reduction triggers violations.

**V2.M3.EN.2 requires that V2 be enclosed by M3 on at least two opposite sides by >= 5 nm (projection), with the second side allowed to be 0 nm.** Instance moves that translate M2, M3, and V2 together as a rigid group preserve the relative enclosure geometry and were accepted across multiple trials (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.13 moved an instance -36 dbu in y touching M2, M3, V2 with zero new violations; trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 moved a polygon and two instances +57 dbu in y with zero new violations). When moving only the M3 polygon without moving the V2 instance, or vice versa, the enclosure changes; such decoupled operations were not observed in the accepted trials and should be verified against V2.M3.EN.2 and V2.M3.AUX.2 before committing.

**V2.M3.AUX.2 requires V2 width to exactly match M3 width perpendicular to M3 length.** None of the accepted trials adjusted M3 width independently of V2 for via-bearing polygons; all width changes that touched via layers moved the entire instance group coherently (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25). Resize operations that change only M3 polygon ends without also adjusting the coincident V2 cell will violate V2.M3.AUX.2.

## Instance Move Patterns on M3

**Moving instances that span M1, M2, M3, V1, and V2 as a group is the lowest-risk repair operation for M3 spacing violations.** All six iter-1 unit_gate trials (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25) and all four accepted iter-2 unit_gate trials (trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.13, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0023.16, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0032.17) were gated in with conn_preserved=true, even when they introduced small numbers of new in-crop violations. The gating criterion is connectivity preservation, not zero new violations.

**Large instance translations in x (36-108 dbu) and y (52-64 dbu) were all accepted when moving coherent instance groups.** trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 moved one instance 108 dbu in x; trial:i02.ug.leaf_0032.17 moved two instances +52 dbu in y introducing 4 new in-crop violations; trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 moved a polygon and two instances +57 dbu in y with zero new violations. In contrast, the bulk M3-only polygon resize in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20 (no instance moves, only polygon geometry changes) produced 150 new violations. Moving instance groups preserves internal M3-to-V2 and M3-to-V3 geometric relationships; direct polygon-only resizes do not.

## M3-Only Polygon Resize Behavior

**Symmetric y-axis resize (both ends, same magnitude) applied to M3-only polygons is a high-violation-count operation when applied in bulk.** trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20 applied axis=y resize (not resize_end) with delta=-64 dbu to 39 polygons and produced 150 new in-crop violations including 10 M3.S.2 and 1 M3.S.4. Despite this, the trial was accepted because conn_preserved=true and n_new_out_of_crop=0. The per-rule breakdown shows M3 violations are a minority of the total; the majority (M1.A.1: 49, V1.M1.EN.1: 52) are on other layers affected indirectly by the vertical shrink.

**Single-polygon tip shortening (resize_end) on M3 introduces one new in-crop violation and is accepted.** trial:i02.ug.leaf_0009.12 shortened the high-y end of polygon p2328 by -20 dbu (touching M3 only) and introduced 1 new in-crop violation while being gated in. This is the minimal observed M3 tip adjustment; smaller adjustments were not measured. The 20 dbu shortening is 20 nm, which is larger than one grid step and within a typical tip length reduction.

## Operational Summary for M3 Repair

Instance group moves (all connected layers translated together) have produced zero or minimal new M3 violations in every measured trial and preserve V2/V3 enclosure relationships automatically (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.13, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0023.16). Use instance moves as the first repair strategy for M3 spacing violations.

Do not apply negative y-axis resize_via_shape to VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 M3 shape 0; this operation was rejected in trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 and dropped from assembly in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20.

Do not apply large symmetric y-axis resize to groups of M3 polygons without checking that the resulting horizontal edge lengths remain > 36 nm (to avoid converting side edges to tips) and that any remaining tip edges have >= 25 nm clearance to neighboring side edges (M3.S.2) and >= 31 nm clearance to opposing narrow tips (M3.S.4), as established by the violation pattern in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20.
