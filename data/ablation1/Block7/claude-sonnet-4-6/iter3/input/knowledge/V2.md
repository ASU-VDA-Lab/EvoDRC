## M3 end-cap classification governs which spacing floor applies

V2.S.1 through V2.S.4 encode four spacing contexts determined by whether each via has a 5 nm M3 end-cap. A via is classified as wec (with end-cap) when its edges are not all coincident with M3 edges; it is nec (no end-cap) when all its edges are coincident with M3 edges. The spacing floors are: 18 nm projection for same-track or aligned parallel-track vias (V2.S.1); 23 nm euclidean for wec-wec pairs (V2.S.2); 30 nm euclidean for nec-nec pairs (V2.S.3); 27 nm euclidean for wec-nec mixed pairs (V2.S.4).
