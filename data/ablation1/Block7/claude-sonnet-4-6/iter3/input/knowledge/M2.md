**Spacing rule context and move sizing**

M2.S.1 sets 18 nm as the minimum side-to-side spacing for edges longer than 36 nm. M2.S.2 requires 25 nm tip-to-side. M2.S.3 requires 27 nm tip-to-tip when both tips are 24-36 nm long.
