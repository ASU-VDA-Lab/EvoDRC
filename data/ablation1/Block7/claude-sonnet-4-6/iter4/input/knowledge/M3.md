## M3 Spacing Rule Sensitivity

### M3.S.2 (Tip-to-Side, 25 nm)

Bulk simultaneous shrink of multiple M3 polygon heights -- 39 symmetric y-axis resize operations each at -64 dbu applied across the full block locus -- introduced 10 new M3.S.2 violations (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20). Avoid applying large-count symmetric y-resize operations across many M3 polygons in a single pass; this pattern directly caused M3.S.2 tip-to-side failures (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20). Single-polygon resize_end operations at modest deltas (y-high end -20 dbu, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0009.12) do not introduce new M3 violations; prefer single-polygon targeted resize_end over bulk resize when correcting M3 geometry (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0009.12, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03).

### M3.S.4 (Narrow Tip-to-Tip, 31 nm)

The same bulk y-resize pass (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20) also produced 1 new M3.S.4 violation. When M3 polygon tips are narrow (< 24 nm), shrinking them in y brings opposing narrow tips closer together and violates the 31 nm tip-to-tip minimum. Do not apply bulk symmetric y-resize to M3 polygons without verifying that resulting tip separations remain >= 31 nm for narrow-tip pairs (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20).
