## Move Quantum and X-Axis Grid Alignment

The 36 dbu step is the dominant X-axis move quantum across all three iterations. Trials i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01, i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05, i01.ug.Block7_union_row5.17, i02.ug.Block7_union_row6.08, and i03.ug.Block7_union_row24.05 each apply 36 dbu lateral moves to one or more instances and are all accepted with n_new_in_crop=0. Given M1.W.1 (18 nm minimum width) and M1.S.1 (18 nm minimum side-to-side spacing for edges longer than 36 nm), 36 dbu equals the sum of the minimum width and minimum side space — one M1 pitch. Applying this quantum to all instances in a routing row simultaneously preserves inter-polygon spacing relationships along the X axis.

Non-36-dbu X moves occur in a minority of trials. Trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 moves instance i1446 by 108 dbu and i1442 by 40 dbu; trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row8.20 moves i1405 and i1921 by 64 dbu while i1891 moves -36 dbu. These larger or mixed-delta steps appear alongside resize_end operations, confirming that a single-pitch step was insufficient for the required polygon extension or that opposing instance moves require polygon bridging. Trial i01.ug.leaf_0002.23 uses a 72 dbu instance move paired with a 128 dbu resize_end on polygon p3695 axis x high, demonstrating that when a polygon must also clear an additional M1.S constraint, the resize delta exceeds the instance move delta.

## Paired resize_end with move_instance

When a move_instance repositions an instance that anchors a polygon endpoint, a resize_end on the same axis and same end is required to extend the connected polygon to the new position. Trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14 shows the direct case: instance i0215 moves +36 dbu on X and polygon p3058 is resized +36 dbu on x-high. Trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15 shows an asymmetric case: instance i1623 moves -36 dbu on X while polygon p3379 is resized +49 dbu on x-high, a mismatch of 85 dbu indicating the polygon endpoint was not at the instance origin and required independent alignment. Trial i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 extends this to two axes simultaneously: instance i0347 moves [+72, +44] and polygon p3538 is resized +36 on x-high and +44 on y-high, maintaining the connection in both routing directions.

Shrinking a polygon from its low end uses a resize_end with a negative delta on the low end. Trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 applies `axis x, delta_dbu -36, end low` to polygon p3300 in the same operation set that translates polygon p2720 by +56 dbu on X. The low-end shrink removes metal from the left side of p3300 while the p2720 translate shifts a separate routing segment; both are accepted with n_new_in_crop=0, confirming neither the shrink-induced area reduction nor the shifted polygon introduced new violations.

Polygon p3538 receives resize_end adjustments in three separate trials across two iterations: `axis x high +36` and `axis y high +44` in trial i01.ug.leaf_0024.25, then `axis y high +44` in trial i02.ug.Block7_union_row13.00, then `axis y high -44` in trial i03.ug.Block7_union_row13.00. The repeated Y-high adjustments indicate this polygon connects structures in both the horizontal and vertical routing directions and must be trimmed or extended each time the connected instances shift.

## Polygon Translate (op: "move")

Three trials use `op: "move"` on a polygon rather than resize_end. Trial i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 translates polygon p3631 by +57 dbu on Y. Trial i03.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 translates the same polygon by -57 dbu on Y, returning it toward its prior position. Trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 translates polygon p2720 by +56 dbu on X. In all three cases the polygon translate is paired with move_instance operations on associated instances (i0794 and i0810 by [0,+57] and [0,-57] in the two row17 trials), confirming that a polygon translate moves a floating segment together with its attached instance without altering polygon dimensions. The +57 then -57 Y-translate of p3631 across iterations 2 and 3 is a reversal applied alongside additional operations; trial i03.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 accompanies the revert with a `axis y low, delta_dbu +20` resize_end on polygon p2594, a separate adjustment not present in iteration 2.

## Y-Axis Adjustments and Enclosure

Y-axis instance move magnitudes span a wider range than X-axis moves and do not converge on a single dominant quantum. Values of 8, 16, 44, 52, 57, 64, 72, -16, -44, -48, -52, -64, -88 dbu all appear across the three iterations. The 44 dbu Y step is the most recurring: instances i0041, i0347, and i1062 each receive Y moves of ±44 dbu in multiple trials. Instance i0920 receives +64 dbu on Y in trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 and -64 dbu on Y in trial i02.ug.leaf_0021.14, then +64 dbu in trial i03.ug.Block7_union_row14.01 — the same oscillatory pattern seen for X-axis instances. V1.M1.EN.1 requires 5 nm enclosure on the two opposite sides along one axis and 2 nm on the other; Y-axis instance moves that shift M1 relative to embedded V1 vias must therefore keep the M1 polygon boundary at least 5 nm beyond the via edge on the moved axis, which sets the minimum magnitude of Y resize_end operations paired with those moves. Trial i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 confirms this: the y-high +44 resize_end on p3538 matches exactly the +44 Y component of the instance move for i0347, maintaining the polygon's overhanging edge at a fixed offset from the via.

V0.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V0 by 5 nm on two opposite sides, allowing a 5 & 0 nm configuration. V1.M1.EN.1 requires 5 & 2 nm. Because both rules are projection-mode checks, only the edges parallel to the enclosure direction matter; a Y-direction instance move does not affect the horizontal projection enclosure and vice versa. All trials in this dataset that include Y moves also include V1 in touched_layers, and all are accepted with conn_preserved: true.

## Coordinated Multi-Instance Row Moves

Row-level repairs apply the same delta to multiple instances in a single trial. Trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05 moves seven instances by [36, 0]; trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row6.18 (iteration 1) moves a single instance by [28, 0] and is accepted with zero new violations, while trial i02.ug.Block7_union_row6.08 (iteration 2) moves five instances in the same row by [36, 0]. The evolution from a 1-op to 5-op repair for the same unit across iterations confirms that upstream changes in iteration 1 shift the row geometry enough that more instances require correction in iteration 2.

When instances within the same trial receive different deltas, a polygon resize bridges the gap. Trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 moves instance i1140 by [+4, 0] while instances i1811 and i1180 move by [-36, 0]; polygon p3305 is resized +50 dbu on x-high, a value equal to the 4 dbu move of i1140 plus the 36 dbu offset of the opposing moves, plus rounding, spanning the total displacement between the two groups. Trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 combines a +64 x-high resize of p3200 with a +48 x-high resize, Y resizes on p3576, and simultaneous Y moves of instances i0894 and i0920, confirming that enclosure compliance in both axes can require concurrent resize_end on multiple polygons.

## Instance Oscillation Across Iterations

Several instances undergo opposing moves in successive iterations without converging, establishing a pattern of competing-constraint oscillation.

Instance i0949 and i0950 move [0, -52] in trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09 (iter 1), then [0, +52] in trial i02.ug.leaf_0032.17 (iter 2), then [0, -52] again in trial i03.ug.Block7_union_row19.03 (iter 3). Each move is accepted as gated_in with conn_preserved: true.

Instance i0041 moves [-36, +44] in trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row23.13 (iter 1), then [0, -44] in trial i02.ug.leaf_0041.19 (iter 2), then [0, +72] in trial i03.ug.Block7_union_row23.04 (iter 3). The Y component cycles: +44 → -44 → +72.

Instance i0347 moves [+72, +44] in trial i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 (iter 1), then [0, +44] in trial i02.ug.Block7_union_row13.00 (iter 2), then [0, -88] in trial i03.ug.Block7_union_row13.00 (iter 3). The net Y displacement after iteration 3 is 44+44-88=0, returning to the iteration-1 starting Y position while the X position remains shifted +72 dbu.

Instance i0920 moves [0, +64] in trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 (iter 1), then [0, -64] in trial i02.ug.leaf_0021.14 (iter 2), then [0, +64] in trial i03.ug.Block7_union_row14.01 (iter 3) — exact cancellation repeated in each pair. When an instance oscillates by equal and opposite Y steps, it indicates that a neighboring geometry repair in one iteration displaces the spacing clearance in the opposite direction, re-triggering the original violation at the next iteration's DRC check.

Instance i0041 is also the subject of trial i02.ug.leaf_0041.19 which introduces n_new_in_crop=5, and trial i03.ug.Block7_union_row23.04 which introduces n_new_in_crop=0 with a larger [0,+72] step. The larger step in iteration 3 resolves the violations introduced in iteration 2 while introducing none of its own.

## Connectivity Preservation as the Acceptance Gate

Every trial in this dataset is accepted with decision: gated_in and conn_preserved: true, including trials with n_new_in_crop greater than zero. The trials with the highest new violation counts are i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11 (n_new_in_crop=9, single op: instance i0100 moved [+4, 0]), i02.ug.leaf_0041.19 (n_new_in_crop=5), i03.ug.leaf_0020.09 (n_new_in_crop=5), i02.ug.leaf_0032.17 (n_new_in_crop=4), i03.ug.leaf_0010.08 (n_new_in_crop=4), i01.ug.Block7_union_row22.12 (n_new_in_crop=2), and several others with n_new_in_crop=1-2. In all cases n_new_out_of_crop=0. A repair that preserves connectivity and introduces no violations outside the crop region is accepted regardless of how many violations it introduces inside the crop, with the expectation that those new in-crop violations are addressed in the same or subsequent iterations.

The smallest accepted move that introduces new violations is the 4 dbu move of i0100 in trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11 (n_new_in_crop=9). This confirms that even sub-pitch adjustments on M1 can introduce multiple new DRC hits if the surrounding geometry is tightly packed.

## Multi-Layer Trials and Operation Count

Trials touching M1+M2+M3+V1+V2 require more operations than M1+M2+V1 trials. Trials with the five-layer set consistently reach 6-9 ops: i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 (9 ops, M1+M2+M3+V1+V2), i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 (9 ops), i03.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 (9 ops), i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 (8 ops), i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03 (5 ops), i02.ug.leaf_0023.16 (3 ops, also M3+V2). M1+M2+V1-only trials range from 1 to 7 ops, with several single-op trials: i01.ug.Block7_union_row6.18, i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11, i02.ug.leaf_0007.10, i02.ug.leaf_0035.18, i02.ug.leaf_0022.15, i02.ug.leaf_0041.19, i03.ug.Block7_union_row23.04, i03.ug.leaf_0020.09.

The polygon `op: "move"` translate appears only in multi-layer trials (i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02, i03.ug.Block7_union_row17.02), confirming that floating-segment translates are a technique reserved for routing adjustments that span multiple metal layers.

## GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL Constraint

All resize_end and polygon-move operations in this dataset are strictly axis-aligned: every op specifies `axis: "x"` or `axis: "y"` with no diagonal component. All instance moves use delta_dbu arrays of the form `[dx, 0]` or `[0, dy]`, with three exceptions where both dx and dy are nonzero: instances in trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row22.12 (i0132: [+36, -48]) and trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row23.13 (i0041: [-36, +44]), and trial i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 (i0347: [+72, +44]). These diagonal moves are on instance origins, not on M1 polygon edges directly; the resulting M1 polygon geometry remains rectangular and orthogonal because polygon edges are adjusted separately via resize_end on a single axis. The GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL rule fires on any M1 edge with angle outside 0 and 90 degrees; no trial in this dataset introduces or accepts such edges.

## M1.A.1 Area Constraint Under Polygon Shrink

M1.A.1 requires a minimum M1 polygon area of 504 nm^2. The shrink operations present in this dataset (negative low-end resize_end in trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, y-high resize from +44 to -44 across trials on p3538) are all accepted with n_new_out_of_crop=0, confirming the resulting polygons remain above the minimum area threshold. No trial introduces a new M1.A.1 violation (it would appear as n_new_in_crop or n_new_out_of_crop), establishing that the shrink magnitudes applied — the largest being the -36 dbu low-end resize of p3300 in trial i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 — do not reduce M1 polygons below 504 nm^2 within these repair loci.
