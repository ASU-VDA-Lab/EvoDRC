**Instance oscillation across iterations signals axis-switching convergence.** Instance i1358 was repositioned across four consecutive design states: [0,+8] in iter1 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02), [0,-36] in iter2 (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.13), [0,+36] in iter3 (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.07), and [-36,0] in iter4 (trial:i04.ug.leaf_0002.02).
