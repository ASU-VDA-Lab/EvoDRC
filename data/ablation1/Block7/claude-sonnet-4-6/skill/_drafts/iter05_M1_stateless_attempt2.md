## Repair Primitives

`move_instance` is the primary repair operation across all accepted M1 trials; 63 of 64 history records include at least one `move_instance` op. Use `move_instance` to carry all geometry belonging to a cell (M1 segments, vias, overlying metal) as a unit, since every accepted trial that touches M1 also requires matching V1 and M2 adjustments in the same trial (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row6.08). `resize_end` appears as a supplementary op in trials where instance moves alone do not satisfy enclosure or width constraints (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24). The `move` polygon op appears in three trials to reposition a single M1 polygon body without instance-level granularity (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02, trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row17.02).

## Resize-End for Enclosure and Width

Apply `resize_end` when an M1 polygon endpoint must be extended to satisfy V0.M1.EN.1 (5 nm enclosure of V0 by M1) or V1.M1.EN.1 (5 nm and 2 nm enclosure of V1 by M1) requirements without moving the containing instance. Observed resize-end magnitudes range from 4 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00) to 164 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24). Extending the high end of an M1 polygon in the x direction is the most frequent resize pattern: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15 (x high +49), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 (x high +56), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 (x high +48), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23 (x high +128), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24 (x high +164), trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row19.04 (x high +92), trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.01 (x high +108). Retracting a low end by a negative delta extends coverage from the opposite side: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 (x low -36), trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.01 (x low -40). When two resize_end ops target opposite ends of the same polygon within one trial, the net effect is a rigid body translation of that polygon: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 applies both y-high and y-low resizes to polygon p3694 in the same trial.

Large resize deltas (> 36 dbu) convert short M1 tip edges (length <= 36 nm per M1.S.2/S.3 classification) into longer side edges (length > 36 nm), relaxing the applicable spacing rule from M1.S.2 or M1.S.3 to M1.S.1; this is consistent with the very large resizes in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23 (delta=128) and trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24 (delta=164), both accepted with n_new_out_of_crop=0.

Y-axis resize_end operations appear in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 (y high +8 and y low -8 on p3694), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 (y high +64 on p3576), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 (y high +44 on p3538 and y high +20 on p2333), trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row14.01 (y high +8 on p3515), and trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 (y low +20 on p2594). Use y-axis resize_end to satisfy enclosure requirements along the M1 length direction.

## Combining Multiple Ops Per Trial

Issue all ops needed to repair a unit's violations in a single trial. Accepted trials routinely contain 5 to 9 ops: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 has 9 ops (6 move_instance and 3 resize_end); trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 has 9 ops (7 move_instance, 1 resize_end, 1 polygon move); trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 has 8 ops (5 move_instance and 3 resize_end); trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 has 9 ops (5 move_instance, 1 polygon move, 2 move_instance, 1 resize_end). Multi-op trials mix move_instance, resize_end, and polygon move ops as needed to simultaneously satisfy M1 spacing, enclosure, and connectivity constraints (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 uses 2 move_instance and 4 resize_end ops across M1, M2, M3, and V1 in one trial).

## Connectivity Preservation

All 64 accepted trials record `conn_preserved=true`. Move instances as a coordinated group rather than individually when they share a net, ensuring every segment of the net shifts by compatible deltas: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05 moves 7 instances by the identical [36,0] delta; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 moves 7 instances with varying x deltas (36, 36, 36, 36, 108, 56, 56 dbu) while preserving the relative topology of the net. Pair polygon resize_end ops with matching via or landing-pad adjustments when resizing an M1 polygon that hosts a V0 or V1 (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 resizes p3538 on both x and y while also moving instance i0347 and i1292 and resizing p3706).

## M1 Area and Width Constraints

M1.A.1 requires a minimum area of 504 nm-sq; M1.W.1 requires a minimum width of 18 nm. All observed resize_end operations in the 64 accepted trials extend polygon endpoints rather than shorten them; no trial applies a resize_end that reduces M1 polygon area or width. Avoid resize_end deltas that retract an M1 endpoint so far that the remaining polygon falls below 18 nm width or 504 nm-sq area; the accepted trials confirm that extension-only resizes do not trigger M1.W.1 or M1.A.1 violations (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row19.04, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.01).

## Axis Alignment

All move_instance, resize_end, and polygon move ops in the 64 accepted trials specify only x or y axis displacements with integer dbu deltas; no diagonal or fractional moves appear. Issue only axis-aligned ops on M1 geometry, as confirmed by every accepted trial in the history (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.01, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0004.04). This is consistent with the GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL rule, which rejects any M1 edge not at 0, 90, 180, or 270 degrees.
