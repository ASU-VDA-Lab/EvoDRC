## Repair acceptance criterion

All nine accepted repairs share `conn_preserved: true` and `decision: gated_in` (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.13, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0023.16, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0032.17). Connectivity preservation is the gating criterion. Accepted repairs may introduce zero new in-crop violations (trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.13, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0023.16, n_new_in_crop=0) or up to four (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0032.17, n_new_in_crop=4); in all accepted cases n_new_out_of_crop=0. No accepted repair propagates violations outside the crop region.

A repair is rejected with `decision: rejected_net_positive` when the net change in total violations is zero (delta_total=0), even when conn_preserved=true (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). Do not apply a repair operation that leaves the total violation count unchanged.

## Multi-layer co-movement scope depends on the repair's instance hierarchy

Most accepted repairs touch M1, M2, M3, V1, and V2 simultaneously (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0023.16, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0032.17). However, a repair touching only M2, M3, and V2 (without M1 or V1) is also accepted when those are the only affected layers (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.13, n_ops=1, single move_instance [0,−36]). Co-move only the layers that are geometrically coupled to the displaced V2; do not move M1 or V1 structures that have no spatial relationship to the repair locus. In all cases, do not move a V2 instance without co-moving its enclosing M2 and M3 structures, as V2.AUX.1 requires V2 to remain inside both M2 and M3 and V2.M3.AUX.2 requires V2 to be exactly as wide as M3 perpendicular to the M3 length direction (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21).

## M3 polygon-end resize accompanies V2 displacement when M3 does not fully translate

Four iteration-1 accepted repairs include at least one `resize_end` operation on an M3 polygon alongside instance moves: p3273 x-high +56 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02); p3526 x-high +4 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03); p3576 y-high +64 dbu and p3200 x-high +48 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04); p3300 x-low −36 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21). Apply a `resize_end` to the proximate M3 end when a V2 instance is displaced but the M3 segment does not translate with it in full, to maintain the coincident-edge relationship required by V2.M3.AUX.2 and the ≥5 nm enclosure required by V2.M3.EN.2 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21). Iteration-2 repairs that fully co-translate the M3 segment with the V2 instance (trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.13, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0023.16, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0032.17) require no `resize_end`.

## Do not resize via cell geometry to repair spacing violations

The `resize_via_shape` operation on M3 geometry inside a via cell definition (cell VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36, M3 layer, y-axis −40 dbu) was rejected with `decision: rejected_net_positive` because it produced delta_total=0 (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). Do not modify via cell M3 shape dimensions to attempt V2 DRC repair; use instance-level moves and polygon-level operations in the design layout instead.

## M3 end-cap classification governs which spacing floor applies

V2.S.1 through V2.S.4 encode four spacing contexts determined by whether each via has a 5 nm M3 end-cap. A via is classified as wec (with end-cap) when its edges are not all coincident with M3 edges; it is nec (no end-cap) when all its edges are coincident with M3 edges. The spacing floors are: 18 nm projection for same-track or aligned parallel-track vias (V2.S.1); 23 nm euclidean for wec–wec pairs (V2.S.2); 30 nm euclidean for nec–nec pairs (V2.S.3); 27 nm euclidean for wec–nec mixed pairs (V2.S.4). The `resize_end` operations in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 alter the M3 end extent relative to via edges and therefore change the end-cap classification of affected vias; use the post-resize classification when determining which spacing floor to apply.

## V2.W.1 and V2.M3.AUX.2 jointly constrain M3 width at via locations

V2.W.1 sets the minimum via width at 18 nm along the M3 length direction. V2.M3.AUX.2 requires V2 to be exactly as wide as M3 perpendicular to M3 length, so M3 width at any via location must also be ≥18 nm. The `resize_end` operations in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 (p3273 x-high +56 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 (p3200 x-high +48 dbu), and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 (p3300 x-low −36 dbu) extend or shift M3 ends without narrowing the segment at the via position. Do not resize an M3 end inward past the via boundary, as this violates both V2.M3.AUX.2 and V2.W.1 simultaneously (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21).

## Bidirectional and mixed-axis moves are supported in a single repair sequence

Accepted repairs use pure x-axis moves (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02: +36 dbu; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03: +40 dbu; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21: +36, +56, +108 dbu), pure y-axis moves (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09: −52 dbu; trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02: +57 dbu; trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.13: −36 dbu; trial:i02.ug.leaf_0023.16: −16 dbu; trial:i02.ug.leaf_0032.17: +52 dbu), and mixed x+y moves within a single repair sequence (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04: y-displacements of +64 and −12 dbu alongside x-displacement of +28 dbu and M3 y-resize of +64 dbu). All moves in all nine accepted trials are axis-aligned, consistent with the NONORTHOGONAL rule that forbids non-45°-grid-aligned edges on V2.

## Direct polygon moves supplement instance moves for independent metal repositioning

Two iteration-1 and two iteration-2 accepted repairs include `move` operations on individual polygons. trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 moves polygon p2720 (+56 dbu in x) alongside instance moves and a `resize_end`. trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 moves polygon p3631 (+57 dbu in y) alongside instance moves on i0794 and i0810. trial:i02.ug.leaf_0023.16 moves polygon p3516 (−16 dbu in y) alongside instance moves on i0336 and i0308. Use a direct polygon `move` when an M3 segment requires displacement independent of its parent instance hierarchy, provided V2.AUX.1 and V2.M3.EN.2 remain satisfied after the operation (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0023.16).
