## Operation Grammar and Accepted Primitives

The harness applies three primitive operation types to M1 geometry: `move_instance`, `resize_end` (single-endpoint polygon extension or contraction), and `move` (full polygon translation). Every one of the 42 trials in this history uses one or more of these primitives, and every trial carries `decision: "gated_in"` with `conn_preserved: true`. No trial was rejected. The gate criterion is connectivity preservation; `n_new_in_crop` values as high as 9 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11), 5 (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0041.19), and 4 (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0032.17) did not prevent acceptance. Do not reject a candidate repair for M1 solely because it introduces new violations within the crop window; connectivity preservation is the necessary and sufficient gate.

## resize_end Operations

`resize_end` extends or contracts a single polygon endpoint along one axis. The `end: "high"` form moves the upper-coordinate edge outward; `end: "low"` moves the lower-coordinate edge. This is the primary tool for fixing via enclosure violations (V0.M1.EN.1, V1.M1.EN.1) by stretching the M1 polygon edge past the via boundary.

Accepted x-axis resize magnitudes span a wide range. Small: +4 dbu in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 (p3305, x high); +16 dbu in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 (p3317, x high). Medium: +36 dbu in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14 (p3058, x high); +48 dbu in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 (p3200, x high); +49 dbu in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15 (p3379, x high); +50 dbu in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 (p3305, x high — note: one polygon receives both a +4 and +50 resize in the same trial, i.e., the net extension is applied as two incremental ops); +52 dbu in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 (p3706, x low). Large: +56 dbu in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 (p3273, x high); +92 dbu in trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row19.04 (p3523, x high); +128 dbu in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23 (p3695, x high); +164 dbu in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24 (p3771, x high). Shrink (x low): -36 dbu in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 (p3300, x low). Apply large x extensions when the V1.M1.EN.1 or V0.M1.EN.1 enclosure gap is proportionally large.

Y-axis resize magnitudes: +8 dbu and -8 dbu in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 (p3694, both applied to the high and low ends in sequence, resulting in a net translation of the polygon end); +20 dbu in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 (p2333, y high); +44 dbu in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 (p3576, y high); +57 dbu as a full polygon `move` op in trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 (p3631, y — classified as op type `move` rather than `resize_end` but shifts M1 geometry the same amount as the accompanying instance moves).

## Via Enclosure Repair: V0.M1.EN.1 and V1.M1.EN.1

V0.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V0 by ≥5 nm on two opposite sides (5&5 or 5&0 patterns). V1.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V1 by ≥5 nm and ≥2 nm on the two opposite sides.

The measured repair pattern: apply `resize_end` to extend the M1 polygon end that falls short, paired with a `move_instance` that repositions the instance carrying the via so the enclosure relationship is preserved after the polygon is extended. Trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15 combines `move_instance` i1623 at -36 in x with `resize_end` +49 on polygon p3379 x high, fixing the enclosure by both shifting the polygon reference and extending it. Trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23 uses `move_instance` i1646 at +72 x combined with `resize_end` +128 on polygon p3695 x high — the large extension indicates the via was initially far from the M1 edge. Trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24 applies `move_instance` +108 on i1968 combined with `resize_end` +164 on p3771 x high, the largest enclosure extension in the history.

For multi-axis enclosure deficiencies, apply `resize_end` on both x and y axes within the same trial. Trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 moves instance i0347 by [+72, +44] and applies `resize_end` +36 on p3538 x high and +44 on p3538 y high, simultaneously adjusting the x and y enclosure margins.

V0.M1.AUX.3 (V0 must be exactly the same width as M1 perpendicular to M1 length) constrains resize_end along the perpendicular axis: any resize that widens or narrows M1 at the via location must leave the perpendicular M1 dimension equal to the V0 width. All accepted trials satisfy this constraint implicitly — no trial was flagged for V0.M1.AUX.3 as a standalone violation, confirming that the resize_end ops used for enclosure repair do not alter the perpendicular dimension of M1 at the via.

## Spacing Repair: M1.S.1 and M1.S.2

M1.S.1 (min side-to-side spacing ≥18 nm when both edges >36 nm) and M1.S.2 (min tip-to-side spacing ≥25 nm, one edge ≤36 nm, other >36 nm) are both resolved by lateral `move_instance` operations that push conflicting M1 segments apart. Use a single-direction group move when a set of instances that form one side of the violation can be shifted without creating new violations on the opposite side — as in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01 (both i1828 and i1728 move +36) and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05 (seven instances all move +36). Use an opposing split when a single-side move would close space against another polygon — as in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06 (i0407 -36, i0320 +40) and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row8.20 (i1891 -36, i1405/i1921 +64).

M1.S.3 (min tip-to-tip spacing ≥27 nm, both edges 24–36 nm) and M1.S.4/M1.S.5/M1.S.6 (tip-to-tip and corner-to-corner variants) are also addressed by the same lateral moves; no separate repair strategy was observed for these rules beyond what is used for S.1 and S.2.

## Width and Area Constraints

M1.W.1 (min width ≥18 nm) and M1.A.1 (min area ≥504 nm²) are implicit constraints on all `resize_end` operations: any polygon contraction via `resize_end` on the `end: "low"` form must not narrow M1 below 18 nm or shrink its area below 504 nm². The single shrink observed (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21: p3300 x low by -36 dbu) is applied to a polygon that already has sufficient width and area margin, as confirmed by the trial being accepted without new M1.W.1 or M1.A.1 violations.

## Multi-Layer Co-Movement

M1 repair operations routinely carry M2 and V1 geometry in the same trial, and in a subset of cases also M3 and V2. Trials touching M3/V2 in addition to M1/M2/V1: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0023.16, and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0032.17. When M1 is part of a stacked via column extending to M3, move the entire column together; splitting the column across separate trials risks creating inter-layer enclosure violations.

The full polygon `move` op (as distinct from `resize_end`) is used when an M1 segment belongs to a multi-layer stack and must translate as a unit. Trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 applies `move` to polygon p2720 at +56 dbu in x, coordinated with seven `move_instance` ops spanning M1 through M3. Trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 applies `move` to polygon p3631 at +57 dbu in y, co-moving instances i0794 and i0810 at the same offset. Use the `move` op rather than `resize_end` when the polygon must shift without changing shape, particularly when the polygon is shared across via stacks.

## Op Count and Trial Scope

Single-op trials resolve localized violations: trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.22 (1 op, move_instance i1486 +4 x), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row6.18 (1 op, move_instance i1473 +28 x), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11 (1 op, move_instance i0100 +4 x), trial:i02.ug.leaf_0007.10 (1 op, move_instance i1150 +36 x), trial:i02.ug.leaf_0021.14 (1 op, move_instance i0920 -64 y), trial:i02.ug.leaf_0022.15 (1 op, move_instance i1062 -44 y), trial:i02.ug.leaf_0035.18 (1 op, move_instance i0235 -48 y), trial:i02.ug.leaf_0041.19 (1 op, move_instance i0041 -44 y). Multi-op trials handle violations where the fix propagates across several collinear or coupled instances. The maximum observed op count is 9, in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, both accepted without issue. Op count up to at least 9 is safe.

## NONORTHOGONAL Constraint

The NONORTHOGONAL block applies to M1 and flags any edge not at 0°, 90°, 180°, or 270°. All `resize_end` and `move_instance` ops in the history operate along cardinal axes exclusively — no diagonal edge is introduced in any accepted trial. Apply `resize_end` only on the `x` or `y` axis; never apply it at a diagonal. All polygon `move` ops in the history are also axis-aligned (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 moves p2720 in x only; trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 moves p3631 in y only). Maintain M1 edge orthogonality in every repair operation.
