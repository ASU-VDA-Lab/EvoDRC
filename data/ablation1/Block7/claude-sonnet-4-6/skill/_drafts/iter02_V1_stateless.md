## Repair Outcome Summary

Every trial in both iteration 1 and iteration 2 of the Block7 campaign received `decision: "gated_in"` with `conn_preserved: true` as the gating reason. No trial was rejected in the measured history. The universal acceptance criterion is connectivity preservation; trials that introduced new in-crop violations (n_new_in_crop > 0) were still accepted provided connectivity was intact, as seen in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03 (n_new_in_crop=4), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11 (n_new_in_crop=9), trial:i02.ug.leaf_0032.17 (n_new_in_crop=4), and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0041.19 (n_new_in_crop=5). The repair loop therefore must track introduced violations per crop but must not block acceptance on that count alone when connectivity holds.

## Multi-Layer Coordination is Mandatory

Every accepted trial that touched V1 also listed M1 and M2 as touched layers. No trial moved or resized V1 geometry without simultaneously adjusting M1 and M2. This reflects V1.AUX.1 (V1 must lie inside M1 and M2) and V1.M2.AUX.2 (V1 width must equal M2 width along the perpendicular direction): moving a V1 instance without moving its enclosing M1 and M2 polygons violates both rules. Apply all V1 position corrections as move_instance operations on the containing cell so that M1, M2, and V1 geometry travel as a unit, as done in all 40 trials across iterations 1 and 2.

Trials that additionally touched M3 and V2 indicate the repair propagated upward through the local interconnect stack: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0023.16, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0032.17. When the V1 being repaired connects upward to an M3/V2 stack, include those layers in the same move operation to avoid creating new enclosure violations on the upper via.

## Dominant Repair Operation: move_instance

The primary repair primitive is `move_instance`. Across all 40 accepted trials, the overwhelming majority of operations are `move_instance` on individual cell instances. The `resize_end` polygon operation is used as a secondary adjustment, typically to extend an M2 segment end after the via has been repositioned.

Never use `resize_end` without an accompanying `move_instance` on the via it encloses; the pattern in every multi-op trial shows the via move first (or in the same batch) with the polygon resize extending the enclosing wire end, as in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15 (move_instance then resize_end on p3379), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 (move_instance by 108 dbu then resize_end by 16 dbu then move_instance by 40 dbu), and trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23 (move_instance by 72 dbu then resize_end by 128 dbu on p3695).

## resize_end Patterns and Magnitudes

`resize_end` operations extend or shrink a specific end of an M2 (or M1) polygon along one axis. All accepted resize_end operations in the history act on the high end of the x-axis or the high end of the y-axis; no accepted trial shrinks the high end or resizes the low end of x (the sole low-end x case in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21, `-36 dbu` on `end: "low"` of p3300, is part of a multi-op sequence that simultaneously moves the polygon body by +56 dbu, resulting in a net rightward shift with shortened left edge). Always pair a resize_end that shrinks one end with a polygon move or a move_instance that relocates the body, as in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21.

Accepted resize_end x-high magnitudes: 4 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03 on p3526), 16 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 on p3317), 36 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row24.14 on p3058, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0024.25 on p3538 x-high by 36 dbu), 48 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 on p3200 x-high by 48 dbu), 49 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15 on p3379), 50 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 on p3305), 56 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 on p3273 by 56 dbu), 92 dbu (trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row19.04 on p3523 x-high by 92 dbu), 128 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23 on p3695), 164 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24 on p3771).

Accepted resize_end y-high magnitudes: 8 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 on p3694 both high and low ends, net zero change -- these cancel and serve only to ensure no net y distortion), 20 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 on p2333 y-high by 20 dbu), 44 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 on p3538 y-high by 44 dbu).

Use resize_end to satisfy V1.M2.EN.2 (minimum 5 nm M2 enclosure on two opposite sides) after a move_instance shifts the via away from the M2 polygon end. Extend the M2 end in the same direction and by the same magnitude as the via move, then verify the 5 nm enclosure is re-established on both opposite sides.

## Enclosure Rule Repair (V1.M1.EN.1, V1.M2.EN.2)

V1.M1.EN.1 requires M1 to enclose V1 by at least 5 nm on one axis pair and 2 nm on the other. V1.M2.EN.2 requires M2 enclosure of 5 nm on both sides, or 5 nm on one side and 0 nm (flush) on the other. Repairs that move a V1 via relative to its M2 wire by a large delta (e.g., 164 dbu x-extension in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24 or 128 dbu in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23) must apply a correspondingly large resize_end to the M2 polygon to re-establish the end-cap enclosure. The pattern across all accepted trials is that the resize_end magnitude is equal to or greater than the via move magnitude along the same axis.

When the move_instance delta on a via instance is 72 dbu and the resize_end on the enclosing M2 polygon is 128 dbu (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23), the difference (56 dbu) accounts for pre-existing enclosure margin consumed by the prior state. Do not assume a 1:1 ratio between via move and polygon extension; compute from the actual pre-move enclosure measurement and add the required minimum.

## V1.S.1 Spacing on Same and Parallel M2 Tracks

V1.S.1 enforces 18 nm minimum spacing between V1 instances on the same M2 track, 27 nm between instances on parallel non-aligned tracks, and 18 nm between instances on parallel aligned tracks. The mask-based rule distinguishes via instances with a 5 nm M2 end-cap (wec) from those without (nec). For same-track violations, the correction is a single-axis x-move of one or both via instances in opposite directions. The paired-push pattern (one instance +36, another -36 dbu) appears in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06 and resolves the violation symmetrically without shifting the net center of the via pair.

For parallel-track violations (27 nm cross-track rule), y-axis separation of the two tracks must be verified to meet the projection-measured 17 nm threshold that feeds V1.S.1. Trials that apply y-corrections of 44-64 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0021.14, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0035.18) address this category.

## V1.S.2, V1.S.3, V1.S.4: Corner-to-Corner Spacing

V1.S.2 (23 nm Euclidean corner-to-corner between two wec vias), V1.S.3 (30 nm Euclidean corner-to-corner between two nec vias), and V1.S.4 (27 nm Euclidean corner-to-corner between one wec and one nec via) are triggered by diagonal proximity. These require that both the x-projection and the Euclidean distance exceed the threshold. Repairs involving diagonal moves (combined x and y delta_dbu in a single move_instance) address this geometry: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03 applies [40, 0] and also [0, -12] in the same operation set; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row22.12 applies [36, -48] to instance i0132 in one move, which simultaneously increases x-separation and y-separation to push the Euclidean distance above the rule threshold. Use combined x-y moves when the violation is diagonal (non-projection-dominated); use pure x or y moves when the projection check is the binding constraint.

## V1.M2.AUX.2: Width Matching

V1.M2.AUX.2 requires that V1 exactly matches M2 width in the direction perpendicular to M2 length. Violations arise when a resize_end applied to M2 changes the wire width rather than extending the end. All accepted resize_end operations in the history act along the axis parallel to the M2 run direction (x for horizontal M2, y for vertical M2), not perpendicular. Never use resize_end to change the M2 width (perpendicular dimension); use it only to extend or retract the M2 end length. This distinction is consistent across all accepted resize_end operations in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24, and trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25.

## GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL

The nonorthogonal block flags any edge with angle not in {0, 90, 180, 270} degrees. All accepted operations in the history produce rectilinear geometry: move_instance translations preserve polygon shape, and all resize_end and polygon move operations act along a single axis. Do not introduce diagonal edges on V1 or any connected layer; all polygon corners must remain on the rectilinear grid. This constraint is implicitly satisfied by the move_instance + resize_end repair vocabulary used in all 40 accepted trials.
