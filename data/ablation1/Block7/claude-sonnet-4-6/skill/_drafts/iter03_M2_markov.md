**Spacing rule context and move sizing**

M2.S.1 sets 18 nm as the minimum side-to-side spacing for edges longer than 36 nm. M2.S.2 requires 25 nm tip-to-side. M2.S.3 requires 27 nm tip-to-tip when both tips are 24–36 nm long. M2.S.4 requires 31 nm tip-to-tip when both tips are under 24 nm. M2.S.5 requires 31 nm between a 24–36 nm tip and a sub-24 nm tip. M2.S.6 requires 20 nm corner-to-corner (euclidian). M2.S.7 forbids a tip-to-tip gap of 18 nm when the side-to-side spacing is also ≤ 32 nm, unless the parallel run length is ≥ 35 nm. M2.S.8 requires 80 nm diagonal center-to-center distance between tip-to-tip gaps on different tracks. The 36 dbu move magnitude, accepted across the largest number of trials (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row18.08, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row6.08, trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row17.02, trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row24.05, and many others), exceeds the 18 nm side-to-side minimum by a factor of two, providing margin against spacing violations when shifting a wire relative to neighbors. Moves of 28 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row6.18, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row14.01) exceed the 25 nm tip-to-side minimum. Moves of 40 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19) exceed all pairwise spacing minima defined by M2.S.1 through M2.S.5. Small moves of 4 dbu and 8 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11, trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row14.01 i0874) fall below the 18 nm side-to-side minimum and were accepted, indicating they are fine-tuning adjustments in contexts where the dominant spacing constraint is not M2.S.1.
