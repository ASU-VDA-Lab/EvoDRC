## Operation Mechanics and Grid Quantization

All accepted repairs in this history use only axis-aligned (orthogonal) move_instance and resize_end/move operations on M1 polygons. No non-orthogonal edge is introduced across any trial. The NONORTHOGONAL rule applies to M1; every gated-in repair respects this by construction.

The dominant x-displacement quantum across this history is 36 dbu. In trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05, seven instances are each moved exactly +36 dbu in x. In trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row6.08, five instances are each moved +36 dbu in x. In trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01 and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06, and trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row21.05, the same 36 dbu quantum is applied uniformly. This 36 dbu step equals the minimum M1 width (M1.W.1: 18 nm) plus minimum M1 side-to-side spacing (M1.S.1: 18 nm), i.e., one minimum pitch unit. Moving instances by this quantum shifts all downstream M1 segments by one pitch, resolving side-to-side spacing violations without introducing new width violations.

Smaller quantized steps also appear. Adjustments of 4 dbu occur in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row10.00 (resize_end x+4 on p3305) and trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11 (move_instance x+4 on i0100). Steps of 8 dbu appear in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row12.02 (y-axis resize_end ±8 on p3694) and trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row14.01 (x+8 on instances and resize_end). These fine adjustments target enclosure margins (V0.M1.EN.1 requires 5 nm; a 4–8 nm step directly closes a near-miss) rather than full pitch realignment. Apply pitch-quantum (36 dbu) steps when resolving M1.S.1 spacing violations; apply fine steps only when the shortfall is measured at less than one pitch correction.

Large moves of 64, 72, 104, 108 dbu are used where multiple pitch violations coexist or where enclosure shortfalls are severe. trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row7.19 moves i1446 by +108 dbu x; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 moves i1117 by +108 dbu x and i1168/i1271 by +56 dbu x; trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.01 moves i1140 by +108 dbu x and i1144 by +60 dbu x. A step of 108 dbu equals three times the minimum pitch (3×36), indicating three accumulated pitch-unit violations resolved in a single move. Use multi-pitch steps when geometric analysis shows stacking of violations; avoid them when only one pitch unit is needed, as over-correction can place M1 into a new spacing conflict on the far side.

## Enclosure Repair: V0.M1.EN.1 and V1.M1.EN.1

When a V0 or V1 via is moved relative to its enclosing M1 rectangle, enclosure on the trailing edge drops. The standard repair is resize_end on the M1 polygon's high end in the direction of instance displacement. trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row3.15 moves i1623 by -36 dbu x then extends p3379 high-x by +49 dbu, net M1 extension of 13 dbu beyond the instance shift, sufficient to restore ≥5 nm enclosure. trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.23 moves i1646 by +72 dbu x then extends p3695 high-x by +128 dbu; trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.24 moves i1968 by +108 dbu x and extends p3771 high-x by +164 dbu. In all three cases the resize_end delta exceeds the instance displacement, confirming the enclosure extension must overshoot the displacement by at least 5 nm (the V0.M1.EN.1 requirement) to satisfy the projection check on both opposite sides.

V1.M1.EN.1 requires 5 nm on one axis and 2 nm on the other. When M1 is shared between a V0 below and V1 above, a single instance move can violate both enclosure rules simultaneously. trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 applies resize_end in both x (+36 on p3538 high) and y (+44 on p3538 high, +20 on p2333 high) alongside a -16 dbu x instance move and -52 dbu resize_end low on p3706, touching M1, M2, M3, V1 layers. This multi-axis, multi-polygon repair confirms that when a unit carries both V0 and V1, M1 enclosure must be satisfied simultaneously on both the x and y axes.

Always pair an instance move with a compensating resize_end on the M1 polygon's trailing edge. The resize_end delta must be the instance displacement plus at least the rule margin (5 nm for V0.M1.EN.1 on the tight axis). Do not apply only the instance move without the compensating resize_end; the history contains no accepted trial that does so when a via is present.

## V0.M1.AUX.3 and Width Maintenance

V0.M1.AUX.3 requires V0 to match M1 width exactly in the perpendicular direction. Resizing M1 width in isolation violates this rule if V0 is not also resized. All resize_end operations on M1 in this history are along the M1 length axis (extending an endpoint), not perpendicular to length. This preserves the M1 width seen by V0. Avoid resize_end operations that change the M1 dimension perpendicular to its long axis unless the V0 within it is repositioned simultaneously; no such violation-introducing operation appears in the accepted trials.

## Spacing Rule Repair: M1.S.1 Through M1.S.3

M1.S.1 (18 nm side-to-side for edges >36 nm), M1.S.2 (25 nm tip-to-side), and M1.S.3 (27 nm tip-to-tip for edges 24–36 nm) are the spacing rules most directly addressed by the 36 dbu move quantum. Moving an instance by 36 dbu in x separates two parallel M1 runs by one full pitch, clearing M1.S.1 by construction when the original separation was zero (merged shapes). The trials involving purely uniform x-moves of a set of instances (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row15.05, trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row6.08, trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row11.01) all achieve n_new_in_crop=0, confirming that group-uniform 36 dbu displacements do not create new spacing violations within the crop.

When instances in the same row move by different amounts (e.g., trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row16.06: i0407 at -36, i0928 at -36, i0320 at +40), non-uniform displacements are required to separate specific M1 tip pairs. The +40 dbu move (larger than 36 dbu by 4 dbu) on i0320 resolves a residual gap to meet M1.S.2 or M1.S.3 after the -36 moves cleared the primary M1.S.1 constraint. When adjacent-instance moves differ, apply the larger displacement to the instance whose M1 tip faces a long side, satisfying M1.S.2 (25 nm tip-to-side) rather than M1.S.1 (18 nm side-to-side).

## Coordinated Multi-Instance Moves

Multiple instances within a single crop are moved together in the same trial when their M1 geometries are coupled — either connected by metal run continuity or sharing a common spacing violation with a third polygon. trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 moves eight distinct elements (two instances y+64, then y-12, then three instances x+28, then resize_end x+48 on p3200, one more instance x+28) within a single trial. This compound operation preserves connectivity (conn_preserved=true, n_new_in_crop=0), confirming that within a single locus crop, mixed x+y moves and resize_end on M1 are safe when all coupled instances are moved together.

Do not move only one instance from a coupled group: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11 moves a single instance by only x+4 dbu and introduces 9 new in-crop violations (n_new_in_crop=9), the highest count in the history. The trial was still gated_in (conn_preserved=true), but the new violations required subsequent repair in iterations 2 and onward. Prefer moving all instances in the coupled group by the same displacement in the same trial to minimize cascading new violations.

## Y-Direction Corrections and Enclosure Asymmetry

Several trials apply corrections purely or primarily in the y-direction, indicating row-to-row M1 spacing or vertical enclosure failures. trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row14.04 moves two instances +64 dbu y. trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09 moves two instances -52 dbu y. trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row14.01 moves one instance +64 dbu y with additional x+8 corrections. The y-delta of 44 dbu recurs: trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row22.12 (i0132 at [+36,-48]), trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row23.13 (i0041 at [-36,+44]), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 ([+72,+44]), trial:i02.ug.leaf_0041.19 ([0,-44]), trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row13.00 ([0,+44]), trial:i04.ug.leaf_0006.04 ([0,-44]), trial:i03.ug.leaf_0010.08 ([+4,+44]). The 44 dbu y step addresses V0.M1.EN.1 or V1.M1.EN.1 vertical enclosure violations. The V0/V1 enclosure rule requires 5 nm on both opposite (horizontal) edges; a vertical shift adjusts enclosure on the top/bottom edges relative to horizontal-run M1 segments. Apply y-corrections in multiples near 44 dbu when the via enclosure violation is on the vertical axis of the M1 segment.

The shift from x-corrections (iterations 1–2) to y-corrections (iterations 3–4) indicates that x-direction M1.S.1 violations were cleared first, exposing underlying y-direction enclosure or spacing violations that were masked by the x-violations in earlier iterations. Expect to rotate from x-axis to y-axis correction in later iterations for units that require more than two iterations of repair.

Block7_union_row19 appears in iter1 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row19.09), iter2 (trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row19.04), and iter3 (trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row19.03). The return of the -52 dbu y move on the same instances (i0949, i0950) in both iter1 and iter3 means the iter2 x-correction re-introduced the y enclosure problem that was originally fixed in iter1. ## Polygon p3631 Oscillation

Polygon p3631 undergoes a full-move (op: move) in y across three iterations: +57 dbu in trial:i02.ug.Block7_union_row17.02; -57 dbu in trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row17.02; +68 dbu in trial:i04.ug.leaf_0007.05. The +57 then -57 is an exact reversal. In iteration 2, the surrounding instances i0794 and i0810 also move y+57; in iteration 3 they move y-57 in the same trial, and in iteration 4 they move y+68. This polygon has not converged after four iterations. When a polygon requires a full-move operation (as opposed to resize_end) and the move direction reverses between iterations, the locus contains at least two competing constraints of comparable severity on opposite sides. In such cases, a stable solution requires identifying both constraints simultaneously and selecting a y-position that satisfies both, rather than correcting for one at a time.

## Resize_end on M1 Low End

Contracting the M1 polygon low end appears in trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 (resize_end low x-36 on p3300) and trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.01 (resize_end low x-40 on p3297). These low-end contractions reduce the M1 segment length, trading potential M1.A.1 area risk against resolution of a spacing or enclosure conflict at the low end. In trial:i04.ug.leaf_0001.01, the low-end contraction of -40 dbu x is paired with a high-end extension of +108 dbu x on p3696 in the same trial, indicating a net rightward shift of the M1 polygon's position while maintaining similar total length. Apply low-end contraction only when paired with a high-end extension or when the resulting shortened M1 remains above the 504 nm-sq minimum area threshold (M1.A.1); no trial in the history applies a low-end contraction that reduces M1 below minimum area.

## Accepted New In-Crop Violations

The decision gate accepts trials with n_new_in_crop > 0 when conn_preserved=true. Trials with n_new_in_crop values of 1, 2, 4, 5, 9 are all gated_in across the history. The largest accepted count is 9 (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11, single op x+4 on i0100). This means that introducing new M1 violations within the crop is tolerated when the primary target violation is resolved and connectivity is maintained. However, trials that introduce 0 new in-crop violations (the majority) are preferred because they do not create cascading work for subsequent iterations. The trials that produce non-zero n_new_in_crop tend to involve fewer operations (trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row21.11 has 1 op; trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row13.03 has 5 ops and n_new_in_crop=1; trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 has 6 ops and n_new_in_crop=1). The single-op trial with 9 new violations confirms that minimal interventions on M1 in dense regions can have the highest coupling to surrounding violations.

## Multi-Layer Operation Coordination

Trials that touch M1, M2, M3, V1, and V2 simultaneously require coordinated moves across all layers. trial:i01.ug.Block7_union_row9.21 (9 ops, layers M1/M2/M3/V1/V2), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0024.25 (6 ops, layers M1/M2/M3/V1), and trial:i03.ug.Block7_union_row17.02 (9 ops, layers M1/M2/M3/V1/V2) all accept these as single gated-in repairs. When the M1 fix requires moving a via stack, all metal layers in that stack must be moved in the same trial to preserve connectivity. No trial in the history moves only M1 while leaving an attached V1 or M2 uncompensated; the conn_preserved=true result in every trial confirms that the via stack is always moved as a unit.
