## M3 Geometry Rules: Edge Classifications and Thresholds

M3 minimum width is 18 nm (M3.W.1) and minimum area is 504 nm² (M3.A.1). Spacing rules depend on edge length classification. Edges longer than 36 nm are classified as side edges; edges 36 nm or shorter are tip edges. Side-to-side spacing requires 18 nm (M3.S.1). Tip-to-side spacing requires 25 nm (M3.S.2). Tip-to-tip spacing is 27 nm when both tips fall in the 24–36 nm range (M3.S.3), and 31 nm when either or both tips are shorter than 24 nm (M3.S.4, M3.S.5). Corner-to-corner Euclidean spacing is 20 nm (M3.S.6). Because tips shorter than 24 nm invoke the strictest spacing rules (M3.S.4, M3.S.5 at 31 nm), any operation that shortens a polygon end below 24 nm crosses a threshold that substantially tightens the required clearance to neighbors.

## Bulk Y-Axis Polygon Shrinkage Causes M3.S.2 and M3.S.4 Violations

A repair that applied a uniform -64 dbu y-axis resize to 39 M3 polygons over a wide locus produced 10 new M3.S.2 violations and 1 new M3.S.4 violation within crop (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0045.20). Do not apply bulk symmetric shrinks to M3 polygons across a wide locus; shortening polygon ends simultaneously creates narrow tips that fall below the 36 nm and 24 nm edge-length thresholds, generating tip-to-side (M3.S.2, 25 nm) and narrow-tip-to-tip (M3.S.4, 31 nm) spacing failures at multiple sites in a single operation.
