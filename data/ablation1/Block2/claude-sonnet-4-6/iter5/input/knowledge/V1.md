**Compound repair when move-instance is not sufficient**

When an initial move-instance attempt did not fully clear the violation, the solver escalated to a compound sequence: delete the offending polygon, delete the associated instance, insert a replacement via cell at the corrected origin via `add_via`, and resize the adjacent polygon end with `resize_end` to close the resulting gap (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.00). The same unit had first received a simpler move-instance plus M1 `add_polygon` in the preceding iteration (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.03), and the compound sequence in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.00 was required to complete the repair. Use the compound delete-instance / add_via / resize_end sequence only after a move-instance pass has proven insufficient; trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.03 and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.00 together establish that this escalation path is valid and accepted.
