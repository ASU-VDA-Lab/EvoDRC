## Spacing Constraints and End-Cap Classification

V2.S.1 governs projection-based spacing: 18 nm on the same M3 track, 18 nm for aligned parallel tracks, and 27 nm for non-aligned parallel tracks. V2.S.2 sets a euclidean corner-to-corner minimum of 23 nm when both instances carry a 5 nm M3 end-cap (the wec class). V2.S.3 sets a euclidean corner-to-corner minimum of 30 nm when both instances lack a 5 nm M3 end-cap (the nec class). V2.S.4 sets a euclidean corner-to-corner minimum of 27 nm for mixed wec/nec pairs. The DRC deck computes per-instance end-cap class from coincident M3 edge coverage, so the applicable spacing rule changes when M3 geometry is extended or retracted; resizing V2 shapes in x by +288 dbu per shape, as done in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, changes both the V2 footprint and its effective end-cap classification.
