## Observed repair patterns for layer V3

**Multi-layer co-moves are the dominant repair vehicle for V3.**
In both accepted trials, V3 was a touched layer only as a consequence of repositioning its enclosing metal tiers; no operation targeted a V3 polygon directly. Instance moves in the Y direction (multiples of 24 dbu) shifted entire stacks that included M3, M4, and V3 together (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02). Isolating V3 while leaving its enclosing M3 or M4 unchanged was not the repair path taken in any measured record.

**M4 end-resizing couples with V3 enclosure and spacing constraints.**
trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 resized polygon p937 on the X axis (64 dbu at the low end, 320 dbu at the high end) while simultaneously moving eight instances in Y. M4 was among the touched layers, and V3.M4.EN.2 (minimum 11 nm M4 enclosure of V3 on two opposite sides) and V3.M4.AUX.2 (V3 width must match M4 width along the perpendicular direction) are directly sensitive to X-axis M4 end adjustments. The trial was accepted with connectivity preserved, establishing that asymmetric M4 end-cap changes do not automatically violate those rules when the remaining enclosure margin is sufficient.

**Accepted trials tolerate small in-crop violation counts.**
Both accepted trials carried n_new_in_crop=2 and n_new_out_of_crop=0 at decision time (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02). The gate-in criterion does not require zero new violations inside the crop; repairs that reduce the net violation count while preserving connectivity pass even when two in-crop violations are introduced.

**V3.AUX.1 and V3.M4.AUX.2 constrain the feasible move range.**
V3.AUX.1 requires every V3 instance to remain inside both M3 and M4. V3.M4.AUX.2 requires the V3 width perpendicular to M4 length to exactly match the M4 width. In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 the simultaneous Y moves of instances and X resize of the M4 polygon were accepted without triggering either rule, confirming that the repair preserved containment and width matching. Any Y move of an instance carrying V3 must therefore stay within the M3 and M4 extents or those extents must be extended by the same delta.

**Move granularity is 24 dbu.**
Every instance delta observed across the two trials is an integer multiple of 24 dbu: +/-24 dbu and +/-72 dbu in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01; +/-24 dbu in trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02. This is the finest move step seen in the measured record. V3 spacing rules (V3.S.1 minimum 18 nm, V3.S.2 minimum 23 nm corner-to-corner with end-cap, V3.S.3 minimum 30 nm corner-to-corner without end-cap, V3.S.4 minimum 27 nm mixed end-cap) and V3.M3.EN.1 (minimum 5 nm M3 enclosure on two opposite sides) are all sensitive to sub-nanometer positioning; moves smaller than 24 dbu have not been attempted in the measured record and should not be assumed safe.

Both trials selected for conn_preserved=true before the gated_in decision was recorded (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02). No trial with conn_preserved=false appears in the measured record for this layer.
