## Observed repair patterns for layer V3

**Multi-layer co-moves are the dominant repair vehicle for V3.**
In all four accepted trials, V3 was a touched layer only as a consequence of repositioning its enclosing metal tiers; no operation targeted a V3 polygon directly. Instance moves in the Y direction (multiples of 24 dbu) shifted entire stacks that included M3, M4, and V3 together (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.00, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0003.01). Isolating V3 while leaving its enclosing M3 or M4 unchanged was not the repair path taken in any measured record.

**M3 polygon addition is a confirmed companion operation when instances are moved large distances.**
trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.00 added three M3 polygons (heights 48 dbu, 96 dbu, and 48 dbu; width 360 dbu each) at Y positions interleaved among the moved instances, in the same trial that moved instances by ±48 dbu and ±96 dbu. The result was n_new_in_crop=0, the cleanest outcome in the measured record. The added strips appear to bridge M3 coverage gaps that opened when adjacent instances diverged in Y, keeping V3.M3.EN.1 (minimum 5 nm M3 enclosure on two opposite sides) satisfied. When a repair involves instance Y moves larger than ±48 dbu, inserting M3 fill polygons in the vacated gaps is an observed path to zero net new violations.

**M4 end-resizing couples with V3 enclosure and spacing constraints.**
trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 resized polygon p937 on the X axis (64 dbu at the low end, 320 dbu at the high end) while simultaneously moving eight instances in Y. M4 was among the touched layers, and V3.M4.EN.2 (minimum 11 nm M4 enclosure of V3 on two opposite sides) and V3.M4.AUX.2 (V3 width must match M4 width along the perpendicular direction) are directly sensitive to X-axis M4 end adjustments. The trial was accepted with connectivity preserved, establishing that asymmetric M4 end-cap changes do not automatically violate those rules when the remaining enclosure margin is sufficient.

**The gate-in decision reason is conn_preserved; accepted trials span n_new_in_crop from 0 to 13.**
All four accepted trials carry the explicit decision reason "conn_preserved" (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.00, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0003.01). The observed n_new_in_crop values across accepted trials are 2, 2, 0, and 13; n_new_out_of_crop is 0 in every case. The gate-in criterion is connectivity preservation, not a cap on in-crop violations. A trial introducing 13 new in-crop violations is accepted if connectivity is intact; a trial introducing zero new violations is also accepted. Minimizing n_new_in_crop is desirable but is not a hard gate.

**V3.AUX.1 and V3.M4.AUX.2 constrain the feasible move range.**
V3.AUX.1 requires every V3 instance to remain inside both M3 and M4. V3.M4.AUX.2 requires the V3 width perpendicular to M4 length to exactly match the M4 width. In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 the simultaneous Y moves of instances and X resize of the M4 polygon were accepted without triggering either rule, confirming that the repair preserved containment and width matching. Any Y move of an instance carrying V3 must therefore stay within the M3 and M4 extents, or those extents must be extended by the same delta (or supplemented with fill polygons as in trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.00).

**Move granularity is 24 dbu.**
Every instance delta observed across all four accepted trials is an integer multiple of 24 dbu: ±24 dbu, ±48 dbu, ±72 dbu, and ±96 dbu appear in the record (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.00, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0003.01). This is the finest move step seen in the measured record. V3 spacing rules (V3.S.1 minimum 18 nm, V3.S.2 minimum 23 nm corner-to-corner with end-cap, V3.S.3 minimum 30 nm corner-to-corner without end-cap, V3.S.4 minimum 27 nm mixed end-cap) and V3.M3.EN.1 (minimum 5 nm M3 enclosure on two opposite sides) are all sensitive to sub-nanometer positioning; moves smaller than 24 dbu have not been attempted in the measured record and should not be assumed safe.

**Connectivity preservation is a hard gate for V3 repairs.**
All four accepted trials were selected for conn_preserved=true before the gated_in decision was recorded (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01, trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0002.00, trial:i05.ug.leaf_0003.01). No trial with conn_preserved=false appears in the measured record for this layer. Do not apply any move or resize that disconnects a V3 net, even if it would resolve a spacing or enclosure violation.
