**Enclosure rules V2.M3.EN.2 and V2.M3.AUX.2**

V2.M3.EN.2 fires when M3 encloses V2 by less than 5 nm on both members of one opposite-side pair (the threshold is 5 & 5 nm or 5 & 0 nm, projection). V2.M3.AUX.2 fires on any V2 that does not share two coincident edges with M3 in the direction perpendicular to M3 length, i.e., any V2 whose perpendicular width differs from M3. When V2 shapes are narrower than their enclosing M3 in x, or shift off-center, both V2.M3.EN.2 and V2.M3.AUX.2 can fire simultaneously, producing paired violation counts. The −24 reduction achieved by symmetrically moving V2 shapes outward (±144 dbu) while widening each by +288 dbu in x confirms that expanding V2 width to match M3 extent in x and recentering the outer V2 shapes resolves both enclosure and width-match violations in a single pass (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00).

**Axis and direction for V2 resizes inside via cells**

Resizing V2 shapes in the x-axis by a uniform +288 dbu, combined with symmetric x-displacement of the outer shapes, achieved full violation clearance in both affected windows without connectivity loss (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00).
