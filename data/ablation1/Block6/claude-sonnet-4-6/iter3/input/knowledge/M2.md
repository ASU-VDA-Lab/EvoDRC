## M2.S.7 and M2.S.8 Interaction Awareness

M2.S.7 forbids an 18 nm tip-to-tip gap co-located with side-to-side spacing <= 32 nm unless parallel run length is >= 35 nm. M2.S.8 adds a minimum 80 nm diagonal center-to-center spacing between tip-to-tip gaps on different tracks.

In trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.03, extending the low end of p1920 by +60 dbu simultaneously with moves of +12 and -40 dbu on two instances resolved the local violation without triggering M2.S.7 or M2.S.8, establishing that a +60 dbu low-end extension is sufficient to satisfy the >= 35 nm run-length requirement in that geometry. Use a resize_end delta large enough that the resulting tip-to-tip gap is eliminated or the parallel context satisfies >= 35 nm before committing.
