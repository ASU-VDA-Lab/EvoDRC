## M2.S.7 and M2.S.8 Interaction Awareness

M2.S.7 forbids an 18 nm tip-to-tip gap that is co-located with a side-to-side spacing <= 32 nm unless the parallel run length is >= 35 nm. M2.S.8 adds a minimum 80 nm diagonal center-to-center spacing between tip-to-tip gaps on different tracks. In trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.03, two instances were displaced in opposite X directions (+12 and -40 dbu) while the M2 polygon end was simultaneously extended +60 dbu at its low end. This three-operation combination resolved the local violation without triggering M2.S.7 or M2.S.8, indicating that extending the low end of an M2 polygon by 60 dbu is sufficient to increase the parallel run length past the 35 nm M2.S.7 threshold in that geometry. Use a resize_end delta large enough that the resulting tip-to-tip gap is either eliminated or the parallel context satisfies the >= 35 nm run length requirement before committing the repair.

## Area Rule Consideration

M2.A.1 requires a minimum M2 polygon area of 504 nm2. Any resize_end operation that shrinks an M2 polygon risks violating this rule if the polygon is already near the minimum area. All three resize operations in this history used positive (extending) deltas and were accepted; do not apply negative resize_end deltas without verifying the post-resize area remains >= 504 nm2 (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.03, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row8.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.07).

## V2 Enclosure Awareness

V2.M2.EN.1 requires M2 to enclose V2 by at least 5 nm on at least two opposite sides. While no V2 layer appears in the `touched_layers` of any trial in this iteration, any M2 polygon resize that shortens a polygon end must verify that V2 vias attached at that end retain 5 nm enclosure on both directions.
