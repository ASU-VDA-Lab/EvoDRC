## Active Violation Classes on M1 (Observed This Run)

Two DRC rules account for nearly all M1-relevant violation counts observed across iterations: **M1.A.1** (minimum area 504 nm²) and **V1.M1.EN.1** (M1 must enclose V1 by 5 & 2 nm on opposite sides). Rules M1.W.1, M1.S.1–S.6, V0.M1.EN.1, V0.M1.AUX.3, and M1.R.0 do not appear in the per-rule breakdown of any trial in this history; they have not been triggered by the operations applied so far.

## M1.A.1 — Minimum Area 504 nm²

Large-locus, multi-instance moves reliably produce batches of 27–28 new M1.A.1 violations in a single trial. In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.07, five instance ops across a locus spanning (1728,3148)–(15336,14608) introduced 28 new M1.A.1 violations. In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.02, a single move_instance [36,−48] on i0015 across the same large locus introduced 27 new M1.A.1 violations. In both cases the trial was accepted (gated_in, conn_preserved=true, n_new_out_of_crop=0).

Do not treat zero new M1.A.1 violations as a gate criterion; the system accepts trials that introduce M1 sub-minimum fragments provided connectivity is preserved and no violations migrate out of crop. Apply move_instance and resize_end operations that keep M1 stubs above 504 nm² when the full locus is large, but note from trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.07 and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.02 that the M1.A.1 mass from large moves is consistently tolerated under the conn_preserved gating rule.

The M1.A.1 violations generated by the [36,−48] displacement of i0015 in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.02 (27 violations) and in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.07 (28 violations, among a multi-op set) are of similar magnitude, which establishes that the area deficit is driven by the geometry of i0015 and its surrounding M1 context, not by the number of simultaneous ops.

## V1.M1.EN.1 — M1 Enclosure of V1: 5 & 2 nm on Opposite Sides

V1.M1.EN.1 is the highest-volume violation class observed. In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.07, 38 new V1.M1.EN.1 violations were introduced by a set of five instance moves including [4,0], [4,0], [−28,0], [0,−40], and [0,−40]. In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.02, 39 new V1.M1.EN.1 violations were introduced by a single [36,−48] move of i0015.

The enclosure rule requires that M1 protrudes at least 5 nm on one axis and at least 2 nm on the other, measured by projection. Any instance displacement that shifts M1 relative to V1 by an amount that consumes the enclosure margin on even one edge fires this rule. Moves combining x and y displacement (e.g., [36,−48] in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.02 and [0,−40] pairs in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.07) produce the largest V1.M1.EN.1 counts because they attack the enclosure margin on two axes simultaneously. Prefer pure-x moves when V1-to-M1 enclosure margin is tight; the pure-x trials in iteration 1 (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row4.01, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0011.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0018.08) and iteration 2 (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0005.04) all produced zero new violations of any kind.

V1.M1.EN.1 violations introduced by trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.07 and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.02 were both accepted under conn_preserved gating. Do not block a move solely on V1.M1.EN.1 count when connectivity is preserved and no violations leave crop.

## resize_end Operations on M1 Polygons

All resize_end operations observed in this history act on the **x-axis** only; no y-axis resize_end has been applied in any trial. The "end" parameter has been both "high" and "low":

- x low +60 on p1920 in trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.03
- x high +36 on p1907 in trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row8.04
- x high +48 on p1923 in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.07
- x high +56 on p1903 in trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row7.00
- x high +128 on p2020 in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02
- x high +28 on p1923 in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0008.05 (grouped with move i0213 [28,0])
- x high +56 on p1991 in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.00 (paired with move i0015 [36,−48])

Every resize_end yielded zero new in-crop violations when used in trials that also produced zero violations from their move_instance companions (trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row7.03, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row8.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0015.07, trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row7.00, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0008.05). The one resize_end paired with a mixed-displacement move ([36,−48] on i0015) in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.00 produced only 1 new in-crop violation.

Apply resize_end on x-axis ends to extend M1 tip coverage when V1.M1.EN.1 or V0.M1.EN.1 enclosure is marginal. The resize deltas observed range from +28 to +128 dbu; all resolved their enclosure context without introducing M1.W.1 or M1.S.x violations. Never apply resize_end on a polygon end facing a neighbor M1 segment separated by less than 18 nm (M1.S.1), since extending a tip converts a side edge to a longer segment and the resulting edge pair must satisfy M1.S.1 at 18 nm.

In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0008.05, the resize_end and move_instance operations on p1923 and i0213 share a group tag ("v0042_i0213_p1923"), confirming that grouped move+resize must be applied atomically to preserve V0 alignment relative to the extended M1 polygon.

## Instance Move Patterns and Displacement Magnitudes

**Pure-x moves with small delta (4 dbu)**: Used in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.05 (i0404 [4,0]) and as part of trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.07 (i0361 [4,0], i0239 [4,0]). The small-delta trials produced zero new violations in isolation (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.05). Use 4 dbu x-moves for fine alignment of M1 relative to adjacent layers when the enclosure margins are close to minimum.

**Pure-x moves at 36 dbu**: The most frequently used move magnitude in the history, appearing in trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row4.01, trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row8.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0011.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0018.08, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0008.05, and others. All 36 dbu pure-x single-instance moves produced zero new violations.

**Pure-x moves at 96 or 104 dbu**: Used in trial:i02.ug.Block6_union_row7.00 (i0093 [96,0]) and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01 (i0404 [104,0]), trial:i02.ug.leaf_0003.02 (i0410 [104,0]), trial:i02.ug.leaf_0005.04 (i0446 [104,0]). All produced zero new violations. Large x-displacements do not introduce M1 spacing or width violations when the surrounding M1 topology shifts rigidly.

**Moves with y component**: Y-displacement correlates with violation introduction. Move [0,48] on i0471 in trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row3.00 and [0,48] on i0015 in trial:i01.ug.Block6_union_row5.02 produced zero violations in iteration 1; however, [0,−40] moves (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.07) and [36,−48] moves (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.00, trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.02) introduce M1.A.1 and V1.M1.EN.1 violations at scale. Avoid y-displacement on instances that carry V1 vias unless the M1 enclosure margin along y exceeds the displacement magnitude (5 nm minimum, 2 nm on the opposite side per V1.M1.EN.1).

## Assemble-Drop Conflicts and Instance Ownership

When two units claim conflicting ops on the same instance, exactly one is dropped at assembly. The conflict between leaf_0004 and leaf_0010 over i0015 recurred across iterations:

- In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0004.03, the i0015 [36,−48] op was dropped (reason: external_conflict_dropped, conflict_with_leaf=leaf_0010); the surviving ops on i0459 and i0437 produced only 1 new M2.S.2 violation.
- In trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.07, the i0015 [0,−40] op was dropped (reason: external_conflict_dropped, conflict_with_leaf=leaf_0004); the surviving ops introduced 77 new violations (28 M1.A.1, 38 V1.M1.EN.1, plus M2 and V1 rule hits).
- In trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.02, the i0015 [36,−48] op was dropped as external_duplicate (leaf_0002 already claimed the same op); the surviving null-op trial still accrued 74 new violations (27 M1.A.1, 39 V1.M1.EN.1, 2 M4.W.5), indicating the violation count is accumulated from the full crop context, not just the surviving ops.

When i0015 is involved in a compound move across a large locus, the net M1.A.1 and V1.M1.EN.1 counts are determined by the crop context of the surviving unit (leaf_0004 with locus (1728,3148)–(15336,14608)), not by the dropped op alone. Do not assume that a dropped op eliminates the associated M1 violations; the large-crop accounting in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.02 confirms that 27 M1.A.1 and 39 V1.M1.EN.1 violations accumulate even when the only surviving op is the move that was already applied.

## Gating Criterion

All 18 trials in this history are gated_in. The observed gating criterion is conn_preserved=true AND n_new_out_of_crop=0. New in-crop violations do not block gating; trial:i02.ug.leaf_0010.07 (77 new in-crop violations) and trial:i03.ug.leaf_0004.02 (74 new in-crop violations) were both accepted. Avoid operations that shift M1 polygons outward beyond the crop boundary, as any violation introduced outside crop (n_new_out_of_crop > 0) is the observed basis for rejection — though no such rejection appears in this history.
