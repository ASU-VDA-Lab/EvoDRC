## M3 Via-Shape Resizing via cu_pool

Shrinking the M3 shape of cell VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 along the y-axis by 40 dbu produced a net violation reduction of 8 in iteration 3 (trial:i03.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, delta_total=-8, leaf_0001: 18->10, leaf_0002: 16->16 unchanged). The identical single-op shrink on the same cell in iteration 2 (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00) produced delta_total=0 and was rejected as not net-positive. Cu_pool rejection of an M3 via-shape op in one iteration does not preclude its success in a later iteration after the design state advances; re-attempt isolated single-op shrinks on subsequent iterations rather than discarding them permanently.

Do not bundle a VIA_VIA23 M3 y-shrink with large M3 resizes on other via cells and simultaneous polygon end-resizes in a single trial. The 10-op combined attempt in iteration 2 (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01) -- resizing VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52 M3 by y=-112 dbu, VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 M3 by y=-40 dbu, and six end-resize ops on p891, p892, p893 at y=+/-32 dbu -- produced delta_total=+5 (leaf_0005: 20->28, leaf_0006: 16->13), a net worsening. Expanding the concurrent scope of M3 shrink ops across multiple via cells in a single cu_pool trial introduces more violations than it resolves.

Expanding an M3 via shape as part of a coordinated chain fix across adjacent via and metal layers is effective. Resizing the VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52 M3 shape by y=+48 dbu together with matching V3 resizes (+88 dbu each on two V3 shapes) and an M4 resize (+88 dbu) in iteration 1 (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.00) reduced total block violations by 13 (leaf_0009: 25->18, leaf_0010: 25->19) and was applied. Chain fixes that expand M3 while coordinating V3 and M4 achieve larger reductions than isolated M3-only adjustments and carry no net-positive risk in the recorded cases.
