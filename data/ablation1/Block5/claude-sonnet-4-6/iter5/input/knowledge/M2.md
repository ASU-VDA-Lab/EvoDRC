## Repair Operation Patterns

Accepted trials across iterations 1-3 that were fully applied used strictly orthogonal operations. Unit-gate channel trials have all used X-axis-only `move_instance` (and, where enclosure demands it, X-axis `resize_end`), consistent with the GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL constraint that forbids non-axis-aligned edges on M2 (trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row3.00, trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row6.01, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.02, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.03, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0005.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0006.05, trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.02). Y-axis operations applied directly to M2 polygon edges appeared only in a rejected trial (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01); no accepted trial validates direct Y-axis modification of M2 polygon edges. By contrast, a Y-axis `resize_via_shape` applied to an M3 cell shape (VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36) was accepted in the cu_pool channel (trial:i03.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00); the distinction between M3 cell shape modification and direct M2 edge geometry modification is load-bearing.

## Decision States: applied vs. gated_in

A `decision: gated_in` result means the trial preserved connectivity (`conn_preserved: true`) but introduced at least one new DRC violation inside the crop window (`n_new_in_crop >= 1`) and was therefore not committed. trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 was gated_in with `n_new_in_crop: 1` after moving single instance i0111 by +36 dbu on leaf_0002 locus [1728,3148,9072,7652]. A `decision: applied` result requires both `conn_preserved: true` and `n_new_in_crop: 0`. Do not treat a gated_in trial as a validated repair; it confirms the operation was unsafe at that locus.

## move_instance

`move_instance` is the dominant repair operation for unit-gate channel trials. Confirmed accepted X-axis move deltas include +4 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row6.01), +8 dbu (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01), +36 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row3.00, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.02, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.03, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0005.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0006.05), +72 dbu (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.02), and +104 dbu (trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00). Apply `move_instance` in the +X direction first when resolving M2 spacing or enclosure violations.

When two adjacent instances both participate in a spacing conflict, move them in opposite directions simultaneously: trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.03 applied +36 dbu to i0056 and -36 dbu to i0103 in a single accepted trial, confirming that bidirectional instance spreading is valid and does not generate new violations. When two instances share the same violation locus and the same net context, moving both by the same positive-X vector is equally valid, as confirmed by trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00 (i0025 and i0019 each +104 dbu) and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01 (i0104 and i0061 each +8 dbu).

Moving only one of a geometrically coupled pair of instances can introduce new violations even when the step size is otherwise proven. trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 moved single instance i0111 by +36 dbu at leaf_0002 locus [1728,3148,9072,7652] and was gated_in with n_new_in_crop: 1. The prior accepted repair at leaf_0002 (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.03) moved both participating instances. Move the full set of instances participating in the locus; do not move a subset.

The small step sizes (+4 dbu, +8 dbu) have each been validated in one specific locus; the history does not establish them as universally safe for tip-to-tip violations. The +36 dbu step exceeds the strictest tip-to-tip spacing requirement (31 nm) and is the most broadly validated step size, but it is not unconditionally safe when applied to only a subset of participating instances (trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01).

## resize_end

`resize_end` on the `x` axis, `high` end, was used alongside `move_instance` in three accepted trials. trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row3.00 extended polygon p967 by 36 dbu at its high-X end while moving two instances by +36 dbu. trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.02 extended polygon p974 by 72 dbu at its high-X end while moving one instance by +36 dbu. trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00 extended polygon p955 by 20 dbu at its high-X end while moving two instances by +104 dbu. All three were accepted with no new violations. The resize magnitude does not need to equal the instance move delta; a resize smaller than the accompanying move is valid when the resulting geometry satisfies enclosure rules (trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00, resize 20 dbu vs. move 104 dbu). Apply `resize_end` on the high-X end when a polygon must be lengthened to restore V1 or V2 enclosure after an instance is displaced.

Y-axis `resize_end` applied directly to M2 polygon endpoints appeared only in the rejected trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01, which applied +/-32 dbu at both low and high Y ends of M2 polygons p891, p892, and p893. This trial was not accepted; direct Y-axis `resize_end` on M2 polygon geometry is not confirmed as a safe operation.

`resize_via_shape` on an M3 cell shape (Y-axis, -40 dbu, cell VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36, shape_index 0) was accepted in trial:i03.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, reducing violations in unit leaf_0001 by 8 (from 18 to 10) while touching layers M2/M3/V2, with conn_preserved: true and n_new_in_crop: 0. The operation modifies the M3 shape within the via cell definition; M2 is a touched layer only indirectly through V2 enclosure geometry, not through direct M2 edge manipulation. The prior rejections of iteration-2 trials on the same target (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 and trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01) can no longer be attributed solely to the use of Y-axis operations on M3; the specific delta and shape parameters in iteration 3 differ, and the iteration 3 result was applied cleanly.

## Connectivity Preservation

Every applied trial reported `conn_preserved: true` and `n_new_in_crop: 0, n_new_out_of_crop: 0`. The gated_in trial:i03.ug.leaf_0002.01 also had `conn_preserved: true` but was blocked by `n_new_in_crop: 1`; connectivity preservation is necessary but not sufficient for a trial to be applied. Keeping `move_instance` deltas consistent across all instances sharing a net preserves connectivity, whether by moving all connected instances by the same vector (trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01) or by pairing opposing moves (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.03). Resize operations that extend a polygon toward the displaced instance preserve net connectivity, as confirmed by trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row3.00, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.02, and trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00.

## Enclosure Rules (V1.M2.EN.2, V1.M2.AUX.2, V2.M2.EN.1)

The enclosure rules V1.M2.EN.2 (5 nm minimum enclosure of V1 by M2 on two opposite sides) and V1.M2.AUX.2 (V1 width must match M2 width perpendicular to M2 length) impose a tight coupling between M2 polygon extent and V1 placement. The `resize_end` operations in trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row3.00 (+36 dbu), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.02 (+72 dbu), and trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00 (+20 dbu) all extended M2 in the same direction as the accompanying instance moves, confirming that extending M2 toward the displaced V1 is the correct direction to satisfy V1.M2.EN.2 without creating new violations. Do not move an instance away from an M2 polygon endpoint without either also moving the polygon or extending it; the accepted trials show that paired `move_instance` + `resize_end` sequences resolve this coupling cleanly.

## Spacing Rule Context

M2 side-to-side spacing (M2.S.1) requires 18 nm minimum between edges longer than 36 nm. Tip-to-side (M2.S.2) requires 25 nm. Tip-to-tip rules vary by tip width: both edges >= 24 nm and <= 36 nm require 27 nm (M2.S.3); mixed wide/narrow tips require 31 nm (M2.S.5); both tips < 24 nm require 31 nm (M2.S.4). The compound rule M2.S.7 forbids any 18 nm tip-to-tip gap co-located with a side spacing <= 32 nm unless parallel run length is >= 35 nm. The accepted move deltas of +36 dbu, +72 dbu, and +104 dbu all exceed the strictest tip-to-tip spacing requirement (31 nm), making each a reliable step size for resolving both side-to-side and tip-to-tip violations when applied to the complete set of participating instances (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0005.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0006.05, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.02, trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00). The smaller steps of +4 dbu and +8 dbu were sufficient in their specific loci (trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row6.01, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01) but the history does not establish them as generally safe step sizes for tip-to-tip violations.
