## Repair Operation Patterns

All accepted trials across iterations 1 and 2 used strictly orthogonal, X-axis-only operations on M2-touching units and were accepted with zero new DRC violations (trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row3.00, trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row6.01, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.02, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.03, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0005.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0006.05, trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.02). No Y-axis displacements or diagonal moves appeared in any accepted trial, consistent with the GEOMETRY.NONORTHOGONAL constraint that forbids non-axis-aligned edges on M2. Y-axis resize operations on M2 polygons appeared only in rejected trials (trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01); no accepted trial validates Y-axis modifications to M2 geometry.

## move_instance

`move_instance` is the dominant repair operation. Confirmed accepted X-axis move deltas include +4 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row6.01), +8 dbu (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01), +36 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row3.00, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.02, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.03, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0005.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0006.05), +72 dbu (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.02), and +104 dbu (trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00). All of these preserve connectivity and introduce no new violations on the touched layers. Apply `move_instance` in the +X direction first when resolving M2 spacing or enclosure violations.

When two adjacent instances both participate in a spacing conflict, move them in opposite directions simultaneously: trial:i01.ug.leaf_0002.03 applied +36 dbu to i0056 and -36 dbu to i0103 in a single accepted trial, confirming that bidirectional instance spreading is valid and does not generate new violations. When two instances share the same violation locus and the same net context, moving both by the same positive-X vector is equally valid, as confirmed by trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00 (i0025 and i0019 each +104 dbu) and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01 (i0104 and i0061 each +8 dbu).

The small step sizes (+4 dbu, +8 dbu) have each been validated in one specific locus; the history does not establish them as universally safe for tip-to-tip violations. The +36 dbu step, larger than the strictest tip-to-tip spacing requirement (31 nm), remains the most broadly validated step size.

## resize_end

`resize_end` on the `x` axis, `high` end, was used alongside `move_instance` in three accepted trials. trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row3.00 extended polygon p967 by 36 dbu at its high-X end while moving two instances by +36 dbu. trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.02 extended polygon p974 by 72 dbu at its high-X end while moving one instance by +36 dbu. trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00 extended polygon p955 by 20 dbu at its high-X end while moving two instances by +104 dbu. All three were accepted with no new violations. The resize magnitude does not need to equal the instance move delta; a resize smaller than the accompanying move is valid when the resulting geometry satisfies enclosure rules (trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00, resize 20 dbu vs. move 104 dbu). Apply `resize_end` on the high-X end when a polygon must be lengthened to restore V1 or V2 enclosure after an instance is displaced.

Y-axis `resize_end` on M2 polygons appeared only in the rejected trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01, which applied +/-32 dbu at both low and high Y ends of polygons p891, p892, and p893. This trial was not accepted; Y-axis `resize_end` on M2 is not confirmed as a safe operation.

## Enclosure Rules (V1.M2.EN.2, V1.M2.AUX.2, V2.M2.EN.1)

The enclosure rules V1.M2.EN.2 (5 nm minimum enclosure of V1 by M2 on two opposite sides) and V1.M2.AUX.2 (V1 width must match M2 width perpendicular to M2 length) impose a tight coupling between M2 polygon extent and V1 placement. The `resize_end` operations in trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row3.00 (+36 dbu), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0001.02 (+72 dbu), and trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00 (+20 dbu) all extended M2 in the same direction as the accompanying instance moves, confirming that extending M2 toward the displaced V1 is the correct direction to satisfy V1.M2.EN.2 without creating new violations. Do not move an instance away from an M2 polygon endpoint without either also moving the polygon or extending it; the accepted trials show that paired `move_instance` + `resize_end` sequences resolve this coupling cleanly.

## Spacing Rule Context

M2 side-to-side spacing (M2.S.1) requires 18 nm minimum between edges longer than 36 nm. Tip-to-side (M2.S.2) requires 25 nm. Tip-to-tip rules vary by tip width: both edges >= 24 nm and <= 36 nm require 27 nm (M2.S.3); mixed wide/narrow tips require 31 nm (M2.S.5); both tips < 24 nm require 31 nm (M2.S.4). The compound rule M2.S.7 forbids any 18 nm tip-to-tip gap co-located with a side spacing <= 32 nm unless parallel run length is >= 35 nm. The accepted move deltas of +36 dbu, +72 dbu, and +104 dbu all exceed the strictest tip-to-tip spacing requirement (31 nm), making each a reliable step size for resolving both side-to-side and tip-to-tip violations (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0005.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0006.05, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0002.02, trial:i02.ug.Block5_union_row6.00). The smaller steps of +4 dbu and +8 dbu were sufficient in their specific loci (trial:i01.ug.Block5_union_row6.01, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01) but the history does not establish them as generally safe step sizes for tip-to-tip violations.
