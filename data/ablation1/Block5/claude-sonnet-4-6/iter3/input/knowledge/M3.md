**Channel Behavior: unit_gate vs cu_pool**

The unit_gate channel accepts M3 operations with decision=gated_in when conn_preserved=true, even when new in-crop violations are introduced. Trial:i01.ug.leaf_0010.07 moved p910 by +8 dbu on x and was gated_in despite introducing 3 new in-crop violations, because conn_preserved=true. Trial:i02.ug.leaf_0005.03 resized p897-p894 by -64 dbu on y and was gated_in despite 2 new in-crop violations (V1.M1.EN.1), again because conn_preserved=true.

The cu_pool channel rejects operations whose delta_total is zero or positive. Trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 achieved delta_total=0 and was rejected_net_positive. Trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 achieved delta_total=+5 and was rejected_net_positive. Only trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.00, which achieved delta_total=-13, was applied.

**M3 Via Shape Resizes: Positive y-Growth**

Positive y-axis growth of an M3 via shape is accepted by cu_pool when it is issued as part of a chain_fix group that simultaneously resizes the associated V3 shapes and the upper metal layer via shapes. Trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.00 grew VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52's M3 shape by +48 dbu on y alongside V3 shape resizes of +88 dbu and M4/V4 shape resizes of +88 dbu; the combined group achieved delta_total=-13 (decision=applied). No positive y-growth operation on an M3 via shape has been tested without a corresponding multi-layer chain; the only accepted M3 via shape growth in this history (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.00) was always part of that coupled group.

**M3 Via Shape Resizes: Negative y-Shrinkage**

Negative y-axis shrinkage of M3 via shapes for VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 does not reduce violations. Trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 shrunk the M3 via shape by -40 dbu on y as the sole operation and achieved delta_total=0 (rejected_net_positive). Trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 expanded the operation to also shrink VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52's M3 via shape by -112 dbu on y and resize_end wires p893, p892, and p891 by -32 dbu on both y ends, and achieved delta_total=+5 (rejected_net_positive, net worse). Do not apply negative y-shrinkage to VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36's M3 via shape; both single and combined forms worsened or neutralized the violation count per trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 and trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01.

**Assembler Drop Behavior**

The unit_gate assembler excludes cu_pool-rejected ops from combined patches before gating. Trial:i02.ug.leaf_0005.03 records an assemble_drops entry for the VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 M3 resize_via_shape (-40 dbu y) with reason=cu_pool:rejected_net_positive, matching the standalone rejection in trial:i02.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00. Do not re-include ops that carry a cu_pool:rejected_net_positive tag in assembled patches; the assembler removes them regardless of surrounding operations.

**V2.M3.EN.2 and V2.M3.AUX.2**

V2.M3.EN.2 requires M3 to enclose V2 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides (5+5 nm or 5+0 nm). V2.M3.AUX.2 requires V2 to exactly match M3 width in the direction perpendicular to M3 length. Instance moves that translate M3 and V2 together in the same direction maintain the relative enclosure geometry and width match without additional fixup; trial:i02.ug.leaf_0001.01 demonstrates this with zero new violations after moving both layers together by the same delta.

**V3.M3.EN.1**

V3.M3.EN.1 requires M3 to enclose V3 by at least 5 nm on at least one pair of opposite sides. Growing M3 via shapes alongside V3 shapes in a coordinated chain_fix group satisfies this rule. Trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52.00 grew both the M3 and V3 shapes of VIA_VIA34_1_2_58_52 together, and the operation was applied without V3.M3.EN.1 appearing in any rejection reason.
