## Operation Classes and Grid Discipline

M2 geometry in this design is modified through two distinct operation types observed across all iteration-1 trials: `move_instance` and `resize_end`. All `move_instance` deltas applied to instances touching M2 are x-axis only (y-component = 0) and are multiples of 36 dbu — specifically +36 dbu or +108 dbu in positive-x trials:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00, i01.ug.Block3_union_row2.01, i01.ug.Block3_union_row5.02, i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03, i01.ug.leaf_0006.04, i01.ug.leaf_0007.05, i01.ug.leaf_0008.06, i01.ug.leaf_0009.07, i01.ug.leaf_0012.08, and -36 dbu in trial:i01.ug.leaf_0013.09. Do not apply sub-36-dbu move deltas to instances whose touched_layers include M2; the 36 dbu quantum is the effective placement grid for this layer in Block3.

`resize_end` on M2-touching instances uses the same 36 dbu quantum: trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.06 applied `axis:"x", end:"high", delta_dbu:36` to polygon p1261 alongside two move_instance ops and was accepted with no new violations. When a spacing violation requires extending one M2 segment endpoint rather than moving the whole instance, resize the high or low end by a 36 dbu increment.

## unit_gate Channel: Accepted Trials and What They Confirm

Every unit_gate trial in this iteration was decided `gated_in` with `conn_preserved:true`, `n_new_in_crop:0`, and `n_new_out_of_crop:0`. The full set — trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00 (3 ops, locus 6856–10512 x, 2268–3132 y), trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row2.01 (2 ops), trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row5.02 (3 ops), trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03 (4 ops), trial:i01.ug.leaf_0006.04, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0007.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.06, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0009.07, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0012.08, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0013.09 — confirms that instance moves at the 36 dbu quantum, including the single-instance move and the four-instance grouped move (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row8.03), do not introduce secondary M2 DRC violations within the crop window. Apply single-digit or small groups of instance moves at +/-36 or +/-108 dbu on x when repairing M2 spacing; the evidence covers loci spanning from x=3400 to x=11016 and y=2068 to y=10892, indicating this discipline holds across the full Block3 footprint at this design state.

M2 co-moves with M1 and V1 in all unit_gate trials. Because touched_layers is consistently ["M1","M2","V1"], no unit_gate operation in this iteration moved M2 geometry independently of its adjacent layers. When moving instances to resolve M2 violations, expect M1 and V1 to translate by the same delta; verify that the resulting V1-to-M1 and V1-to-M2 enclosures remain legal after any instance move.

## cu_pool Channel: Via Landing Pad on M2

Trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 demonstrates the via-landing-pad adjustment pattern for the VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 cell. The applied sequence was: move shape_index 0 on V2 by -144 dbu on x-axis, then resize shape_index 0 by +288 dbu, resize shape_index 1 by +288 dbu, move shape_index 2 by +144 dbu, resize shape_index 2 by +288 dbu. Touched layers were M2, M3, V2. This reduced the aggregate DRC violation count by 27 (delta_total=-27) and improved two units: leaf_0018 dropped from 32 to 17 violations and leaf_0019 dropped from 35 to 23. The pattern — offset a via shape by N dbu then expand by 2N — re-centers and widens the metal landing on both sides, directly improving V2.M2.EN.1 enclosure (minimum 5 nm on at least two opposite sides). Apply this move-then-resize-by-2x pattern to via cell shapes when V2.M2.EN.1 violations appear and the via cell definition is editable.

The 144 dbu offset and 288 dbu resize (a 2:1 ratio) used in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 are multiples of 36 dbu (144 = 4×36, 288 = 8×36), consistent with the 36 dbu placement grid observed in unit_gate trials. Use 36 dbu multiples for all via shape adjustments on layers touching M2.

## Enclosure Rules: V1 and V2 at M2

V1.M2.EN.2 requires M2 to enclose V1 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides (5 & 5 nm or 5 & 0 nm). V1.M2.AUX.2 additionally requires V1 to share exactly two coincident edges with M2 along the direction perpendicular to the M2 length — V1 must be the same width as M2 in the cross-track direction. All unit_gate trials (trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00 through trial:i01.ug.leaf_0013.09) moved instances containing V1 and M2 together and produced zero new violations, confirming that rigid co-translation of a V1-M2 stack by the same delta satisfies both V1.M2.EN.2 and V1.M2.AUX.2. Never slide M2 relative to V1 without also adjusting the V1 shape or using a resize_end to maintain the enclosure and coincidence conditions.

V2.M2.EN.1 requires M2 to enclose V2 by at least 5 nm on at least two opposite sides. The cu_pool repair in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 targeted this violation class by expanding M2/M3 landing geometry around V2 shapes. When V2 enclosure violations appear, expand the M2 segment enclosing V2 at both ends (resize_end on high and low sides) rather than shrinking or repositioning V2.

## Spacing Rule Thresholds Active in This Context

The measured operations uniformly affect x-axis geometry, consistent with M2 running horizontally (length edges are y-direction, tip edges are x-direction). The relevant spacing hierarchy for horizontal M2 wires:

- Side-to-side (long edges, >36 nm): 18 nm minimum (M2.S.1). Tip-to-side (one edge ≤36 nm, one >36 nm): 25 nm minimum (M2.S.2). The 25 nm tip-to-side constraint is tighter than the 18 nm side-to-side; when moving an M2 wire endpoint (resize_end), the resulting end edge length determines which rule applies — an end edge ≤36 nm incurs M2.S.2 against neighboring long edges.
- Tip-to-tip spacing is non-uniform: both tips ≥24 nm and ≤36 nm → 27 nm (M2.S.3); one tip ≥24/≤36 nm and one tip <24 nm → 31 nm (M2.S.5); both tips <24 nm → 31 nm (M2.S.4). The 36 nm tip-length boundary (used in multiple rules) coincides with the 36 dbu placement quantum. At the minimum 18 nm M2 width, the tip edge is 18 nm — below the 24 nm threshold — so M2.S.4 (31 nm) and M2.S.5 (31 nm) are the active tip-to-tip rules for minimum-width wires. Prefer wire widths ≥24 nm at endpoints to allow the more relaxed 27 nm M2.S.3 tip-to-tip spacing where track density permits.
- Corner-to-corner (M2.S.6): 20 nm euclidian minimum, checked for gaps that fail euclidian but pass projection. This binds at convex corners of jogs or L-shapes; the 36 dbu move quantum (= 36 nm at 1 dbu = 1 nm) exceeds the 20 nm corner threshold, so single-step instance moves do not create new M2.S.6 violations between wires that were previously compliant.
- Minimum area (M2.A.1): 504 nm². An 18 nm-wide wire requires at least 28 nm of length to meet area (18 × 28 = 504). Avoid creating M2 stubs shorter than 28 nm when trimming or splitting segments.

## M2.S.7 and M2.S.8: Forbidden Configuration Constraints

M2.S.7 forbids the co-occurrence of an 18 nm tip-to-tip gap and a side-to-side spacing ≤32 nm on the same polygon pair, and requires parallel run length ≥35 nm when side spacing ≤32 nm. M2.S.8 requires that diagonal center-to-center spacing between tip-to-tip gaps on different tracks be ≥80 nm (euclidian). These are configuration-level constraints: they are triggered by the spatial relationship between adjacent M2 segments, not a single wire's geometry. None of the iteration-1 trials directly repaired M2.S.7 or M2.S.8 violations, but the unit_gate trials demonstrate that x-axis instance moves of 36–108 dbu did not introduce them (all returned n_new_in_crop=0, trial:i01.ug.Block3_union_row1.00 through trial:i01.ug.leaf_0013.09). When fixing M2.S.1 or tip spacing violations by moving instances, check that the post-move configuration does not bring two gaps to within 80 nm diagonal distance (M2.S.8) or co-locate a narrow tip-to-tip gap with a close side-to-side neighbor (M2.S.7).

## Non-Orthogonal Geometry

All M2 edges must be orthogonal (horizontal or vertical). The NONORTHOGONAL rule fires on any M2 edge whose angle is not 0° or 90°. All resize and move operations in this iteration used axis-aligned deltas on orthogonal polygons (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.06 resize_end axis:"x"; trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 all ops axis:"x"). Apply only axis-aligned operations to M2 polygons.
