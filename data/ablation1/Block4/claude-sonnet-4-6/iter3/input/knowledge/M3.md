## M3 Repair Patterns -- Iteration 1

### Grouped y-axis resize of M3 with concurrent via-shape adjustment is the effective repair pattern

The only repair that achieved a net violation reduction on M3 in this iteration used a coordinated group (`group_v2m3`) of operations: simultaneously resizing a set of M3 polygons on the y-axis (delta_dbu=-64 each) and resizing the M3 shape inside the via cell `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` (delta_dbu=-40, y-axis). This bundle reduced the total violation count by 24 (from 88 to 64 in the affected window) and was applied with `conn_preserved=true` (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). The group covered polygons p1411 through p1422, thirteen consecutive M3 shapes spanning the repair locus.

Do not apply the via-shape resize in isolation. When the same `resize_via_shape` op on `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` M3 shape was submitted without the accompanying polygon resizes, it achieved delta_total=0 and lost the tournament (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01). The via-shape-only move carries no standalone repair value under the observed conditions.

### Dropping the via-shape resize during assembly leaves residual violations

In trial:i01.ug.leaf_0026.12, the unit-gate channel applied y-axis resizes of -64 dbu to five M3 polygons (p1406-p1410) but the accompanying `resize_via_shape` op on the M3 layer of `VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36` was dropped at assembly (`cu_pool:lost_tournament`). The repair still gated in (`conn_preserved=true`) but introduced two new violations outside M3 (M1.A.1 and V1.M1.EN.1), and the via-shape issue had to be resolved separately through the cu_pool channel. When the via-shape op is dropped from an M3 polygon resize bundle, expect downstream enclosure-related violations in adjacent layers rather than in M3 itself.

### V2 enclosure rules (V2.M3.EN.2, V2.M3.AUX.2) are the driving motivation for y-axis M3 shrinks

All M3 y-axis resize operations in the history targeted the same via cell (`VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36`) and touched layers M2, M3, and V2 together (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01). V2.M3.EN.2 requires M3 to enclose V2 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides; V2.M3.AUX.2 requires that V2 width along the direction perpendicular to M3 length matches M3 exactly. Shrinking M3 in y while simultaneously adjusting the via shape satisfies both constraints when M3 was previously over-enclosing or mismatched in the perpendicular direction.

Trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06 included a move of M3 polygon p1477 by -28 dbu in x and a resize_end of polygon p1395 by +172 dbu in x (high end), alongside instance moves and changes to M1, M2, M4, V1, V2.
