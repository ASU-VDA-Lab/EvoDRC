## Unit-gate instance moves on M2

All nine unit_gate trials applied during iteration 1 were accepted with `gated_in` decisions and zero new DRC violations in crop (`n_new_in_crop=0`, `n_new_out_of_crop=0`). Every trial preserved connectivity (`conn_preserved=true`). The operations in this channel consistently combined `move_instance` ops--sometimes three or four in a single trial--with occasional `move` or `resize_end` polygon ops on M2, and none of these combinations introduced new M2 width, spacing, area, or via-enclosure violations.

Horizontal-only instance moves (y-component zero) across a wide magnitude range have been safe for M2: single-instance shifts of 12 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row10.01), 28 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row2.02 for i0163, trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row3.02), 36 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row6.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.08, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0020.09, trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row10.01 for i0120), 52 dbu endpoint-extension (trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row7.03), 72 dbu (trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row1.00 for i0265), 96 dbu (trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row10.01 for i0025), 108 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row2.02 for i0250, trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row7.03 for i0153) all passed without new in-crop violations. Multi-instance trials combining mixed-sign x displacements--including +37/+37/-36/+37 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row5.04) and +36/-108/+96 dbu (trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row10.01)--also passed without new M2 violations, confirming that mixed-sign x displacements within the same unit do not inherently create new M2 violations. Do not treat any single measured magnitude as a hard ceiling; the relevant constraint is the resulting inter-polygon spacing after the move, not the magnitude of the move itself.

A diagonal (two-axis) instance move of [36, 44] dbu (positive y) was accepted without new violations (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0021.10), touching M1, M2, and V1.

## Negative y-only instance moves introduce M2 violations

Two iteration-2 trials applied pure-y, negative-direction instance moves and both introduced new M2 in-crop violations, despite being accepted as `gated_in` on connectivity grounds:

- trial:i02.ug.leaf_0008.04: `move_instance` [0, -44] on i0038 produced `n_new_in_crop=2`, including M2.S.2:1 and V1.M2.EN.2:1.
- trial:i02.ug.leaf_0013.06: `move_instance` [0, -36] on i0038 produced `n_new_in_crop=9`, including M2.S.7:1 among violations on M1, M4, and V1.

In both cases the trial was still admitted (`gated_in`), but the M2 violation debt increased. The M2.S.2 hit (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0008.04) indicates that a -44 dbu downward shift brought a tip edge within the 25 nm tip-to-side clearance required by M2.S.2. The M2.S.7 hit (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0013.06) indicates that a -36 dbu downward shift placed an 18 nm tip-to-tip gap co-located with a side-to-side spacing <=32 nm, triggering the compound spacing rule.

Negative-y instance moves on M2, even at magnitudes of 36-44 dbu, are not free of M2 DRC risk. The positive-y diagonal move at [36, 44] (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0021.10) was clean; the asymmetry is directional: moving instances downward (-y) compresses clearances to shapes below, which are denser in the observed design state. Do not apply negative-y moves expecting zero new M2 violations without verifying post-move tip-to-side and S.7 clearances.

## M2 polygon resize operations (unit gate)

Endpoint extension on M2 (`resize_end`, x-axis, high end) has been safe in all measured trials when the extension moves the endpoint away from neighboring shapes or when associated instance moves increase clearance on the opposite side:

- trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.08: p1604 extended +92 dbu, combined with +36 dbu instance move. Accepted, no new violations.
- trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06: p1395 extended +172 dbu, combined with two -28 dbu instance moves and two -28 dbu polygon moves. Accepted, no new violations (touched M1, M2, M3, M4, V1, V2).

The measured safe range for `resize_end` on x extends from 16 to 172 dbu. None of these trials involved extending into a stationary M2 edge without a compensating move; care is warranted in that configuration, which remains unmeasured.

A `move` (translation without resize) of M2 polygon p1548 by +16 dbu on x within the same trial as two instance moves (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row2.02) also produced no new violations. This is consistent with a small positional realignment.
