## Via Enclosure Repair: Coordinated M3 Resizes Outperform Isolated Via-Shape Shrink

A single-operation resize of the M3 pad shape of VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 (y delta -40 dbu, no other ops) lost the cu_pool tournament and was not applied (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01). Do not issue resize_via_shape on M3 in isolation expecting a tournament win.

The identical resize_via_shape on M3 (y delta -40 dbu) combined with coordinated y-axis resizes of 12 surrounding M3 polygon segments (delta -64 dbu each, grouped under "group_v2m3") achieved a net violation reduction of -24 and was applied (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00). When using resize_via_shape on M3 to repair V2.M3.EN.2 or V2.M3.AUX.2, always pair it with y-axis resizes of the adjacent M3 segments in the same group operation; the isolated via-shape shrink does not survive the tournament on its own (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01 vs. trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00).
