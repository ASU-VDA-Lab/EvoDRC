## M3 Repair Knowledge — Layer M3 (Iteration 4)

### Grouping M3 polygon y-resizes with via shape resizes is required for net DRC reduction

When addressing M3 violations near V2 cells, resizing only the M3 shape inside the via cell (op `resize_via_shape`, M3 layer, y-axis, -40 dbu) without simultaneously resizing the connected M3 polygons produces zero net reduction and loses tournament selection (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01, delta_total=0). The same M3 via shape shrink applied as part of a coordinated group that also shrinks the surrounding M3 polygons in y by -64 dbu per polygon reduced the window violation count by 24 (trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, applied, delta_total=-24). Do not submit `resize_via_shape` on M3 in isolation; always pair it with matching `resize` ops on the abutting M3 polygon set in the same group.

The same via shape shrink attempted as a standalone op in the unit_gate channel was dropped at assembly with reason `cu_pool:lost_tournament` (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0026.12, assemble_drops entry for VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 M3 shape). This confirms the solo via-shape-only pattern is non-viable regardless of channel.

### Moving V2 in x is an effective alternative repair path for V2.M3.EN.2 and V2.M3.AUX.2 violations

When the via cell VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 generates violations on M3-adjacent windows, shifting the V2 shape within the cell laterally (op `move_via_shape`, V2 layer, x-axis, +144 dbu) resolved 17 violations across two units without modifying any M3 polygon geometry (trial:i03.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00, applied, delta_total=-17). Rule V2.M3.EN.2 requires enclosure of V2 by M3 of at least 5 nm on two opposite sides; rule V2.M3.AUX.2 requires V2 width to match M3 width perpendicular to the M3 run direction. Lateral repositioning of V2 within M3 satisfies both enclosure and alignment constraints without altering M3 geometry or risking M3 width/spacing violations.

Prefer `move_via_shape` on V2 over `resize_via_shape` on M3 when the M3 polygon set around the via is already at or near minimum dimensions, because shrinking M3 risks M3.W.1 (18 nm minimum width) or M3.A.1 (504 nm² minimum area) violations on shapes that are already compact.

### M3 polygon y-resize in bulk (group_v2m3) is the primary M3 repair op for spacing violations

The repair that succeeded in trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 shrank twelve M3 polygons (p1411–p1422) each by -64 dbu in y under group tag `group_v2m3`, alongside the M3 via shape shrink. The parallel unit_gate trial (trial:i01.ug.leaf_0026.12) performed the same -64 dbu y-resize on five M3 polygons (p1406–p1410) and was accepted with conn_preserved=true, though it introduced two violations on M1 (M1.A.1, V1.M1.EN.1) as side effects. The M1 side-effect violations are not M3-layer violations; they arise because M3 resizes propagate connectivity constraints downward. M3-specific rules M3.S.1 (side-to-side 18 nm, edges > 36 nm), M3.S.2 (tip-to-side 25 nm), M3.S.3 and M3.S.4 (tip-to-tip 27–31 nm) are the spacing rules most sensitive to y-dimension changes because a y-shrink shortens side edges and can convert side edges to tip-class edges (length ≤ 36 nm), tightening the applicable spacing rule from M3.S.1 (18 nm) to M3.S.2 (25 nm) or M3.S.3/M3.S.4/M3.S.5 (27–31 nm). Apply bulk y-resizes only when the post-resize edge length budget is verified against these thresholds.

### Small x-axis resize_end adjustments on M3 remain viable with low-magnitude moves

trial:i04.ug.leaf_0003.01 (iter 4, gated_in, conn_preserved) applied a -8 dbu x-axis resize on the high end of M3 polygon p1410 together with an instance move of -8 dbu in x for instance i0238. The result was accepted with two new in-crop violations, but connectivity was preserved and the trial was gated in. This establishes that single-digit dbu end-point trims on M3 polygons are accepted by the gate even when they introduce a small number of in-crop violations, provided conn_preserved is true. Avoid larger end-point trims without verifying that the resulting tip length does not fall below 24 nm (M3.S.3 threshold) or 36 nm (M3.S.2 threshold), as crossing those boundaries changes the applicable spacing rule and may create new violations at adjacent polygons.

### Combined instance moves with M3 polygon extension can resolve violations without introducing new ones

trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06 (iter 1, gated_in, delta n_new_in_crop=0, n_new_out_of_crop=0) moved two instances (i0158, i0124) by -28 dbu in x, moved two M3 polygons (p1596, p1477) by -28 dbu in x, and extended M3 polygon p1395 on its high x-end by +172 dbu. The net result introduced zero new violations of any kind. Extending an M3 polygon end by a large delta (+172 dbu) while shifting adjacent polygons and instances by a matching -28 dbu offset is a coherent repair pattern: it closes a gap that was generating M3 spacing violations without creating tip-length violations, because the extended end grows rather than shrinks the edge length. Use this coordinated translate-plus-extend pattern when a spacing gap between two M3 polygons is the root cause; it preserves M3.W.1 and M3.A.1 on p1395 while the lateral shift keeps M3.S.1 satisfied for p1596 and p1477 relative to their neighbors.
