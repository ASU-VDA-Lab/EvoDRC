## X-Axis Instance Moves Are the Primary M2 Repair Operation

The unit_gate channel resolves M2 spacing and enclosure violations almost exclusively through `move_instance` operations along the X axis. Across all gated_in unit_gate trials in iterations 1 and 2, the X-axis delta is always nonzero and the Y-axis delta is zero or absent: trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00 (+36 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row10.01 (+12 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row2.02 (+108, +28 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row5.04 (+37, +37, -36, +37 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row6.05 (+36, +36, +36 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row3.02 (-28 dbu), trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00 iter 2 (+72, +36 dbu), trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row7.03 (+108 dbu). All of these produced zero new in-crop violations.

## Y-Axis Instance Moves Consistently Introduce New Violations

Moving an instance in the Y direction triggers M2 spacing, tip rules, and via-enclosure violations. trial:i02.ug.leaf_0008.04 applied `move_instance i0038 [0,-44]` and introduced 2 new in-crop violations: M2.S.2 (tip-to-side, 25 nm minimum) and V1.M2.EN.2 (M2 enclosure of V1, 5 nm minimum). trial:i02.ug.leaf_0013.06 applied `move_instance i0038 [0,-36]` and introduced 9 new in-crop violations including M2.S.7 (tip-to-tip 18 nm co-located with side spacing ≤32 nm). Both trials were still gated_in because `conn_preserved=true` and `n_new_out_of_crop=0`, so the gate did not block them, but the in-crop violation count increased in both cases.

Do not use Y-only moves as a primary M2 repair strategy. When a Y-axis move is unavoidable due to external assembly constraints, verify that resulting M2 tip-to-side clearances (M2.S.2, minimum 25 nm) and M2 enclosure of V1 (V1.M2.EN.2, 5 nm on two opposite sides) remain satisfied, and that no M2 tip-to-tip gap at 18 nm lands within a window where side spacing ≤32 nm (M2.S.7).

## M2.S.7 Is Triggered by Y-Axis Displacement Reducing Inter-Row Gaps

M2.S.7 forbids an 18 nm tip-to-tip gap co-located with side-to-side spacing ≤32 nm; it also requires parallel run length ≥35 nm when side spacing ≤32 nm. trial:i02.ug.leaf_0013.06 introduced an M2.S.7 violation via a -36 dbu Y move. X-axis moves in the same row context (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row5.04, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row6.05) produced no M2.S.7 violations at all. To avoid M2.S.7, keep row-to-row M2 displacement confined to X; if Y displacement is forced, ensure the resulting parallel overlap region either drops below the 18 nm tip gap threshold or the side-to-side clearance exceeds 32 nm.

## V1.M2.EN.2: Y Displacement Breaks Enclosure, X Displacement Preserves It

V1.M2.EN.2 requires M2 to enclose V1 by at least 5 nm on two opposite sides (5 & 5 nm or 5 & 0 nm). All X-only moves in the unit_gate channel preserved this enclosure with zero new V1.M2.EN.2 violations (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row6.05, trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row3.02, and others). The Y-axis move in trial:i02.ug.leaf_0008.04 ([0,-44]) introduced one V1.M2.EN.2 violation, showing that shifting M2 vertically relative to V1 reduces enclosure on one horizontal side below the 5 nm floor.

## resize_end on M2 High-End Must Be Paired With Instance Move When an Instance Is Also Displaced

Several trials combined `resize_end` (extending the high X end of an M2 wire) with a `move_instance` for the same unit. trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.08 paired `move_instance i0274 [36,0]` with `resize_end p1604 x +92` and produced zero new violations. trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06 paired two `move_instance [-28,0]` ops with two polygon X moves and `resize_end p1395 x +172`, again producing zero new violations. trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row10.01 combined three `move_instance` calls with `resize_end p1551 x +16 high`, also clean. trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row7.03 combined `move_instance i0153 [108,0]` with `resize_end p1595 x +52 high`, also clean. In all cases the resize_end extends only the high (far) end of the wire, not the low end, and the result maintains M2.W.1 (18 nm minimum width) and M2.A.1 (504 nm-sq minimum area).

## V2.M2.EN.2: Coordinated M2 Y-Resize With Via Shape Resize Is Required

trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00 (cu_pool channel, decision=applied) fixed a V2.M2.EN.2 violation by simultaneously resizing 12 M2 polygons by -64 dbu in Y and resizing the VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36 via shape by -40 dbu in Y. This coordinated operation reduced the total violation count by 24. trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.01, which resized only the via shape by -40 dbu in Y without touching the M2 polygons, lost the tournament (decision=lost_tournament, delta_total=0). Resizing the via shape alone without adjusting the enclosing M2 wires does not satisfy V2.M2.EN.2; the M2 polygons bounding the via must be resized in the same operation, as demonstrated by trial:i01.cu.def:VIA_VIA23_1_3_36_36.00.

## External Conflict Resolution: Shared Instances Drop to a Single Claimant

trial:i02.ug.leaf_0008.04 and trial:i02.ug.leaf_0013.06 both attempted to move instance i0038 (leaf_0021 territory). In each case, the assembly stage dropped the conflicting op and retained only the non-conflicting portion of the trial. The trial that lost the dropped op (leaf_0008) received the op `[0,-44]`; the trial that also contended for i0038 (leaf_0013) had its corresponding op `[0,-36]` dropped. Both trials remained gated_in because the remaining ops still preserved connectivity. When multiple units share an instance, only one trial's move for that instance will be applied; the other trial must rely solely on its non-conflicting ops to achieve DRC improvement.

## Move Magnitude Patterns Observed in Gated-In Trials

X-axis move deltas in gated_in trials are multiples of 4 dbu or near-grid values: 12, 16, 28, 36, 37, 52, 72, 92, 96, 108, 172 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row10.01, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row2.02, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row3.02, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row5.04, trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row7.03, trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0008.08, trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row10.01, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row2.02, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06). The minimum M2 spacing rule M2.S.1 is 18 nm; M2.W.1 is 18 nm. A 36 dbu move (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00, trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row6.05, trial:i01.ug.leaf_0020.09) corresponds to two M2 grid pitches and is the most frequently used single-instance delta. Negative X moves of 28 dbu (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row7.06, trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row3.02) also produced zero new violations, confirming that magnitude alone does not determine safety; direction and context relative to neighboring wires govern whether spacing rules are satisfied.
