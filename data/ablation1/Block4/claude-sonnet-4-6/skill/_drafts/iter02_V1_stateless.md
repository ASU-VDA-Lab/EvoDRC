## Summary of repair operation patterns for V1

Use X-axis instance displacements and X-axis polygon resize_end operations when touching V1 geometry; these produced zero new V1 violations across all thirteen clean trials (trial:i01.ug.Block4_union_row1.00 through trial:i02.ug.Block4_union_row7.03). Do not apply Y-axis instance displacements to cells containing V1 shapes without simultaneously adjusting M1 and M2 polygon boundaries to maintain the enclosure margins required by V1.M1.EN.1 and V1.M2.EN.2; both Y-direction trials (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0008.04, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0013.06) introduced new V1 enclosure violations. When assembly conflict resolution drops one of two competing Y-axis moves on the same instance, treat the surviving move as enclosure-risk and verify M1/M2 margins before accepting (trial:i02.ug.leaf_0008.04, trial:i02.ug.leaf_0013.06).
